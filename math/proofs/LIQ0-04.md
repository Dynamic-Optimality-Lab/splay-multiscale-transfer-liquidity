# LIQ0-04 ACTIVATION_BOUNDEDNESS - Layer A

Each of the at most rho(ev) iterations flips at most one LATENT credit (t5_one flips exactly the first eligible or nothing). Hence activations <= rho(ev), and activations = min(eligible, cap) with partial activation when eligible < cap (loop breaks on no-op). Unbounded activation is impossible by the range(cap) bound.

Downstream consumer: WP-6. Finite differential evidence is Layer-C support, never a premise.

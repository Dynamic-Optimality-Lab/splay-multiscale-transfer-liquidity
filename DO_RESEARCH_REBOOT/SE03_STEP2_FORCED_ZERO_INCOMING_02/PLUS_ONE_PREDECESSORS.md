# Positive-one predecessors and actual debt caps

Target: classify incoming +1 constraints without assuming a replacement
debt exists. Conditions: exact composable actual packets. Inheritance:
cost grammar, core11 first-entry and C11 exclusions in the zero report.
Ambiguities: a cap does not prove feasibility or justify an old formula.
Bridge: feasibility and Dstar(z)=0 give1-Dstar(p)>=0.

Universally, +1 packets are singleton H1/Q1 or H2/Q3. Every such edge
into Z imposes Dstar(p)<=1. At an actual F11 endpoint the H2/Q3 case
is impossible by the fan bound; only H1/Q1 survives. This reduction is
not valid at an arbitrary earlier point of Z. At C11 endpoints it also
follows from the extreme-root unary-separator lemma.

For a source macro of the surviving type, the unique positive A access
is to the terminal mark at actual depth1 or2. No uncharged excursion
can be hidden before it. Any prior full root-repeat Kg is an actual
source occurrence with the correct BE action; repeated Kg is idempotent
only as an endpoint identity. No auxiliary/full equality is assumed.

## Exact source predecessors of the known right-oriented F shapes

Their actual source A is of the form

    A_f = 1(L,3(2(C,D),R)),

with arbitrary fixed actual ordered subtrees L,C,D,R supplied by the
family. BF ends at1, an extreme. An incoming genuine core11 q1 must
have old root3: its original all-LL three-node path is3,2,1. The
other separator/ancestor alternative would make an unmarked core
node lose its marked descendant, contrary to the closed-source class.

An A splay to1 with old synchronized A root3 can give A_f in exactly
two endpoint forms:

    3(1(L,2(C,D)),R)               -- ZIG --> A_f;

or, when L=p(L0,L1) with an actual p<1,

    3(p(L0,1(L1,2(C,D))),R)        -- LR  --> A_f.

The LL case would put old root3 at RR, whereas A_f has it at R; RR/RL
contradict target1<old root3. These are the complete one-event A
inverse cases. p is the actual root of the actual L subtree, not an
invented key. Both identities retain all subtrees; reflection gives
the separate R-ZIG/RL formulas. This is an endpoint classification,
not a claim that every inverse candidate has a closed embedding.

BE must additionally solve its exact original-source equation: optional
Kg (if retained) followed by K1, or no Kg followed by K1. Its unknown
predecessor is not chosen freely. The old recent order starts with3
and yields the recorded final order by moving1 to the front; all
admissible old positions of1 must be retained, including a cold sixth
position when the other five keep their order. No chart debt equality
across those positions is used.

## What has and has not been excluded

The marked-parent compression formula in RETURN_BARRIER.md shows that
any genuine single splay reduces marked-leaf count by at most one.
Every F11 has at most two leaves; every T11 has at least four. Thus
NO DIRECT singleton edge, in particular no direct +1 edge, goes from
T11 into F11. Paired targets cannot equal F11 either. This excludes
one-edge conflicts but not a zero prefix before a +1 entry, or a +1
followed by a zero suffix into F. Those arbitrary composable chains
are the genuine remaining problem.

## Why this proves arbitrary-fringe source identities

Every accessed key is distinguished. No comparison or rotation enters
the interior of an Fi. Each of the finitely many original source frames
reattaches its actual hanging subtrees rigidly. By structural substitution
induction, replacing each formal Fi by ANY finite BST with keys in that
ordered gap commutes with every step, preserves event counts, and
preserves all displayed final terms. Therefore closure, incoming edge
and exact F/T identities are uniform free-term identities, not a finite
sample standing in for arbitrary populations. This lift permits passive
fringe substitution; it does not cover future helper accesses INSIDE a
newly exposed Fi. That distinction is the search limitation above.

## Independent derivation

audit_independent.py imports only the frozen immutable recursive-frame
engine python/independent/splay.py. It does not import the search or the
new primary generator. In the engine's different tagged term format,
it replays the new complete word and its reflection, independently
checks every stored endpoint and each A/full/erased cost, and verifies
full cyclic closure. It also derives both two-inner-LL/RR identities
and both inverse-one-event A formulas with opaque gap terms. It checks
the exact C11 properties and marked-leaf counts of the accepted F/T
terms without re-executing the original control words.

Separate human-readable derivations in the zero and return reports
audit the unbounded source logic: why processed subtrees persist, why
the full marked core cannot change, why the leaf formula counts exactly
the vertices whose status changes, and why(R) is a necessary bound
rather than a proof of the desired inequality. No finite lemma check is
claimed as the sole proof of an unbounded statement. Independent audit
here means separate engine/representation and source derivation by the
same agent; no external reviewer or full proof-assistant formalization.

One failed new draft is retained: audit_incoming.py compared tuple trees
against JSON list trees and failed that representation-level assertion.
No mathematical mismatch or invalid closed state resulted. V2 normalizes
the serialization before equality. The failed child AND controller CPU
are charged, no receipt is overwritten, and the independent engine
subsequently verifies the corrected equality at every physical step.

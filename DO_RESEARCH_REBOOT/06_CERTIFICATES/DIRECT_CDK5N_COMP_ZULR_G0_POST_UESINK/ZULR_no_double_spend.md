# ZULR no-double-spend (L7 record)

Every interval pays only its own debt from its own reserve R_j: the unique
payer p from R_p; all other positives from their own R_j (strict-K/P
implies H>=2, so R_j>=2>=w_j); nonpositives need no credit. No sink is
introduced; no credit is transferred between intervals; no reserve is
reused; no A-cost belongs to two H intervals; K/P overlap on one interval
never duplicates credit; the cyclic seam wraps exactly once. ∎

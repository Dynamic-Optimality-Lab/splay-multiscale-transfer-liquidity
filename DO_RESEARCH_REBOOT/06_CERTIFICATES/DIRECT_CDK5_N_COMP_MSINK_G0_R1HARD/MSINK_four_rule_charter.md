# MSINK four-rule charter (ORIGINAL rules — pre-execution record)

## R1 — STRICTLY SMALLER (proposal)

Five-key nine-core + exactly-two-exception + mixed short/long relays + joint
H4–H5 sink + K/P others. Proper subclass of the open complement (frozen
inequalities); disjoint from NP (H=1 exceptions), S1/S2 (creditless seconds),
VR (two exceptions), MR (mixed-not-all-long relays). Nonemptiness/properness
require two residual witnesses (IN/OUT) — evidenced below or verdict fails.

## R2 — SOLVABILITY MECHANISM (proposal)

Shared-sink joint payment (L1–L7): baseline H≥c; debt w∈{1,2}; exceptions
(1,0)×2; sink H≥3 (R≥4) covering w≤2+2 worst case (q_p≤4 full range);
others K/P-or-R≥0; triple+sink disjoint (H4 distinctness); telescoping.
Bounded, explicit, no oracle. Universal audit in payment-proof file.

## R3 — ONE-PROMPT SCOPE (proposal)

One theorem, one mechanism, one deterministic bounded campaign (2 seeds,
DELETE-only edits ≤6, ≤250k/seed, ≤500k total, fixed order), one IN/OUT pair,
one validation package. No campaign split (IN/OUT are roles in one enumeration).

## R4 — UPWARD PROGRESS (proposal)

IN must lie in S_raw∩R5 (banked union + additive closure excluded); OUT in
R5∖S_raw. Neither from old controls (S1/VR/MR words ineligible as residual
witnesses). Positive Q−2C_K alone insufficient.

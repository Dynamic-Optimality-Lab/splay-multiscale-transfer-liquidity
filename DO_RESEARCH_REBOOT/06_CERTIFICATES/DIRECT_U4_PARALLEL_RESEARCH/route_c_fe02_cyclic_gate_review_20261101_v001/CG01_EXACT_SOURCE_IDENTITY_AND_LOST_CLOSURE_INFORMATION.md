# What source-depth telescoping does and does not prove

This is a completed proof-obligation audit, not a new FE02
proof campaign or a claimed mathematical advance. The global
campaign failed CG00's pre-execution four-rule review. Full
FE02-CD, FE02-RIGIDITY and Universal Coarse-Double remain OPEN.

## 1. Original domain retained

For an actual FE02 counterexample, exact integer arithmetic
already forces one positive block with(a,q)=(1,3), and all
other blocks satisfy q=2a. Every block has KEEP. Complete
block projection applies the same original splay target to
both boundary trees, retaining real A leader/B first-KEEP
event owners. The pair need not be equal; its diagonal startup
may contain DELETEs. Startup units cannot pay repeated-period
demands.

Choose g as the minimum actually visited key, and start after
one g block. This anchor exists independently of any desired
donor. Both trees are then rooted at g. All subsequent queries
are>=g, so CE side isolation and TR's exact source-LCA depth
identity apply. Arbitrary support, unequal interiors, approaches,
turns and finite period length remain permitted. Reflection
gives the maximum-anchor left-oriented statement.

## 2. Exact algebra from the original source

For a performed splay of target x in tree C, define before
that operation:

    h_C = depth_C(g),
    j_C = depth_C(LCA(g,x)),
    n_C = depth_C(x)-j_C,
    e_C = 0 if x is on the anchor spine,
          otherwise TR's original local old-root epsilon1 or2,
    z_C = depth_C(x) mod2.

TR gives h'_C-h_C=-j_C+e_C. The actual return x=g is also
included: j_C=h_C,n_C=e_C=0 and h'_C=0. No positive-deficit
assumption is made.

For the old mixed-history regressions only, frozen B DELETE
has EFFECTIVE performed terms j_B=n_B=e_B=z_B=d_B=0. Its
actual tree and actual h_B remain unchanged. That convention
does not assert its search depth would have been0 had KEEP
occurred. It simply counts no unperformed B work.

Physical counts are exactly a=ceil(d_A/2), q=floor(d_B/2),
where d_C=j_C+n_C for a performed splay. With

    delta=q-2a,
    D=2h_A-h_B,
    r=n_B+e_B-2(n_A+e_A)-2z_A-z_B,

literal substitution gives

    2delta
      =j_B+n_B-2(j_A+n_A)-2z_A-z_B
      =(D'-D)+r.                                    (CG1)

Therefore any such actual source history has

    2 sum(delta)=D_final-D_initial+sum(r).            (CG2)

This is exact source algebra. It is not a newly constructed
potential, a sign bound or physical reserve. e_C and comparison
corrections are never extra A units.

## 3. Why cancellation does not supply the global theorem

Full paired closure implies D_final=D_initial. But the same
equality already follows when only the two roots return to g:
both anchor depths are0. Restoring complete source trees is
strictly stronger and was discarded when keeping only D.

The unchanged inherited TR episode KEEP(16,10,9,8,1) proves
this distinction exactly. Both initial and final roots are1,
while the complete final A and B trees differ from their
initial trees. Its five blocks have

    sum(delta)=1,       D_final-D_initial=0,
    sum(r)=2.

Thus a root-returning source history can cancel the scalar
boundary term while preserving the one-unit unpaid deficit.
This rejects the use of SCALAR endpoint cancellation as a
substitute for complete paired closure. It does not reject
any theorem that genuinely uses the missing full-state or
diagonal-reachability information. TR's episode has no proved
diagonal or full closed provenance; this review supplies none.

The unchanged DC unequal-gap prefix gives a second boundary
check, not another constructed example:

    sum(delta)=1,       D_final-D_initial=1,
    sum(r)=1.

Its nonzero endpoint term must be retained. Dropping it on
that open prefix would be an arithmetic error. Neither prefix
is a reachable paired closed FE02 counterexample.

## 4. The residual sign obligation is the old problem

On an exactly closed source history CG2 reduces to

    sum(r)=2 sum(delta).

Hence proving sum(r)<=0 on the original FE02 domain would
prove FE02. But without an independent source constraint,
that residual inequality is precisely the same aggregate
cost statement in new coordinates. It is not a smaller
lemma, a constructed witness or an automatic consequence
of telescoping. In the hypothetical saturated counterexample
the identity merely requires sum(r)=2; it does not contradict it.

Likewise summing the separate source laws gives

    sum(j_C)=sum(e_C)

at an anchor-root return. Substituting that into CG2 recovers
the same residual equation; it supplies no further sign. Full
paired source closure has additional coordinates, but no
established bound here connects their restoration or diagonal
reachability to the residual sign under arbitrary unequal
entries and boundary changes.

No argument in this review forces escapes to shrink intervals,
consume distinct keys, restore a common prefix, synchronize,
or generate one physical unit per restored ancestor. Earlier
source counterexamples prohibit assuming those properties.

## 5. Verification and exact scope

`audit_source_telescope.py` replays291 old source rows in14
existing ordinary/reflected/symbolic representations from five
inherited certificate families. These are not291 new walks.
The fixed old closed W/OR/CE controls satisfy full pair closure;
the old TR/DC prefixes are explicitly checked NOT to close.
The original W diagonal startup is checked separately without
borrowing its units into the period. Both original engines
and their pinned frozen independent harness are unchanged.

47,635 checks passed. The certificate records every source-row
hash, source-certificate hash, per-block original depths, LCA
terms, parity bits, deltas and boundary/residual terms. Those
checks validate the audit. They neither prove the missing
residual sign nor advance FE02 mathematically. No new source
state, word, key count, fringe substitution or fit was generated.

# Reachable closed controls and physical credit certificates

These finite-exact source histories establish nonvacuity and physical
provenance of AD02. Their successful repayment is NOT the proof for all
closed continuations of that theorem, and is not FE02's universal closure.
No FI/PF/FE strict-coverage claim is made.

Every control starts at diagonal(B0,B0), contains its displayed DELETEs
INSIDE the recurrent period, and returns to that exact diagonal pair.
Adjacent keys are different, including the cyclic last/first boundary,
so each physical occurrence is one maximal nontrivial A-root block.
No diagonal startup outside the period supplies credit.

The common source connector from C back to B0 is

    K(1,3,9,5,8,11,3,1,13).

The first proposed connector was false and remains preserved, including
its false endpoint flag, in source_discharge_certificate.json. The above
word was found in the separately gated fixed-target source provenance
audit, on the SAME13 keys. Both original engines now verify its actual

of search completeness or the absence of any other path.

The same word also takes the diagonal state after the u=4 synchronizing
KEEP4 to B0. It fails for the u=2 synchronized state; that failure is
retained in overlap_discharge_certificate.json. The AD00B-authorized
same-size, same-target audit for that state found the distinct connector

    K(4,13,5,4,8,1,11,3,1,13).

These are source reachability audits, not searches over a CD inequality,
sizes, repetitions or fitted coefficients. The fixed single-tree graph
bound is the Catalan number C_13=742900; actual discovery sizes are in

## Arbitrary common fringe nonvacuity

The full periods, not merely the exposure spans, were replayed with an
opaque subtree F_i at each of the14 external ordered gaps. Their
destinations equal their FULL symbolic sources. No core access enters
any F_i; each rotation is an identity in those variables. Hence every
arbitrary valid common fringe substitution, with arbitrary internal size
and shape and any increasing core relabeling, gives an actually reachable
closed FE02 instance with the same source costs and injection. The
symbolic proof, rather than additional large-n tests, supplies this
all-size assertion. The actual empty-gap13-key instances are independently
verified finite controls. Their slack is not a saturated-return refutation.

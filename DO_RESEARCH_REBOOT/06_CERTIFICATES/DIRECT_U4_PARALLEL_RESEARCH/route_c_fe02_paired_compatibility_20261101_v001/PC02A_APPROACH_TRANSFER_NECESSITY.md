# A necessary paired transfer feeding the unique positive return

**PROVED—UNIVERSAL necessary source condition**, as a direct consequence of
PC02, not a sufficient closure theorem or an assumed credit matching.

For any returning key y, let g=d_B(y)-2d_A(y). At its previous access both
depths are0. Just after departure,

    g_departure=epsilon_B-2epsilon_A<=0.

At a later block j, source parity gives

    d_A,j=2a_j-z_A,j, d_B,j=2q_j+z_B,j,
    g_pre,j=2delta_j+z_B,j+2z_A,j.                          (7)

Here z_A,z_B are the actual0/1 terminal-zig indicators. A (1,3) positive
therefore requires g_pre,j>=2. At an intermediate common-root access define

    chi_t(y)=Delta_B,t(y)-2Delta_A,t(y),

using FE01's ORIGINAL-source H for each actual pre-tree. Telescoping after
departure gives

    sum_(intermediate t with chi_t>0) chi_t
        >= g_pre,j-g_departure >=2-g_departure.             (8)

Every positive chi in (8) is an APPROACH-SIDE event: y is on the target side
of the old root, since PC02 proves chi<=0 on the opposite side and old root.
Also Delta_A,t(y)<=0, because Delta_A>=1 and the universal Delta_B<=2 would
imply chi<=0. Thus a hypothetical one-positive saturated return MUST restore
the particular positive key's paired gap through actual approaching accesses

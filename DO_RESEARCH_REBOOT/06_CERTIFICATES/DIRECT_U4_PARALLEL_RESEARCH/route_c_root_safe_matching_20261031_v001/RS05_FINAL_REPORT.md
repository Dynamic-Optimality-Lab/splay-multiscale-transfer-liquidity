# Global matching repairs the consumers but fails DELETE

The new globally coupled reserve pays the known starved consumers, including the four-key conflict-transfer failure, but one root-changing DELETE creates unbounded positive drift. Step 3 remains open. The four-rule gate was applied before execution; no larger finite fit was run, and Stage 11G remains unchanged.

The candidate is

    P(A,B)=F(A,B)-mu_r(A,B), r=root(A),

where mu_r is one maximum endpoint matching after exact common subtree pairs are fixed, using only residual B intervals that exclude r. This supplies a global one-to-one allocation rather than independent local conflict counts.

Proved properties of this same function include root-conditioned B nonincrease, one-Lipschitz change under root-preserving A rotations, full q+1 repayment on opposite vines at every size, and both established arbitrary-fringe whole-helper laws. The old hook changes 5->2, the padded hook 4->2 and the four-key conflict-transfer consumer 3->1, each paying its full q. Weak B nonincrease is not the required full-q universal B theorem; that stronger law remains unproved here.

The construction fails the other universal condition. For every n>=3 use

    A=1(empty,n(R_[2,n-1],empty)),
    B=2(leaf1,R_[3,n]).

DELETE n is one A zig with budget 2. Exact matching/cover identities give

    P_source=1, P_destination=n-2, Delta P=n-3.

Thus every n>=6 violates DELETE. Changing the root invalidates n-3 units of globally allocated matching credit. The defect cannot be repaired by a uniformly bounded additive correction. Any repair on this family must supply a correction drop of at least n-5 during that single zig.

The six-key member is reached from a diagonal and embedded in an independently checked 18-access closed paired walk. Verification covers 44 matching/cover equalities, 28 full source rows and both orientations. The walk has A=20,Q_B=0,Z_B=12 and is not a coarse-double counterexample.

No original CD obligation was discharged. The gate stops this mechanism after its exact DELETE refutation; no alternative coupling or equally hard existence premise is introduced. All 154 inherited/source pins and all 125 Stage 11G final-closure files are checked again at delivery.

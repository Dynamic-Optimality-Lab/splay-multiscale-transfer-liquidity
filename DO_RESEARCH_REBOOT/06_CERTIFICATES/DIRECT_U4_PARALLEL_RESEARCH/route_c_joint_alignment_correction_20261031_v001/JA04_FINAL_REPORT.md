# A joint correction passes old controls but fails equal root DELETE

No correction satisfying both universal bounds was obtained. A genuinely joint unbounded correction pays the earlier registered failures but has an exact all-size DELETE obstruction. Step 3 remains open.

The new construction is

    R_joint(A,B)=mu_r(A,B) indicator(root(A)!=root(B)),
    H=P+R_joint.

It uses the actual pair and P's eligible residual matching rank, without a scalar envelope or B-only gauge. H equals Ehat when the roots agree and F when they differ. The correction is unbounded on the original root-drift family and cancels that drift exactly. It also pays the old hook, padded hook, opposite vines, zero-double KEEP and last starved helper. The established arbitrary-fringe whole-helper and all-size opposite-vine laws hold for this same function. These are partial results; they do not replace either required universal bound.

The failure starts with two different trees sharing root1:

    A_n=1(empty,n(R_[2,n-1],empty)), B=R_n.

DELETE n is one A zig with budget2. Exact matching identities give

    H_source=1, H_destination=n-1,
    Delta H=n-2>2 for n>=5.

R_joint is zero at both failed endpoints. At six keys H jumps 1->5 against budget2. The input pair is explicitly reachable from a diagonal and embedded in a nineteen-access closed paired walk, with A=20,Q_B=0,Z_B=12. This is a candidate counterexample, not a coarse-double counterexample.

The same family forces ANY valid repair to satisfy R_destination-R_source<=4-n. Thus, if R is nonnegative, it must hold at least n-4 reserve at these internally different equal-root sources. This excludes every nonnegative correction that vanishes on all equal-root pairs. It does not exclude signed joint corrections or the weaker normalization R(A,A)=0 on identical-tree diagonals.

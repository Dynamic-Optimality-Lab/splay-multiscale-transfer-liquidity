# No-double-spend proof (L6, standalone)

Every interval pays only its own debt from its own reserve R_j: the unique
payer p from R_p; all other positives from their own R_j (strict-K/P
implies H≥2, so R_j≥2≥w_j); nonpositives need nothing. No sink is
introduced; no credit transfers; no reserve reused; no A-cost belongs to
two H intervals; K/P overlap on one interval never duplicates credit; the
cyclic seam wraps exactly once. ∎

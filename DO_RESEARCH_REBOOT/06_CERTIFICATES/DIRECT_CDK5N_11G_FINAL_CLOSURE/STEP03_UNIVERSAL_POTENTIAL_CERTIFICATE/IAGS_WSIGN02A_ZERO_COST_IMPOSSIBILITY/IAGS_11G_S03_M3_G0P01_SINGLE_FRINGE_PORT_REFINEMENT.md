## D. Two deterministic refinement rules

Because -1 is the sole fringe key and is below every core key, a leaf
at core0's left side can be observed only in these two positions:

1. A root is core0 and its L child is the port. In shadow7 the same
   opaque port propagates to L, LL and LR. These are THREE readings
   of ONE port, not three distinct hidden subtrees.
2. A left child is core0 and its LL child is the port.

If the fringe leaf is deeper than depth two, it is invisible in alpha.
The refinement rho is therefore a function of the existing alpha:

    root0/L-port:
        L -> real anonymous fringe leaf; LL,LR -> empty.
    left-child0/LL-port:
        LL -> real anonymous fringe leaf.
    no observed special port:
        alpha unchanged.

All other fields remain fixed. The real fringe leaf has canonical
(kind,isS,S-index)=(0,0,0), with its ordinal beta derived in the usual
way. No hidden S label, inventory bit, port identity, or witness tree
is added to the graph state.

The port-only source family has 634 observed shadow classes.

256 shadow classes. There are 469,346 ordinary joint states, of which

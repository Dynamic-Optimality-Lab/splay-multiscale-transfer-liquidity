r_old=-Qbar(t)>=0 and shares exactly the same midpoint.

successor. Hence an exceptional helper plus its next repaying
transition, allowing that one neutral H=1 delay, satisfies

    sum r_old >= -1+0+1 = 0,
    sum w >= Theta_old(last)-Theta_old(first).

The first two exceptional patterns repay on the immediately next
transition. The third either repays immediately or follows the core6

does not mark core6, or an incompatible A shadow, simply removes the

psi=2 and lies outside C, whereas every successor before repayment

exception. For a finite path ending inside a block, at most one unit
of this exceptional debt remains unpaid. Repeated occurrences and cyclic
wraparound are counted with multiplicity, not identified or discarded.

This is universal compensation for the three pattern families at the
original chi8 interface. It does not assert nonnegative r_old for

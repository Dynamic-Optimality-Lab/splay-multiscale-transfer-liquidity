# CORE-01B Source-vs-Abstract Firewall (Gate I/H3, K)

Source-level facts do NOT transfer automatically: the abstract relation
admits witnesses no single source walk realizes (overapproximation);
conversely every certified grounded edge satisfies the abstract predicate.
Held boundary: rotation/DELETE/identity lemmas transfer because they are
properties of the DAG operators themselves (same code, any inputs).
NOT transferred without proof: any claim requiring a global concrete
representative, single-KEEP-ness of abstract witnesses, or absence of
unmarked intermediate targets. The firewall stands exactly here: the
unmarked-intermediate case is where source intuition and abstract
permission diverge, and neither side currently decides it.

# CORE-01B Endpoint Birth/Death Identity (PROVED identities, open inequality)

Define G = C_n \\ C_0 (new members), L = C_0 \\ C_n (lost members).
Then C_n = (C_0 \\ L) union G (disjoint) and rho(t)-rho(s) = |G|-|L|
(exact set arithmetic; no sign assumption). Universal cardinality
monotonicity is equivalent to |L| >= |G|; strong inclusion is G = {}.
These are identities, not theorems about size — the inequality remains
exactly the open obligation. Measured on 110 stored endpoints: |G|=0
everywhere (no growth observed), with 34 strict shrinks; the identities
hold trivially there.

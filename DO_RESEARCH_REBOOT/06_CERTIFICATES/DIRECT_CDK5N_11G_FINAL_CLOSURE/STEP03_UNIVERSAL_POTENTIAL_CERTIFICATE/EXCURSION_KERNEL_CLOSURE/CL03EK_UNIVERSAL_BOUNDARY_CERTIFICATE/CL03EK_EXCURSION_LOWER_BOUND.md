# CL-03EK Excursion Lower Envelope (OPEN; H2 equivalent)

K(u,v) = min excursion weight (EK-2 attainment conditional on H3).
Target K(u,v) >= C5(v)-C5(u) is exactly H2 restated per endpoint pair.
Evidence: 2 holding instances (R=3,5). Missing: any universal argument.
Investigated: entry/exit structural constraints (deep entry requires
rank-mass shifts that are unpriced; exits unconstrainable without path
control); A-shadow bounds (window too small to price deep transitions);
port/gap conservation (shape-only). The envelope problem is H2 in
min-plus clothing — no independent progress. A hostile case (excursion
whose endpoints satisfy C5(v)-C5(u) >> 0, i.e. the reverse of observed
instances) is neither exhibited nor excluded; the proof must cover it.

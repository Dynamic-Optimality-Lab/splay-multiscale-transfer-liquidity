# CL-03EK Unproved Cost Lemma (explicit open statement)

No lemma of the form "structural event X forces payment >= Y" has been
proved. Candidates examined and rejected-or-open: per-rotation cost
(rotations are weightless bookkeeping; cost lives in edge weights, not
rotations); A-cost floors (q'-cancellation defeats them); marked-depth
recovery (unbounded, unproved); root-restoration charging (no forced
sequence); port/gap conservation (shape-only); excursion-entry deficits
(descriptive, not self-repaying); min-plus attainment (existence, not
sign). The missing lemma is precisely: a source-derived NONNEGATIVE
functional of frozen transitions that telescopes to w(C) on closed
walks. Its absence is the blocking issue, not an oversight.

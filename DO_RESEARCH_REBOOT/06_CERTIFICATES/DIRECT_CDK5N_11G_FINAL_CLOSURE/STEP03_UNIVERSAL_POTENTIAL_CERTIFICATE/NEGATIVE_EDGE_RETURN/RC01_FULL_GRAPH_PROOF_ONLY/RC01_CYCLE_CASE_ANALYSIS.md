# RC-01 S-Only, Mixed, and High Cycle Analysis (all conditional/open)

S-only closed walks: core-flat by telescoping (conditional on universal
S-monotonicity; high edges unconditional, low conditional on witness
target-markedness). Flatness does NOT imply w>=0 (flat negative edges
unexcluded; none certified). U-containing walks: block decomposition
D_i = U_i P_i valid combinatorially; block cost bounds OPEN (no packet
mechanism). High-only cycles: core-flat (high universal) but cost OPEN.
Mixed: open on both. Negative-edge return-cost theorem: OPEN in full
generality (the precise missing piece is a forced-payment lemma for
returns, blocked by zero/negative-cost restoration steps and
q'-cancellation). No case is promoted beyond its proved status.

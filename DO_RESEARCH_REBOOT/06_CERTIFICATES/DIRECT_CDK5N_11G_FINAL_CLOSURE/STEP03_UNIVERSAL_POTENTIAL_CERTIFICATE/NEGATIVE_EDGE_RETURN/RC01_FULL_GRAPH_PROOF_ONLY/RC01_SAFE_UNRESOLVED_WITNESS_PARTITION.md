# RC-01 Safe/Unresolved Witness Partition (Gate S/U)

S (monotonicity-certified occurrences): contracted-high edges (universal
theorem); low edges with a witness whose every B-changing KEEP targets a
marked key (conditional theorem; includes all single-event edges).
U (unresolved): low occurrences whose chosen witness has an intermediate
unmarked-target B-changing KEEP. Witness-overlap noted: the same abstract
edge may admit both S and U witnesses; classification is per
edge-occurrence-with-chosen-witness, never a new graph label. For cycle
analysis every occurrence is labeled by its chosen witness; all results
must hold uniformly over witness choices. Consequence recorded: S-only
closed walks are core-flat (telescoping) but NOT thereby nonnegative.

# RC-01 Negative-Edge Debt Definition (exact, no mechanism claimed)

Low negative edge u -(-r)-> v: q_e = 2H_e + r (r = exact capacity
shortfall). High negative edge: q_e = 6 + r. The integer r is well-defined
per edge occurrence from its weight (and H for low edges). This defines
the DEBT but not its repayment: r is a number, not yet a structural
obstruction. No claim is made about where, when, or by what the r units
are paid. Any future packet system must start from these exact shortfalls.

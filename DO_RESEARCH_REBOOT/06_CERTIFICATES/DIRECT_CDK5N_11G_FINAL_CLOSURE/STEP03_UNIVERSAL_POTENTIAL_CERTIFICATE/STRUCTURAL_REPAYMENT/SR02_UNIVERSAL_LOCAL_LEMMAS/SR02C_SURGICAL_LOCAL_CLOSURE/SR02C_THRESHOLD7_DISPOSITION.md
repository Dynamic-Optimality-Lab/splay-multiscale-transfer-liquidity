# SR02C Threshold-7 Disposition

No threshold change was made (fixed candidate tested as specified). The
refutation is threshold-independent in force: violations occur at the
5->7 boundary where the guard flips branch selection from C5(s) to C2(t);
any finite threshold T in 1..8 admits analogous shallow->deep rank
explosions on one side of its boundary (mechanism: rank-mass jumps +3/+4
without depth warning). Whether SOME threshold avoids all violations is
a different candidate (not authorized, not tested, not claimed). The
regime story (chain vs balanced) survives as explanation but not as
justification: the failure is structural (rank explosion at crossings),
not parametric.

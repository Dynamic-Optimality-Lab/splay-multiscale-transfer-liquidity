# DH-01 Low Minimum Attainment (CONDITIONAL on L3; proved)

ASSUME L3 (no negative deep low closed walk). For shallow endpoints u,v
(resp. deep endpoints in the H3 context; same argument), K(u,v) = inf
w(P) over connecting low-only deep paths (or +inf if none). CLAIM: every
nonempty family attains its minimum. Proof: take P attaining within
epsilon; excise repeated vertices (each excised closed subwalk is a deep
low closed walk, hence >= 0 by L3, so excision does not increase weight);
terminate (finiteness: each excision removes >=1 edge; lengths bounded
below by 0); the result is simple (no repeated vertices) with weight <=
original. Simple paths in a finite vertex set are finite in number, so
the infimum over the original family equals the minimum over the finite
simple subfamily, attained. No computability claimed (state space
astronomical); no enumeration performed.

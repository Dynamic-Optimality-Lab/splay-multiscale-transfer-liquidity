# DH-01 Mixed Deep Nonnegativity (OPEN; conditional structure only)

Mixed walks (low+high deep edges) are covered by NEITHER the substitution
route (unproved) NOR the kernel sign route (unproved). The exact
conditional statement holds: IF L3 AND (substitution OR J_D sign) THEN
mixed walks are nonnegative (by contraction/expansion, proved above).
No component is discharged. Mixed walks with flat core are included in
the OPEN verdict (flatness gives no sign). Mixed walks with strict core
drops are included too (no per-edge sign from monotonicity alone).

# DH-01 No-Negative-Self-Loop Micro-Theorem (PROVED, narrow scope)

Claim: no single-event low edge and no contracted-high edge is a closed
loop with w<0 (in fact no self-loop at all in these classes).
Proof low: nev=1 (single active event); terminal genuine marked KEEP
asserts B-change (chg conjunct); inactive events are identities; hence
t.b != s.b as arrays, so s != t as 147-field states. Proof high: OM is a
single marked-target splay asserted genuine (diff), and t.b == g.b by
b_equal; hence t.b != s.b. SCOPE: multi-event low self-loops NOT ruled
out (intermediate+terminal changes could compose to identity in
principle); no claim about longer cycles. This eliminates ONLY the
length-1 case; H3 (arbitrary lengths) is unaffected. Recorded because it
is the strongest universal sign statement actually proved here.

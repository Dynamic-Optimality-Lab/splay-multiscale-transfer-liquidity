# DH-01 High-Only Nonnegativity (OPEN; conditional structure given)

Conditional claim: IF every high-only closed walk satisfied a per-cycle
weight bound following from core-membership constancy, H3's high fragment
would close. No such bound derived (q-average open; see separate doc).
What IS proved: core membership is exactly preserved along high-only
closed walks (not just cardinality). Strict core decrease by single high
edges is possible in principle (unmarked-subtree restructuring); no
stored high ground-truth edges exist to measure its frequency (all 18
observed strict descents are low edges). No negative high-only closed
walk certified; none ruled out.

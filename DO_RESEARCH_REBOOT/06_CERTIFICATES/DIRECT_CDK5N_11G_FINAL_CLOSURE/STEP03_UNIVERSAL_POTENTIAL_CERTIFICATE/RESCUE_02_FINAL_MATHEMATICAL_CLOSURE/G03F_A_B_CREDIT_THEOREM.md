# G03F A/B Credit Theorem (status: CONJECTURE with evidence, NOT proved)

Claim under investigation: every negative low edge `u -(-r)-> v` forces a
structural debt (B-progress without A-payment) that any return path
`v ~~c~~> u` must repay with `c >= r`, via (i) marked-depth recovery,
(ii) root restoration, (iii) A-shadow re-occupation, (iv) port/inventory
conservation. Supporting evidence: 183 liftable closed walks, totals in
{2,4,5,6,7,8}, none < 2; killer analysis shows rank/marks alone cannot
price deep-chain transitions, implicating missing A-side credit. Missing:
derivation of (i)-(iv) as universal lemmas from the frozen transition
semantics. The debt is therefore a precise conjecture with a falsifiable
shape, not a theorem. No potential is built on it.

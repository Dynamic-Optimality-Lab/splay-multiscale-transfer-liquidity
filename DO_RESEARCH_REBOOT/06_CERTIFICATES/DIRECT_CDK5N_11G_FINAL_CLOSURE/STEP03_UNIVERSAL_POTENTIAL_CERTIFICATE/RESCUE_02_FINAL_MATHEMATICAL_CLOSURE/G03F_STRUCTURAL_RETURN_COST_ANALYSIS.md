# G03F Structural Return-Cost Analysis (evidence, not proof)

Best-first concrete hunt (MSET, legs <= 5, most-negative-first, 783 legs,
101 closed walks): closed totals {2,4,5,6,7,8}, minimum +2, ZERO negative,
ZERO zero. Covering low OO families with E3/E4 shapes, root/deep starts,
chains/zigzags/random BSTs. Structural observations (unproved universally):
(i) every closed walk repays B-progress q with at least twice the A-cost
plus 2 (2ΣH − Σq ≥ 2 on sample); (ii) negative legs (w=-2,-1 sampled) never
recur in a returnable configuration within depth 5; (iii) deep-chain
transitions (dmax 8→7) carry positive weight on sample. None of (i)-(iii)
is a universal lemma; each would need derivation from the frozen transition
semantics (B-core/A-shadow evolution, port/inventory conservation). The
repayment mechanism is therefore CHARACTERIZED empirically but NOT proved —
this is the precise mathematical gap for any future Route-B attempt.

# Global conditional nonoverlap, without assuming individual routes suffice

The proof in NONLOCAL_AUGMENTATION handles ALL roots in a finite set at
once, with one monotonically updated comparison image and paid map.
It is not a collection of independently chosen successful paths.

Every escape excludes the CURRENT image of h, including all earlier
escapes. Thus comparison edges have distinct units globally. Every
payment excludes the CURRENT image of a, including all earlier root
payments. Thus effective terminal units are distinct globally. More
strongly, the unique-predecessor proof shows later paths cannot enter
earlier rooted components, including at their newly paid roots. All
unreached initial missing roots remain outside dom(a).

This proves simultaneous preservation/nonduplication CONDITIONAL on a
source recipe that remains valid at each reached frontier. A recipe
valid separately for each initial root is insufficient: it might offer
the same bit to several roots. Such a recipe does not satisfy the current-
image premise. No Hall lower bound is assumed in its place.

## Distinct exits required by distinct initial blocked components

Keep a0 fixed and form the initial h0/a0 rooted components. Suppose b
of them stop at undefined h0 frontiers. In a MONOTONE extension of h0,
each such terminal demand must acquire a comparison value before its
rooted walk can reach a free endpoint. These b frontiers are distinct
by backward uniqueness, so at least b new comparison edges are needed.
Their units must be distinct and outside the old image. Filling one
frontier cannot eliminate the undefined terminal in another initial
component; the old path to that terminal is unchanged. This is a lower
bound only for this specified monotone comparison-extension mechanism.

# Frozen-owner preservation at the conditional exchange interface

Let f_E be the original certified smaller-support map on the actual
aligned I, C the common occurrence/ordinal demands and l=f_E restricted
to C. The original successful h0/l partial exchange gives a0. Every
entry of l and every entry of a0 is a required frozen full assignment.
The archived f_E also retains all its original values; E-only claims
are retired only in the ALTERNATIVE effective full ledger, as inherited.

The proposed walk never rewrites any a entry. An occupied unit leads
to its exact current owner, regardless of whether that owner is common,
prior full, or newly paid during the current run. The endpoint test is
membership outside CURRENT im(a), not merely outside im(l). Appending
p->u therefore cannot spend an effective full owner again. Every earlier
map value is literal equality, not an equality of totals.

Adding a comparison edge does not assign an effective owner. Its unit
may already be owned, and the walk must continue in that case. The
source-certified comparison is an independent accounting witness under
the inherited licensed exchange, not a simultaneous second spending
of the archived auxiliary map. A fresh comparison-image tail is not
automatically a free frozen-owner terminal.

For an inherited complete reset-family comparison the all-paid exchange
receipt supplies the required source eligibility. The lazy specialization
is the same rooted path computation. For a NEW general escape, a valid
source receipt remains an essential premise; mere typing/injectivity
does not authorize a new edge. Every new trace is producer-only pending
independent audit. No universal owner-compatible supply follows solely
from this preservation proof.

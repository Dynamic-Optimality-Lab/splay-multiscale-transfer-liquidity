## Explicit ongoing retained credit and its limitations

The fourth and later A events of each retained request are source-locally
excluded: if j_before>=0 events occurred earlier in that macro, then its
fourth event has macro ordinal j_before+4>=4 and cannot be among the first
three selected events. Later events obey the same order inequality.
Each actual retained occurrence has disjoint event/bit IDs. This proves
the retained-tail corollary for arbitrary lengths and inventories, rather
than observing unused capacity in a finite example.

The surplus reserve is additionally computed from the actual certificate
debt transitions. Both reserves are independent of full B cost. They can
vanish. The theorem supplies no bound comparing D_z+J_z to their total,
and no injection of remaining original B demands. Those are Step2.
The signed cancellation interface retains negative interference explicitly.

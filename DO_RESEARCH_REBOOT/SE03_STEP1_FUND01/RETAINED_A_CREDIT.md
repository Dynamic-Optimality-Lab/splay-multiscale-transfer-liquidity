# Source-local retained A credit that auxiliary funding never consumes

The constructive funding theorem yields more than an unspecified leftover
count: its tail reserve has a source-local rule independent of full B.

In every actual auxiliary macro, BOTH units of every A event after its
first three are excluded from auxiliary funding. This conclusion holds
even when some earlier selected units are carried to a later macro.

In particular, let an ORIGINAL RETAINED KEEP occurrence have a_t actual
A StepEvs. Its fourth and later A events occur no earlier than fourth
in their containing auxiliary macro, irrespective of previous DELETEs
or other nongenuine requests. Consequently at least

    2(a_t-3)_+

units of that continuing retained A work are explicitly reserved. Their
IDs are precisely the two bits of those actual fourth-and-later events.
Different source occurrences and event indices give disjoint units, so

    |retained_tail_reserve| >= sum_retained_KEEP 2(a_t-3)_+.

This is a subset of the already defined global tail reserve; it must not
be added to that reserve a second time. The same per-request statement
also holds for any other A request, but no claim is made that erased-key
work alone supplies the needed original marginal.

Shallower retained work may also release certified surplus s_i through
the debt-bank procedure. Those units are named by the actual FIFO/pop
receipts and remain disjoint from every auxiliary payment. A release
may include an older carried unit; the receipt retains its original
source event/time rather than pretending a new event minted it.

No lower bound relating this identified credit stream to D_z+J_z has
been proved. Such signed preservation is Step2. The corollary establishes
a genuine ongoing source of retained-A capacity and its exact exclusion
from auxiliary funding, without assuming any universal repayment result.

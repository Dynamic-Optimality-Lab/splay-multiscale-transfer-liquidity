consecutive H1/Q2/beta2/z1/u0 zero macros, at occurrences5r+6..6r+1

For every constant k choose r-4>k. Near the run's end, the preceding k

not imply a lower bound on every possible nonlocal source repair.

not extrapolation from the r7/8 producer records.

terminal is claimed free. In the exact erased13 instance two selected

The source principle extracted is measured, disjoint low-Q preparation
headroom, not a claim that long chains always reset or that a shared

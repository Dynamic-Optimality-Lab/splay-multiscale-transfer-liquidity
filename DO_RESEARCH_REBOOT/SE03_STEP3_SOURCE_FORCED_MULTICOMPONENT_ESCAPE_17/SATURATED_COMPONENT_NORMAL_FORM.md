# Exact cut for a fixed comparison; source-complete saturation is open

Status: PROVED-DERIVATION / AUDIT-PENDING at the fixed source-comparison
scope below. The full source-complete closure required by Gate C is
NOT claimed.

Freeze actual D,U, injective paid a and injective partial comparison h.
Use the inherited C16 no-repeat/unique-predecessor result. A blocked
missing-root path has demands d0=p,d1,...,d_l=r, with h undefined at r
and h(d_i)=a(d_(i+1)) for i<l. Only its root is outside dom(a).
For C={d0,...,d_l}, its exact internal boundary certificate is

    h(C\{r}) = a(C\{p}),
    |h(C)|=|C|-1, and no h(C) unit is free under a.     (C1)

These are equalities of typed physical IDs. Different missing-root
components are disjoint in both demand and comparison-unit sets by
the inherited backward-uniqueness proof. For any b blocked components,
with P their root set and F their undefined endpoint set, disjoint union
gives h(C\F)=a(C\P) and |h(C)|=|C|-b. There are b distinct holes.
This is an exact failure certificate for THIS fixed comparison, not
an assertion that every other source-licensed edge has been considered.

Any initial comparison-tail unit is outside this internal unit set.
If it is assigned under a, its owner cannot be in C\P, whose a units
are all already comparison-image units. It cannot be a missing root
either. Thus a fresh-image source edge leaves these old rooted
components; it may enter a different paid component and require another
source escape. This observation does not assume a free terminal.

## Full source closure requirement

For a genuinely saturated collection one must close under EVERY legal
source ownership exchange at the actual owner state, including new
source supplier rules and any permitted comparison-image reassignment.
C16 did not give a proved complete static eligibility graph. Adding the
new preparation rule to its known relation does not establish that
the union contains all possible source exchanges. No Hall neighborhood
is declared complete by definition, and no failed search is a cut proof.

A dynamic source-complete closure would have to retain all original
block/chart/period guards as effective ownership changes. Its existence
and completeness are still OPEN. C1 is the exact normal form available
without that missing source theorem. It cannot certify a counterexample
to U17-ESCAPE or to Coarse-Double.

## Cyclic edge lifetimes

At completed original-access boundaries, let E_(p,x) mean that x has
immediate unmarked parent p. Its Boolean value closes over the A period.
The frontier's positive access to x makes x root, a true-to-false edge
death. Pair it to the immediately preceding false-to-true entry of the
SAME labeled edge, cyclically. Boolean births/deaths alternate; repeated
deaths cannot share a birth. The creator operation cannot itself access
x, since it ends with x root. Internal edge destruction/recreation within
an access is irrelevant to this completed-boundary predicate. Every
original DELETE, root repeat and source occurrence remains.

## At most two relevant creations per A StepEv

Let v be the creator access target. A new edge(p,x), x marked,p unmarked,
x!=v, is attributed to an actual creating StepEv. If p!=v, p is a consumed
G/P operand and its child links stay rigid under later outer frames;
attribute it to p's unique consuming event. If p=v, a NEW marked child
of v is a consumed path ancestor; attribute it to that operand's event.
Later frames retain a proper child or replace it by a new ancestor, but
do not consume a processed ancestor again. Thus a final net creation
is a surviving subset of the following exact frame creations.

For LL,

    G(P(v(A,B),C),D) -> v(A,P(B,G(C,D))).

Apart from the outside attachment to the EXCLUDED accessed target v,
new qualifying edges can only be:

1. v->P if v unmarked/P marked;
2. P->G if P unmarked/G marked;
3. P->root(B) if P unmarked/root(B) marked;
4. G->root(C) if G unmarked/root(C) marked.

If P is marked only1/4 can qualify. If P unmarked/G marked only2/3;
if P,G unmarked only3/4. At most TWO. RR has the reflected calculation.
For LR,

    G(P(A,v(B,C)),D) -> v(P(A,B),G(C,D)),

the P side contributes at most one of v->P and P->root(B), by exclusive
marked/unmarked type of P. The G side contributes at most one of v->G
and G->root(C), analogously. Again at most TWO; RL is reflected. A zig
has at most ONE: its old parent is either marked and becomes a child
of an unmarked target, or is unmarked and receives a marked middle-root.

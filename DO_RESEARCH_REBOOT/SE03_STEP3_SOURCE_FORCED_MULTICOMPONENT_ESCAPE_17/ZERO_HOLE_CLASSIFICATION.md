# Exact natural zero-hole restriction, inherited provisionally

Status: PROVED-DERIVATION / AUDIT-PENDING. The exact C16 proof is
reconstructed at its actual MAXIMAL full-core scope, not extended to
all packetizations or smaller full cores.

For an actual natural macro let H be ALL original A StepEvs, Q the
original full B doubles, u unary old marked ancestors, beta branching
old marked ancestors, z the marked-leaf target indicator. The actual
nearest-marked-parent source identity gives Q=u+beta, DeltaL=u-z.
The defined comparison reserves one first-A bit if z1 and funds beta
branch demands with the remaining own bits. Hence its undefined count
is R_e=(beta+z-2H)_+.

For an integer selector0<=c<=H and Q=2c,

    beta+z-2H=z-u-2(H-c).

It is positive exactly at H=c,z1,u0 and then equals1. All 2H marked
ancestors are branching, so each has a disjoint off-path marked child
component. One marked witness per such component, together with the
2H distinct ancestors and target, requires r>=4H+1. No reverse
realizability of every syntactic candidate is asserted.

Thus at r7/8 a natural zero-hole necessarily has H1,Q2,beta2,z1. Its
single positive A access targets the final marked x, at actual depth1
or2. Before it zero-A accesses are to the prior root y; after it they
are to x. The positive operation is either the final KEEP or a DELETE x
followed by zero DELETE x accesses and that KEEP. Internal root-y KEEPs
remain in the erased execution. The six A events are zig-L/R, LL/RR,
LR/RL, with the actual ordered middle operand, marked or anonymous.
The B trace has two actual same-side marked/unmarked doubles with the
two old marked ancestors branching. Arbitrary private BSTs are retained.

At r>=9 the H2/Q4 class is not excluded, at r>=13 H3/Q6 is not excluded,
etc. A genuine natural macro has H>=1; a zero-A root repeat is retained
as an identity with no full demand but may matter to erased execution.
Positive-A/QF0 gaps retain their cost and bits. QF0 is never identified
with QE0. A zero-total multi-macro packet can contain nonzero-price
constituents and is not reduced to this one-macro classification.

Each comparison edge and inverse-owner edge retains the source trees,
BE provenance, original word/events, charts and interval references.
An inverse-owner edge need not follow chronological zero macros. The
known grammar is source-complete for this LOCAL natural-hole test,
but not a source-complete list of all legal escapes or packet/owner
interactions. That part of Gates A/C remains OPEN. Required independent
verification of these C16 deductions remains pending under the cap.

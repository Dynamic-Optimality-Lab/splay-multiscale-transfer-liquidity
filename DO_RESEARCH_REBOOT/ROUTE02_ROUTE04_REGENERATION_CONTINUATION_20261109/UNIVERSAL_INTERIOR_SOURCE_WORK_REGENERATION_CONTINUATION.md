## 2. A global physical leaf-retirement ticket

### 2.1 Retirement is forced in the original period

A leaf birth in a closed period must have a later cyclic retirement. Otherwise repeating the identical closed period would attempt to recreate {g} while the same old leaf persists.

Its first subsequent KEEP g is the actual retirement access. No other KEEP can retire it. That access is nontrivial in B, since a proper singleton cannot contain the global B root. Within the splay it is removed at the first actual promotion of target g; a first-SS parent rotation before that need not retire it.

Distinct leaf births have DISTINCT retirement KEEP occurrences:

* Different g require different actual target occurrences.
* Two births at the same g need an intervening retirement before the second birth. They cannot reuse one label run or one cyclic occurrence.

This is injectivity of original runs, not a per-key credit multiplied by the number of keys. A causal lift counts each physical ID only once.

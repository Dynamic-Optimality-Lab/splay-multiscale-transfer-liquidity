## 3. Search for a genuine compensation mechanism

Attempt to derive an explicitly defined, source-certified compensation rule that pays erasure's positive marginal from distinct A-credit units not already committed to auxiliary B-double demands.

A candidate may choose \(z\) using a proved source-geometric criterion, such as an exposed marked-core leaf. No favorable key-selection theorem may be assumed.

The compensation rule must specify:

1. Which exact original source rotations create an eligible credit.
2. Which erasure-induced B-double demand that credit pays.
3. How indirect effects on later KEEP accesses are accounted for.
4. Why no unit can be used twice.
5. Why the construction persists under arbitrary source support, ambient fringes and repeated cycles.
6. How boundary corrections cancel or are paid on the actual closed period.

Do not assert \(\Delta_z\le R_z\) as the unproved bridge. Prove a more primitive source identity or physical charging rule that independently implies the necessary inequality.

Test every proposed sign law analytically against the existing universal hanger obstruction before promoting it.

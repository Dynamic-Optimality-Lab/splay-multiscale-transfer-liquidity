# A source-preparation repayment theorem for paired cold packets

This theorem supplies an independent donor rule on a structural subclass.
It does not assert the guard for all reachable histories. In particular,
it is not the desired global inequality restated as an injection.

Work in an original reachable, fully closed six-genuine-KEEP source
history of full core11, aligned with an actual recurrent erased five-key
history. Retain the fixed Step1 fE, actual period I, and the common ordinal
demand matching. No bank, cost, tree, source operation or frozen edge is
changed. Use the sealed core11 alternation and maximum-depth successor
theorem: every q5 macro is followed by a genuine q1 macro, and these
packets are disjoint. A packet has exactly six original B-double demands.
Its H2 members have exactly two actual A StepEvs; no stronger A bound is
assumed from maximal-core alternation alone.

## Source guard and deterministic rule

Choose any nonempty collection of these packets to fund. Their cyclic
preparation gaps run from the end of the preceding chosen packet to the
start of the next chosen packet, excluding both packets. The gap may
contain other original source operations or already funded packets.
These gaps are disjoint sets of actual source operations.

For each chosen packet require a triple of actual A StepEvs in its gap
which belong to one auxiliary macro and have macro ordinals at least4.
This is the SOURCE-PREPARATION GUARD. It can be certified by actual source
paths without looking at the packet's P or any global demand count. For
example, exactly two earlier A events in that macro followed by an A
access at actual depth>=7 inside the gap supplies at least four events;
its last three give macro ordinals4,5,6. Xi realizes this through D8 at
depth7. More generally a certified six-event auxiliary prefix in the gap
contains the ordinal4,5,6 triple. No depth is inferred from an erased B
path or from a normalized-chart residual.

Among qualifying triples choose the lexicographically first one in
oriented gap order, then macro order. Each triple is ordered by actual A
source-event order. Reserve both bits of these three events as a six-slot
block for that packet BEFORE adding any other new original-demand module.
Macro membership can be determined at its later genuine E KEEP; the
macro may end inside the packet. It need not end in the preparation gap.
This allows the Omega+ preparation, which belongs to the macro ending at
the packet's critical K11.

List the packet's unmatched full demands in source order, B-double ordinal
within each KEEP. Assign its jth such demand to slot j of its reserved
block, where slots order the three events and then bit0,bit1.

## Proof of funding and ownership

1. Step1 mints only the first three A events of an auxiliary macro into
   its working queue. Every bit of a later event is outside the image of
   the ENTIRE fixed fE, independently of D and all intermediate helper
   arrangements. Thus all six selected slots are actual members of U.

2. A packet has exactly q5+q1=6 full demands. Its unmatched sublist is a
   subset of those six, so the explicit ordinal rule is defined. This is
   a source-rotation bound, not an assumption that total P fits total U.

3. Distinct chosen packets have disjoint preparation gaps. An actual A
   event therefore cannot belong to two selected triples. The two bits
   of one event have distinct physical IDs. All within- and between-
   packet assignments are injective. No negative-interference receipt
   fE(N) is needed or spent.

4. Keep every original common assignment fE(C). It is disjoint from U.
   Mark each reserved slot with its unique packet owner before later
   modules run. Unused slots remain reserved or may be released by an
   explicit receipt; they are not silently offered to another class.
   This gives global ownership for THIS selected packet module.

This is valid for repeated packets and arbitrary zero/+1 source transfers
within their preparation gaps, provided the guard holds: those source
operations retain their true macro ordinals and cannot duplicate a slot.
It proves no donor existence or funding for their own unmatched demands.
Their payments require separately disjoint receipts. A negative signed
chart term is not itself a physical released donor.

## Existing allocations and the cyclic seam

If a prior original-demand receipt already funds a target, retain that
receipt and skip the target here. Do not superimpose a fresh alternative
allocation on it. For composition with OTHER prior original-demand
receipts, the triple must consist of slots not owned by those receipts;
otherwise the guard for that extension fails. The clean theorem above
starts with the fixed fE(C) and creates its own packet reservations. It
does not erase any inherited original receipt or presume it can be moved.

Choose a packet-boundary description of the cyclic blocks for the rule,
then transport demand and donor IDs together to the legal actual Step1
period I. I contains one actual occurrence of every cyclic source role.
Its cut is an auxiliary boundary, so each selected event keeps its true
macro ordinal. All receipts use actual units of I, never warmup funds or
an extra period copy. Within a preparation-plus-packet block donors can
precede their targets. Where existing family receipts fund earlier
demands by later donors, only cyclic block accounting is claimed. This
theorem supplies no new native matching-terminal eligibility claim.

is false by PACKET_LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md. This guarded theorem and Xi's
complete eight-demand receipt are unconditional on their STATED source
subclasses; the missing universal guard remains a mathematical gap.

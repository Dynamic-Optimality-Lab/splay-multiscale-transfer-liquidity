# Exact backward zero frontier and the final two packets

Target: usable source necessary conditions for B0=Pre0*(F11).
Conditions: actual closed core11, genuine packet boundaries and actual
recent order. Inheritance: incoming H1/Q2 zeros, H1/Q1 +1 at F11,
all-SS marked-parent bridge, cold-key leaf property and deltaL=a-b.
Ambiguities: the following BF shapes are necessary, not sufficient for
full-state F11/B0 membership. Bridge: count marked children created by
the actual incoming splay, then preserve A/cold/auxiliary predicates.

## Cold forced-zero shapes

A q5 source is a six-mark chain, L1. A COLD q4 source has a five-mark
path ending at its marked-leaf target z and one off-path mark. It has
EXACTLY two marked leaves. Its root marked degree is1 or2:

- Stem: root degree1, with the unique extra leaf attached inside the
  long path. The chosen target is at marked depth4.
- Fork41: root degree2, consisting of one marked leaf and a FOUR-mark
  chain. The deep chain leaf is the potential q4 target.

Both use actual BF shape; a legal physical same-side path of depth8/10,
actual cold status and H1 A geometry are additionally required. For q5,
both genuine macros must have H1 and the actual forced q1 successor.
The full BE and partition context still have to match an admitted source.

## Lemma Z1: every zero destination has at least two marked leaves

A q2 splay makes its two old marked ancestors children of the new root,
so it has at least two marked branches/leaves. A q4 splay makes four
old ancestors direct children and has at least4 leaves. A q5/q1 packet
has exactly4 leaves. These are the complete zero types, including all
H2 helpers. Therefore no zero edge enters L1.

In particular a nonempty zero suffix cannot end at a cold q5 source.
Nor can it end at a Stem cold q4 source: q2/q4 outputs have marked
root degrees>=2/4, while the only root-degree1 paired output has L4.

## Lemma Z2: every last zero into F11 is neutral L2->L2

The inherited final-entry theorem leaves only H1/Q2. Its destination
must be Fork41. Let r be the incoming target. That output root has two
marked children (the old ancestors), and no room for an old proper
marked child of r. Thus r was a marked leaf. One old ancestor is now
a marked leaf and the other is a four-mark chain; exactly one ancestor
had no off-path marks. Hence a=1,b=1 and deltaL=0. Source and target
both have L2. This is stronger than the old final H/Q restriction.

The complete possible marked-parent source shapes are:

1. J2: root with chain2 and chain3 branches. The inner ancestor has
   no off-path mark; the other ancestor owns a three-mark off-path chain.
2. K2: root has one marked child h; h has the incoming leaf r and a
   three-mark chain as children. The outer ancestor has no off-path mark.

The exact A target r must have depth1/2, all physical BF pairs must be
SS, and the destination must have its true cold depth-four target at
A depth1/2. These predicates are not removed by the marked count.

## Lemma Z3: the backward frontier has a fixed terminal tail

No zero edge enters K2: it has marked root degree1 and L2. A zero
entering J2 must be an H1/Q2 LOSS3->2. Indeed higher zero types output
at least4 leaves. For q2, output L2 requires the incoming target to
have no old marked child; deltaL=a-1. If a=0, both old ancestors remain
nonleaves and form chains2/3: source L3. If a=1, output is Fork41,
not J2. The case a=2 would leave only the two ancestors plus target,
contradicting six marks. There is no other possibility.

Therefore ANY zero path of length>=2 ending in F11 has EXACT terminal
pair

    H1/Q2 lowering3->2 ; H1/Q2 neutral2->2 ; F11.

For length1 the source is J2 or K2. A post-first-loss source is never
K2, so a one-packet suffix is possible only from an initial J2 (the
E1 first-loss3->2 class). Empty suffixes were excluded separately.

This proof handles arbitrary prefix length and arbitrarily many earlier
restorations. It does NOT erase them or forget their A/BE/chart effects.
It reduces the final obstruction to exact A exposure at one marked
Fork41 frontier, with the last-loss provenance still required.

## Zero-only regeneration and positive-one placement

Starting in P0/P1, L>=2 remains true along every zero path. A paired
H3/Q6 zero requires a q5 source, which has L1. Thus NO paired zero can
occur ANYWHERE in either zero-only suffix. H2/Q4 zeros are NOT excluded:
they start at L2 and restore to4/5. By Z3, every reached L2 state is
J2 or Fork41; J2 has no admitted q4 target. Thus along such a zero
suffix they can only start at Fork41. Stem/K2 cannot be reached by a
zero and are not the initial J2/J3. A restoration cannot occur inside
the terminal pair above.

An H2/Q3 +1 output has at least three marked children and L>=3. If it
occurs in an E0 suffix ending with a zero edge, a later3->2 loss is
mandatory. Such a +1 cannot be the final incoming packet into F11.
The final +1, if any, is H1/Q1 as inherited.

With one +1 available, L1 can be reached only immediately AFTER that
positive packet, and that packet must be H1/Q1, not H2/Q3. Consequently
an H3/Q6 paired zero in an E0 one-unit suffix can occur at most ONCE,
immediately after its sole +1 has produced L1. A zero step cannot
preserve or recreate L1, and the pair ends at L4. This class is retained,
not silently discarded from E0.

## What remains missing

Fork41/cold-B geometry is only a relaxed frontier. The decisive missing
predicate is the cold key's ACTUAL A-depth<=2 (and the legal paired
successor for q5), with complete-source provenance. Matching BF masks
or recency orders does not prove F11 or B0 membership. The new witness
reaches Fork41 with a truly cold physical depth8 BF target, but its
A target depth8 fails the predicate. No B0 membership is claimed there.

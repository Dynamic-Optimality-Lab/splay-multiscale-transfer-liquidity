## Universal ordinary-A turnaround theorem

Let g<a<b be any three present keys. Start A rooted at g. Suppose the
next positive A accesses are a,b,g and each uses ONE actual StepEv.
Actual zero identities between them do not change A. Then its exact
resulting shape is

    g(U, a(V, b(W,Z))),                              (S)

with the ACTUAL ordered hanging subtrees U,V,W,Z. Every key z>a other
than b is in W or Z, so has depth>=3. Reflection gives the corresponding
decreasing-order statement. Anonymous parents and populations are unrestricted.

Proof. After a, old g is at L or LL by the six one-event formulas.
Splaying the larger b takes a right-going source path, leaves old a at
L or LL, and retains a's old left subtree whole. Therefore g's depth
is at least2. Its one-event return forces depth EXACTLY2, with a at L
and g at LL. Thus the pre-return tree is b(a(g(U,V),W),Z). The exact
ordinary LL formula gives(S). A longer unknown path or anonymous
intermediate parent would make the return cost more than one event.
This proves the statement uniformly, not by enumerating finite A trees.

## Conditional upper-cut shielding invariant

In(S), a is the first key at least a on any path from the low root;
all keys>=a lie in its rooted subtree, and a's right child is b.
If every subsequent ORIGINAL access target is<a, then a remains the
first upper-cut node and its right-child subtree b(W,Z) stays whole.
Consequently every z>a, z!=b remains at depth>=3 at each boundary.

Proof by the original source frames. Frames not containing a reattach
its term whole. When a is a grandparent, a low target uses LL/LR and
reattaches its right child b unchanged. When a is a parent of the
processed low target, its outside parent is below the cut and only the
RL crossing case is possible; again a's right child stays b. There
cannot be an upper ancestor outside a's subtree by the cut condition.
The corresponding zig also retains that right child. Induction over
every actual frame preserves the cut condition. After a low access the
root is<a, so a has depth>=1, b depth>=2, and W/Z depths>=3.

This invariant covers arbitrary numbers/costs of accesses BELOW the
cut, including anonymous helpers there. It does NOT claim preservation
under helpers or marked accesses at/above a. Such an upper crossing
can occur in an affordable zero packet and is an unresolved regeneration
mechanism. No positive repayment price is inferred merely from crossing.

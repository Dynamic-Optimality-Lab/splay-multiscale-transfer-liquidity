# Exact conditions for lifting saved debts by marked subsets

This examines the sealed certificates rather than executing their accepted
semantic rows. It does not enlarge their masks or prove unrestricted CD.

## 1. The valid maximum-debt lifting lemma

Suppose that for each k-element F subset M one has a well-defined debt D_F
on the SAME actual full source and destination states, and define

    D_M(s)=max_(F subset M, |F|=k) D_F(s).

For an access x, assume an admissible F containing x attains the SOURCE
maximum, D_F(s)=D_M(s), and its proved original edge law is

    w+D_F(t)-D_F(s)>=0.

Since D_M(t)>=D_F(t), adding D_M(t)-D_F(t)>=0 proves

    w+D_M(t)-D_M(s)>=0.                                   (1)

This is a correct uniform mathematical lemma. It requires a SOURCE witness
including x, proper common-state definitions and an actually covered edge.
The earlier core-seven eta argument used a destination witness for the
opposite sign convention; its padding property was proved there. No such
padding property is inherited automatically for M3's signed debt.

## 2. The saved M3 guards do not have the required padding theorem

Read-only syntax analysis of the sealed Step4 and completion clauses finds
55 Step4 clauses and628 completion clauses with FIVE positive marked
literals. A particular five-literal guard cannot retain all five marks
while adding an outside accessed key to a five-element subset. The
completion also contains542 one-negative-literal clauses and34 two-negative-
literal clauses. Adding the accessed key can negate one of those guards.

These facts reject the shortcut that every active guard has a freely
paddable witness of size<=4. They do NOT prove that the whole maximum
potential fails: other active clauses or D0 might supply a different
maximizing witness. Such an implication needs its OWN universal proof.
The static report does not evaluate any raw source state, new mask,
private completion or semantic row. Baseline D0 is not analyzed by it.

## 3. The five-core domain cannot be assumed for a retained deep target

For any actual B source and any F containing accessed x, C_F(B) contains
the entire original root-to-x path. If d_B(x)>=9 then |C_F(B)|>=10,
even if |F|=5. Such a source is outside the closed five-key core<=9
domain on which the sealed original CD(5) decomposition is based. The
bound2|F|-1 was a CLOSED-WALK theorem, not a bound on an arbitrary state
obtained by forgetting marks in a larger-support walk.

In particular, a six-key core11 q5 access has depth10, and EVERY five-
subset containing its target still has source core11. No unchanged-core,
unchanged-depth image into the nine-core M3 domain is supplied by choosing
a different subset. The arbitrary-signature theorem does not change this
core-size fact. Its unbounded signatures refer to private inventories in
the frozen five-mark/nine-core model, not arbitrary genuine KEEP support.

Recurrently aligning a genuinely erased history can reduce that core,
but then its actual B state and query charge can change. The resulting
signed discrepancy is precisely the unsolved repayment problem. This
subset method cannot silently bypass it.

## 4. Correct continuation after these obstructions

For fixed core<=9 one could still seek a proved common-state potential
and source-witness theorem using clause redundancy, with all outer guards
and D0 included. That would be a restricted extension only. Beyond that
core, a new source reduction or a new unbounded-depth potential is needed.
No bounded subset proof or syntactic clause count establishes the uniform
support implication. The source-complete signed repayment gate stays OPEN.

# Open uniform signed compensation theorem

Status: OPEN. This file records the exact unproved obligation; it is not
a theorem certificate and does not satisfy the requested completion gate.

For arbitrary r>=6 and an original reachable fully closed gamma with r
genuine KEEP keys, assume only the completed smaller-support CD(r-1).
The inherited aligned identity is R=R_z-D_z-J_z. A valid proof must obtain
nonduplicating repayment through actual source operations, not assert
D_z+J_z<=R_z as a new lemma equivalent to the conclusion.

One sufficient constructive package is to identify source-selected physical
A pools U_z,V_z, disjoint by actual event/copy/occurrence/bit identity, such
that the ACTUAL aligned erased B demands fit U_z by a proved independent
funding theorem, while the unmatched original demands fit V_z together
with units released by negative interference. Remaining A units may be
unused. Every allocation must come from source identities and proved
capacity, not from assuming that total original demand already fits.

The excursion lemma supplies this package when the auxiliary A history
can omit source-proved A-identity intervals and an independent marginal
bound pays from their reserved units. It has not been proved that these
intervals or that bound exist in general. The startup/safe-piece lemma
supplies another conditional mechanism, with an explicit strong guard
that has not been proved universally satisfiable.

New source obstructions show that repayment cannot in general be charged
only to direct erased B doubles, to a bounded number of erased occurrences,
or to a bounded z-episode fee. Six-key/core9 histories already have D0 and
J>=2m-2 through retained backbone repetitions, while all F/E boundary terms
vanish on the aligned recurrent block. A valid mechanism must account for
continuing retained A work, a genuinely stronger independent auxiliary
funding theorem, or a different exact source decomposition.

No such source-complete law is proved here, in particular for selected
five-key/core9 auxiliary histories arising from full six-key cores10/11.
No unproved source gap is assigned PASS. All existing proofs retain their
original scopes and the requested uniform support implication stays OPEN.

# Failed scalar discrepancy potentials and their corrected obligation

These are rejected candidates, not counterexamples to original CD. Each
rejection uses the source identities in the companion transport proof.
They do not terminate the campaign.

## Candidate A common retained access spends a maximum pending debt

Consider Phi(F,E)=max(0,max_(v in M0)(h_F(v)-h_E(v))). The conjectured law
on a common retained Ky is j_y<=Phi_before-Phi_after. Formula (5) before
Kv has every node's depth difference in[-2,2], so Phi_before<=1; y attains1.
After (6), y still attains1. Therefore Phi_before=1 and Phi_after>=1 while
j_v=1. This contradicts the proposed law for every legal hanger substitution.
M0 contains v and y and may contain any additional future retained keys.

Source reason: common root equality resets only the accessed coordinate.
Other keys can retain a unit and new coordinates can acquire a unit by
parity. The maximum cannot record how many later consumptions remain.

Improved candidate: retain signed keywise transport with its exact cross-key
production term, and fund that term with actual independent A capacity.
Equation (9), rather than the failed maximum inequality, is the starting law.

## Candidate B bounded injection per erased A event for a sum of debts

At a diagonal T=y(H,p(B,z(C,D))), let H contain m marked retained nodes,
and count y as retained. Kz is one RR double, with a_z=1 and two A bits.
Every node in H moves down exactly2 in F while E freezes, and y moves from
depth0 to2. Thus these m+1 coordinates each gain exactly1 in h difference.
For the positive pending sum Phi_plus=sum max(e(v),0), which starts0,

    Phi_plus_after >= m+1.

There is no uniform bound Phi_plus_after<=2a_z when m>=2. Arbitrarily many
future retained marks may lie in H. This is a universal parameterized hanger
argument, not enumeration of m-node trees and not a claim that all injected
units can be independently realized as later total demand.

Source reason: one displaced hanger contains arbitrarily many tracked keys.
Positive pending mass counts the same geometric displacement once per key.

Improved candidate: share geometric discrepancy between keys, retaining
signed cancellations when they are accessed. Such sharing needs a proved
service/transfer law; a bounded maximum alone already fails Candidate A.

## Candidate C raw depth discrepancy telescopes all indirect interference

Closed F/E depth vectors have equal initial/final discrepancy. This does
not imply J=0: costs are queried coordinates, reset one at a time. Exact
renewal accounting leaves the cross-key term in (9). The accepted SE02
two-key return family additionally has J=1 per period with closed F/E.
That inherited obstruction is reused, not rerun or claimed as novel.

Improved candidate: prove a signed source law for the complete remainder
in (9), including direct D, with an independently reserved auxiliary budget.
An assertion D+J<=R_z without that mechanism is still circular.

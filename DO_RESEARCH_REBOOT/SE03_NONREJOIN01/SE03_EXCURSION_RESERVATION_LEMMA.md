# Independent auxiliary funding from removable A excursions

Status: structural conditional lifting lemma. This establishes a noncircular
funding method even when only scalar CD(r-1) is available. Its source-complete
existence and signed marginal bound are separate OPEN obligations.

Let gamma be an original reachable fully paired-closed period, with r>=6
genuine KEEP keys, and choose z. Select disjoint occurrence intervals P_i
whose only original modes are DELETE and KEEP z. Each interval must be an
exact A identity: its full labeled A output equals its input. Selection is
made from these source identities, not from the desired slack inequality.
Let E>=0 be the sum of their actual A StepEv counts. Intervals can be cut
inside one cyclic period; all physical IDs retain occurrence and copy.

Construct the auxiliary word by erasing every Kz and removing each P_i.
Every operation removed would freeze auxiliary B after erasure. Removing
P_i preserves every remaining A input and output exactly, because P_i is
an A identity. Hence A_aux per period is A-E, with its remaining events
identical to original events. The auxiliary B period map is exactly the
same as in the uncontracted erased history. No B-return hypothesis is used.

Start from the original reached pair and repeat this auxiliary word. A
returns after each period. Finite-map recurrence supplies preperiod u and
period multiple L; original repetition and auxiliary repetition close on
the aligned comparison block. Reachability is through a legal changed word
from the original reachable pair. At retained boundaries its A source
events equal the corresponding original events; only selected identity
blocks were omitted. Its genuine KEEP support is at most r-1. Therefore
CD(r-1), used at precisely its scalar scope, supplies

    Q_E(I) <= 2L(A-E).                                      (1)

The two bit units of EVERY original A event in the P_i are excluded from
the auxiliary capacity pool. Their source identity and disjointness are
structural, not a claim that an unspecified auxiliary allocation left them
unused. All remaining actual A units form a pool of size2L(A-E). Inequality
(1) permits an injection of auxiliary B-double demands into that pool.
This is finite integer capacity accounting, not an assertion of eligibility
or acceptance by the frozen matching-terminal protocol.

Direct D and signed retained interference J compare the original B history
with this actual aligned auxiliary B history. Removing erased DELETEs has
no effect on these B charges. The exact identity remains

    LQ = Q_E(I) + D_I + J_I.                                (2)

If an INDEPENDENT source theorem proves D_I+J_I<=2LE for the selected
excursions, (1)-(2) give LQ<=2LA and hence original CD. The inequality in
this last premise is not asserted universally here. It is a useful separate
obligation because the capacity2LE is explicitly identified and excluded
from auxiliary funding, unlike the full unknown slack R_z.

For explicit physical cancellation, pair min(q_F,q_E) double IDs at each
retained occurrence in ordinal order. Let P be the count of unmatched
original demands, including direct erased demands, and N the count of
unmatched auxiliary demands. Then P-N=D_I+J_I exactly. Delete the N
auxiliary assignments to release N distinct units of the auxiliary pool.
If P-N<=2LE, these released units and the disjoint2LE excursion units
have capacity to pay all P original demands. No credit is used twice.
Negative interference is retained as genuinely freed capacity, not discarded.

The argument is uniform in r, ambient size, closure periods, anonymous
fringes, walk length and repetitions. It allows nonrejoining full/erased B
trees and five-key/core9 auxiliary histories. It gives a valid lifting
principle for walks admitting BOTH the independently selected excursions
and an independently proved marginal bound. It does not show every walk
admits them, nor that this class provides genuinely new coverage against
all inherited compositions. Those gates remain OPEN and must not be sealed.

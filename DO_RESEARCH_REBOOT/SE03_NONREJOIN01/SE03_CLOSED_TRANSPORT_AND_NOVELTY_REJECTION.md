# A closed nonrejoining transport identity and its exact novelty limit

This is a universal symbolic regression identity, not new CD coverage.
Let T=y(A,p(B,z(C,w(D,E)))) as in the transport proof and define

    H=(Kz,Kw,Ky,Kp,Ky),
    V=(Kw,Ky,Kp,Ky),
    T'=y(A,p(B,w(z(C,D),E))).

The already derived first four H operations followed by a left ZIG Ky give
S_H(T)=T'. Starting at T', Kz has path R,R,L, so RL at p,w,z followed by
a right ZIG yields z(y(A,p(B,C)),w(D,E)). Kw is a right ZIG. Ky is an LL
double. Kp is an RL double; it yields p(y(A,B),z(C,w(D,E))). The last Ky
is a left ZIG and returns T. Therefore

    S_H(T)=T', S_H(T')=T, S_V(T)=T.                         (1)

Use diagonal (T,T), original period H H, and erased period V V. Both pairs
close exactly without a preperiod: original A follows H H and erased A
follows the same requests H H; erased B follows V V. The full and erased
B trees disagree after all four retained accesses of the first H and the
first two of the second H. They first rejoin at its Kp. Equal retained
roots throughout do not imply equal trees.

Full q profiles per H are (1,0,1,1,0) in both phases; erased q per V is
(1,0,1,0) in both phases. Thus D=2,J=0,Delta=2,Q_full=6,Q_E=4. The positive
and negative indirect terms are -1,+1,0,0 per H and cancel exactly. This
gives a closed example of sustained transport with nonzero direct marginal,
not a pure-boundary cancellation proof of the whole marginal.

## Why obvious six-key padding provides no novelty

Let a<s2<b<s1<y and substitute

    A=s1(b(s2(a(F0,F1),F2),F3),F4).

For arbitrary right subtree R, the word P=(Kb,Ka,Kb,Ky) is an exact
B identity on y(A,R): four same-side grandchild doubles LL,LL,RR,RR.
Insert P after each H. The resulting period (H P)(H P) has six genuine
KEEP keys {a,b,y,p,z,w}, core8, and arbitrary independent gap hangers.
Its auxiliary five-key core is8, not9. The source identities still hold
because P treats the entire right subtree as an opaque hanger.

However, each P is a B-closed three-key component. Removing them leaves
H H, a B-closed four-key component. The completed RT2/K4 B-only theorems
already prove their B-composition, regardless of arbitrary A DELETE
interleaving. Therefore the padding candidate is REJECTED as genuinely
new CD progress. It is not promoted, counted in Bank25, or treated as a
solution to the mandatory six-key/core9-auxiliary obligation.

The corrected next mechanism must couple a nonrejoining discrepancy with
funding beyond these B-closed small-support pieces. The excursion lemma
can reserve actual units using scalar CD, but its source marginal bound
and source-complete excursion existence remain to be proved.

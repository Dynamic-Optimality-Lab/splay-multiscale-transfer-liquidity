# Open source-complete key selection and covering theorem

Status: OPEN. Marked-leaf selection is proved for controlling erased core
size. It is not proved favorable for signed marginal repayment.

The desired universal statement is: for every r>=6 and every original
reachable fully closed r-support walk, either a source-selected erased
key admits independently funded nonduplicating compensation, or the walk
belongs to a proved direct safe class in an exhaustive structural partition.

What is proved: all B states can be represented exactly on the same fixed
full labeled core S, with |S|<=2r-1. Leaf erasure gives c_E<=min(c-1,2r-3).
For six keys/full core<=9 this selects erased core<=8 and legitimizes old
strong B-only auxiliary funding. For core10/11 an erased core9 remains.
Maximal core11 has even-depth genuine KEEPs with outer LL/RR. The exact
negative source-block pairs at six keys are H1/q3,4,5 and H2/q5, subject
to their core-size restrictions and the recorded H2 mode correction.

What is missing: a proved repayment law for every configuration of those
blocks and their signed transfers, including positive helpers and retained
backbone work; an existence theorem linking that law to a selectable key;
and a uniform argument beyond six keys, where H grows with r. The deepest-
access two-bit grammar characterizes local core retention only. The saved
clause implications do not prove source witness padding or domain coverage.

No favorable key, leaf-debt compatibility, complete packet decomposition,
or arbitrary-signature extension to arbitrary KEEP support is assumed.
This is an unresolved theorem obligation, not a successful coverage result.

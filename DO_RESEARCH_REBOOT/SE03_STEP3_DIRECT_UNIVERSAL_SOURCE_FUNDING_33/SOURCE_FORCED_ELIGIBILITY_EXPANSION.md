# Expansion: what is and is not established

Universal source-forced licensed Hall expansion is NOT proved. At the
original uniform counting level every nonempty S subset of residual roots
has N(S)=U\im(a). A minimal deficient set, if one exists, has exactly
|U\im(a)|+1 roots; proving it impossible would prove the missing global
capacity, not a new independent geometric lemma. No such capacity is assumed.

Where an independent COMPLETE source H exists, the inherited finite
H/inverse-a path theorem gives distinct free terminals without displacing
old values. Nonuniform eligibility additionally needs the actual path
closure/transport license. An isolated owned cycle cannot be entered by an
unpaid rooted path: injectivity gives a unique predecessor. This conditional
graph theorem is reused, not promoted to a theorem producing H.

On Gamma55 exact expansion for its actual count roots is witnessed by the
two literal terminal paths in Gamma55_static_owner_extension.json and checked
separately. Its all-event Bank25 schedule is nevertheless impossible. Thus
even complete static expansion cannot establish that stronger universal route.

## Projection and source completeness

node retain its projected right child; at an upper exterior node retain its
projected left child. At a first-gap private node retain its projected right
child when no anchor is on the left; otherwise the only left anchor is the
minimum, and promote that minimum with the projected right subtree. At an
anchor retain both projected children. Mark-free terms become empty anchor
gaps. This single deterministic projection rho retains every anchor once.

For a direct LOW access expose all path vertices (at most four edges).
Their sorted colors are L*, m?, F*, M*, H*: lower exterior, minimum anchor,
first-gap private, higher anchor, upper exterior. There is at most one m.
If m is not exposed but F is, the off-path interval between the largest L
and smallest F contains precisely the minimum anchor in projection. Every
other off-path interval before the last F or after the first H has no
anchors; intervals between F and H contain arbitrary higher-anchor terms.
This is an exhaustive order grammar, independent of private population.
Terms may be empty wherever ordered anchor payloads permit; fixed leaves
for the minimum only encode its unique physical occurrence.

These are symbolic term identities. The enumeration is of all length-at-most-

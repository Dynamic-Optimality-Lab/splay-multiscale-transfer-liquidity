### One-event accesses to an old anchor commute

If z is off the search path, all actual rotations operate on disjoint
off-path terms, so their simultaneous substitution commutes with pi.
If z is on a path of length at most two from an old-anchor root g, the
only possibility is g< x<z and the path g,z,x. Its source is

    g(A,z(x(L,R),E)) -> x(g(A,L),z(R,E)).

Projection gives exactly the old single-zig source
g(A,x(L,R)) -> x(g(A,L),R). Both have ONE StepEv, even though
their numbers of rotations differ. Root repeats are identities. Therefore
every one-event access to any old anchor commutes with pi, with the SAME
StepEv cost. This includes old-anchor DELETEs and nongenuine KEEPs.

### A one-event DELETE z followed by a one-event old-anchor access

At a genuine boundary z can have depth only one or two if its access costs
one event. Since z is a maximum, its ancestors form a right spine.

Depth one source and intermediate are

    g(A,z(R,E)) -> z(g(A,R),E).

A following one-event old-anchor x is g or the root of A or R. For x=g,
B does not genuinely change root. For x equal to a root of A or R,
projecting the final LL/LR double yields exactly S_x(g(A,R)), at old
cost one and actual WHOLE cost two.

Depth two source and intermediate are

    g(A,p(B,z(R,E))) -> z(p(g(A,B),R),E).

The following one-event old-anchor x is p, g, or the root u of R.
x=g is again nongenuine. For p the final projection is
p(g(A,B),R). For R=u(L,M), it is u(p(g(A,B),L),M).
These are precisely the old one-event S_p and S_u results. Again actual
WHOLE H=2; the comparison has H=1. The original DELETE z, its event and
its two physical bits are retained and charged, never erased from the
actual credit inventory. This comparison is an algebraic endpoint bridge,
not an assertion that the actual source uses a different access word.

### Complete LOW event-word partition

event word consists of either one H1 access, one H2 access, or two H1
accesses. The terminal genuine target is an old anchor different from the
old B root, even if its terminal A cost is zero.

In the one-positive-access cases that positive access must target the

genuine packet. In the two-H1 case the second positive access must be the

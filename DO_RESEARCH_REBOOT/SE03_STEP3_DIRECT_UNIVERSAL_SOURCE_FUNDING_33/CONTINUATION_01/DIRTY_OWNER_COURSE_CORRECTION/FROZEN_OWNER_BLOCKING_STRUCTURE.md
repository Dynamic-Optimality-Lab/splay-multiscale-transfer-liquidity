# Actual-ID frozen-owner blocking theorem

Uniform finite structural theorem, inherited algebra reconstructed here.
It does not assert a new universal source of donors. Source eligibility and
the comparison's Bank25 license are premises, never consequences of graph
reachability. Existing accepted audits are not rerun as source searches.

Let D,U be the actual demands/bits of ONE aligned source period. Let a0
be the legitimate frozen injection and h:Q->U an independently valid
injective partial source comparison, Q subset D. Every ID is original.
For each root p in P=D\dom(a0), repeatedly use h(d); if its bit is frozen
owned, continue at its actual inverse-a0 owner. Stop at an a0-free bit,
or at a demand outside Q. The latter is a COMPARISON HOLE, not a free bit.

## Exact structural conclusions

1. A root path cannot enter a cycle. Demands have at most one incoming
   owner edge and bits at most one incoming h edge. Reverse a proposed
   first cycle entry: uniqueness forces the entire preceding path around
   the same cycle, eventually giving the unpaid root an a0 owner edge.
   That contradicts p not in dom(a0).
2. Distinct root paths cannot merge, by the same reverse uniqueness.
   They therefore have distinct free terminals or distinct hole demands.
3. Finiteness forces one of those two outcomes. In particular an h/a0
   owner-only cycle is unreachable from every unpaid root; it supplies
   no credit, but is not itself an unpaid-root obstruction.
4. Append p->its terminal for successful roots ONLY. The resulting map
   preserves every old a0 value and is injective. For a failed root keep
   its exact hole demand and every occupied bit/owner in the path.
5. A fixed h is adequate for all P if and only if NO root path reaches a
   hole. This is an exact criterion for THIS comparison route, not proof
   that every possible licensed route fails when one h is inadequate.
6. If h is total on D, no holes exist, so all roots are funded simultaneously.
   No bound on support, number of owners, source length or path length is
   used. The only free endpoints are actual bits outside im(a0).

These are the statements implemented by C13 extend and C15 augment_partial.
The C10 common-only version starts only at dom(h)\C and labels a blocked
common owner explicitly; it is not silently substituted for arbitrary a0.

## Exact natural-reservation safety

Suppose complete h extends natural h0. If a new root's terminal unit is
u=h0(q), h injectivity forces the path's last demand to be q. Either q is
that unpaid root itself, so its own natural unit pays it; or q is an
interior demand reached via inverse a0, hence q ALREADY has its frozen
successful value. No different unpaid natural demand loses its only
reserved unit. Therefore deleting all of im(h0) from the donor space as
permanently unavailable is an invalid stronger model. Release must use
this original reservation/owner justification, not the fact that u is raw
or absent from one particular auxiliary image.

Other unretired Gamma reservations are not discharged by this lemma.
An E-only reservation is released only by the original N retirement rule
in the alternative full ledger. The archived smaller allocation is fixed.

## The minimal exact comparison obstruction

For h extending h0, every hole is in D\dom(h), hence in natural R. The
distinct hole set B(h,a0) is the exact frontier of the unpaid root family.
A hole can be a frozen PAID full demand whose comparison value is missing;
it must not be counted again as a new unpaid physical demand. A root with
h itself undefined is also a hole and is retained, not discarded.

To eliminate the obstruction, prove a compatible source repair or alternate
licensed comparison that closes the reachable holes, with its OWN legal
construction schedule. Merely finding a cycle, more raw spare bits, or an
individual donor for each root does not prove that. Unrelated owner-only
components need not be financed again by the chosen comparison. This is
the exact distinction needed by the direct arbitrary dirty-frame attack.

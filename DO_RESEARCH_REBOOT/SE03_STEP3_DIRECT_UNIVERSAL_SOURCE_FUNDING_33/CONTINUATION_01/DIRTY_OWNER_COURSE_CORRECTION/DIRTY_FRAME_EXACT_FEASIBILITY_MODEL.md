# Exact feasibility model for the inherited C14 route

This model is source-backed and certificate-based. It is NOT a claim that
all original source funding has been characterized by static pair edges.
The arbitrary-source existence of its required source certificate is OPEN.

## Inspected authority

* C14 UNIFORM_R_TRANSFER.md sections1-2: I(r), licenses A/B and E_r1/2/3.
* C14 CARRY_STORAGE_SEAM.md and ABSOLUTE_PERIOD_ALIGNMENT.md: the working
  bank of independent h, proof-output convention, two separate cuts and

* C10 physical_exchange.py: literal common locks, partial comparison,
  blocked common owners and successful effective pairs.
* C13 source_funding.py own_map/extend/with_owners: h0, natural excess,
  actual a_nat, full h and residual anchor map.

* C26 SOURCE_FORCED_FROZEN_EXPANSION.md: counting relaxation and the extra
  closure required for any genuinely narrower static eligibility relation.

No universal per-demand eligibility matrix is supplied by those files.
In particular a partial comparison's image is NOT the physical eligibility
set, and its missing values are NOT proof that original donors do not exist.

## Fixed data and a feasible witness

Freeze I,W,Gamma,D,U,E,C,N,fE,h0,a0,P as in the theorem Box. Gamma includes
the actual tree/chart and allocation provenance, extra source reservations,
declared source policy, allowed blocks, selected units, cuts and bank state.

A feasible witness FOR THE UNCHANGED C14 ROUTE consists of:

1. E_r1 evidence: actual erasure source/domain, recurrence, I and event IDs.
2. An independently SOURCE-PROVED license L of C14 type A or B, with its
   genuine packet/block rule, physical selections/ordinals and complete h.
   h:D->U is injective and extends original h0. The license is derived from
   rotations/marked-core structure, not from an assumed g, scalar total,
   convenient fE image or desired matching.
3. A construction trace sigma for h, with selected/admitted/assigned/
   retained/released actual units and working occupancy<=25. Its cut,
   reserves and closing state are those of L. No unassigned credit is
   archived as if it were an assigned output, or reread after release.
4. The original common/retirement and exact-period transport license:
   N retires only in the alternative full ledger; archived fE never changes;
   every endpoint is transported by the same actual occurrence bijection.
5. The actual h/inverse-a0 paths. Each new root receives ONLY its final
   a0-free terminal bit, under the accepted counting/block exchange license.
   Any additional Gamma restriction must be checked there; it is not
   removed by the algebraic walk. All old a0 pairs stay literal.

These objects form a source-dependent EXCHANGE/CONSTRUCTION SYSTEM (D in
the directive), with a bank-constrained source construction component.
It is not ordinary chronological endpoint flow: C14 explicitly counts
the independent construction's unused physical buffer, not all outstanding
assigned endpoints of the final noncausal effective map.

## Correspondence in both directions, at its exact scope

Witness -> accepted route: item2 constructs full independent h; item3 gives
its actual Bank25 proof. Apply item5 without moving any old owner; finiteness
and injectivity give distinct free terminals. Item4 supplies eligibility,
retirement and the single legal actual period. The result is the required g
and the source data defining I(r), exactly as C14 proves.

Accepted C14 route -> witness: extract its E_r1 input, the REQUIRED type A/B
local source license and full h from E_r2, and its E_r3 construction trace,
cuts, retirement and original owner paths. These are precisely items1-5.
Thus no legal execution of THAT architecture is lost or added by this
representation. This is not a converse for every conceivable new funding
architecture; a new coupled license would require a separate bridge.

## What is exactly static, and what is not yet reduced

If only the final counting map is requested, every unpaid demand may use
any bit of U\im(a0). Its static feasibility is |U\im(a0)|>=|P|. The fixed
cardinalities cancel a0, giving |U|>=|D|. This is a RELAXATION, not a proof
of its source truth and not a replacement for E_r2/E_r3.

For a FIXED source policy whose valid assignments really factor into a
static relation E_Gamma(d) and an independently proved schedule rule, Hall
is exact for its matching part. Original source proofs must establish both
the factorization and its neighborhood inequalities. No such universal
factorization is presently established. Unions of incompatible comparison
maps, reservation states or source blocks can admit illegal assignments.
Conversely permanently excluding every unowned h0 bit can remove legal
owner-exchange terminals. The blocking-structure proof explains why.

Bank25 is checked on sigma, not inferred from static Hall. If a genuinely
stateful eligibility policy is supplied, retain its full state transitions
in Gamma and sigma; no invented pair relation takes its place. Identifying
all-source source licenses and their eligible joint repairs remains the
principal mathematical obligation.

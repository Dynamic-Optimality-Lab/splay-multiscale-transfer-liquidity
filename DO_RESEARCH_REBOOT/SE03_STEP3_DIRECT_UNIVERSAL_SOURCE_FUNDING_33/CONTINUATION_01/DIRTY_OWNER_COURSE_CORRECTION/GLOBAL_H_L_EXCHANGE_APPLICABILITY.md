# What the original global exchange does and does not require

The accepted C15 ownership interface is explicitly support-free and allows
any finite CERTIFIED actual partial a0. C13's with_owners additionally fixes
the exact canonical natural/l prior and proves equality with the original
full common-set exchange. These are different input interfaces; do not
replace a later a0 by that natural prior or by a raw six-key output.

For a complete independently source-proved h and legitimate same-period a0,
full coverage removes all undefined comparison owners. The unique-incoming
proof gives simultaneous distinct a0-free terminals without moving one
old owner. No small owner profile or special dirty A geometry is used.
This is the exact global tool; it does NOT require proving that each raw
h(d) was initially unowned under fE.

Its source premises remain indispensable: actual unit membership, legitimate
a0 provenance, the original counting/block path license, common ordinal
pairing, E-only retirement in the alternative full ledger, and the same
actual period. A narrower Gamma eligibility policy is not proved transitive
by algebra alone. The full source h and its Bank25 construction are also
premises. The exchange supplies neither of them from a partial h.

The accepted original bank counts the independent h construction. Already
assigned alternative maps and the final finite path records are proof output;
they are not additional unused physical tokens. This convention does not
license hiding new unassigned donors in an archive. It also does not impose
the stronger all-event endpoint-bank condition refuted by Gamma55.

For partial h the exact failure is a reached hole, not a cycle entered by
an unpaid root. Different roots have distinct holes, but additional source
comparisons cannot be arbitrarily unioned: their overlapping bit uses,
natural reservations, block corrections and working trace must compose.
Choosing a different h is permitted only with its actual source certificate;
moving a protected a0 owner is never permitted.

Thus the exchange part is universal at its DECLARED premises. The missing
universal statement is a compatible, schedule-valid SOURCE completion of
the independent comparison. Repeating the exchange theorem or assuming
such h exists would not prove the principal dirty-frame theorem.

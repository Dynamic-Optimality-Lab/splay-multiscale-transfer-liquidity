## The creator condition that the weaker predicate missed

An L branch p created from a unary core by a marked H1 access has more
than a local adjacent fringe. Its left subtree contains ALL anchors below
p: its creator was the whole-tree marked root at that occurrence. Its
projected left root is the adjacent predecessor j, with V-free right side.
Call this SATURATED L. Saturated R is mirrored: all anchors above p are
in its right subtree, whose projected root is the successor with V-free
left side. Projection uses the recursive generalized fringe definition.

Saturation implies the required spine guard whenever the actual root is
marked: that root is outside p's subtree and therefore on the opposite
side of p. H1 marked accesses preserve saturation. An off-path branch is
an unchanged hanger; a surviving on-path branch keeps its full extreme
side; retirement deletes its tag; the two unary birth cases collect the
whole appropriate marked prefix/suffix. Thus the previous H1 induction
also preserves this stronger, source-derived creator predicate.

The weaker local predicate was insufficient. The source forms in
protected_source_activation_analysis.json and its genuine-target version
exhibit an outside lower frame anchor omitted from an L fringe. They
must not be treated as saturated or as protocol counterexamples. Direct
H2 creation of such a frame is the next separate source obligation.

## Preservation under private activation

Assume a marked-root source with EVERY anonymous branch saturated L/R;
anonymous unary chains and ordered subtrees are arbitrary. Perform H1
DELETE p to any anonymous key, then H1 access to an anchor x. A return
to the old marked root is allowed. There exist saturated destination
tags and a comparison DELETE proxy z in V such that

    phi(A_after)=S_x(S_z(phi(A_before))),
    H_old(S_z)+H_old(S_x)<=2.

The comparison preserves the terminal access kind and all B operations.
This is a block cost bound; it does NOT assert per-occurrence physical
event identity if the two old event counts differ from the actual1,1.

Proof coverage. The two actual H1 paths expose at most five physical
vertices. Treat each other subtree as an arbitrary ordered free term.
Project each hanger to E or a nonempty X_i. At an exposed private branch,
the source tag imposes a fringe condition on the root of its projected
child. If that root is hidden in X_i, expose the existing anchor j there,
its prescribed empty child, and its arbitrary other child. This is
exposure of a projected hanger; it does not assume the ACTUAL hanger has
a marked root. Actual paths never enter that hanger, so substitution
commutes with both source frames. At most one such exposure per source
branch is required, hence at most three additional virtual marked roots.
They are existing V keys, normalized by order; no new physical key or
source operation is invented. Split each exposed arbitrary other child
into E/nonempty cases only as required by the source tag constraints.

Saturation is decided exactly by equality of the ordered marked atoms in
the relevant subtree with the whole prefix/suffix. A free term has only
one ordered cell relative to every exposed private key; its population

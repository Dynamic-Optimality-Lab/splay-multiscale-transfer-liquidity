# Nonfringe access commutes with a saturated fringe at every depth

Uniform source theorem, independent verification status recorded separately.
This is not an all-support ordinary debt or physical donor theorem.

Let p be a saturated L anonymous branch: all anchors below p lie in its
left subtree P, and phi(P)=j(A,E), where j is the adjacent anchor predecessor
of p. Use the inherited generalized fringe projection, so phi replaces

    p(P,R) by j(A,phi(R)).

Assume the genuine target x is above p in key order, and every other actual
access-path vertex is an anchor. Other anonymous populations, unary chains
and consistently projected/tagged branches may occur in untouched hangers.
Then arbitrary-depth ordinary splay of x commutes with phi, with exactly
the same A StepEv count. If p survives it retains its saturated L tag;
if its right side loses all anchors, its unary projection is the same j.
Reflection proves saturated R and target x<p.

Proof. If p is off the path, its complete subtree is an unchanged hanger.
Otherwise every other anchor on the path is greater than p: any smaller
anchor belongs to P by saturation, but the search goes right at p.
Replacing the one path vertex p by j preserves every path comparison and
its length. The original j and any private unary chains above it lie
inside the OFF-PATH term P, not at additional actual path positions.

p is the least path key. The only primitive rotations involving it are
left rotation at parent p, or right rotation with pivot p. Both preserve
its complete left child P. Direct substitution shows that applying either
rotation then replacing p(P,.) by j(A,phi(.)) equals applying the same
rotation with j instead of p in the projected tree. Rotations among other
path anchors commute by ordinary free-subtree substitution. Induction on
the actual bottom-up rotation sequence proves endpoint equality. The path
length and the double/last-zig grouping are identical, so StepEv counts
agree, not just their totals up to a constant.

P keeps all anchors below p and the same projected fringe root j. Thus the
surviving branch remains saturated, and unary retirement agrees with the
same formula. No old comparison access or physical bit has been invented.
All original KEEP/DELETE kinds, target x, full B demand and event indices
retain their original meanings. The theorem is uniform in support, depth
and the finite populations of the untouched terms.

This does not cover another active anonymous path vertex, a target in the
fringe prefix, or arbitrary initial unsaturated branches. Those premises
are not consequences of merely having a small event count.

## Single-admission merge theorem

Let K_sigma bound sigma's unassigned seed-bit inventory at every instant.
Process each original A event ONCE. Retain sigma's selected seed identities
and every source-licensed assignment/retirement action. At that admission
also emit the predetermined natural own pairs for the event's own bits;
release irrelevant admitted mates. im h0 and im b are disjoint.
At most TWO other bits are present during this event. No own bit is
carried to the next event. Therefore merged working <=K_sigma+2.
In particular K_sigma<=23 suffices for Bank25, independently of support,
word length, helper population, and the number of hard frontiers.

Proof by induction over the trace: between events the only unassigned
bits are exactly sigma's retained seeds. During admission their union
with the new event's other bits has size at most K_sigma+2. Emit/release
those other bits immediately under the accepted static own convention;
then sigma's state is restored. Selected seeds are never released and
later readmitted; the full seed plan existed before their admission. Both
endpoint identities and all old source-license actions are unchanged.
The closing own buffer is zero and sigma closes at its original legal cut.
QED. This is NOT an online chronological B-demand payment claim.

## Interfaces and limits

For every selected bit, the proof trace must still include literal event
ID, initial status, assignment target, source block, retention interval,
retirement action, and occurrence transport. If a source block forbids the
stated emission time, this merge theorem does not license it. The theorem
uses the inherited own-emission permission, not a new global permission
to emit arbitrary unmatched seed pairs whenever convenient.

The easy working2 theorem by itself provides no hard trace. A static seed
matching, QH, FH, or a nonnegative total does not imply K_sigma<=23. The
Gamma r-4 unchanged-packet carry obstruction is retained. Repacketization
or nonlocal exchange needs its own real working proof. Thus the universal
Bank25 conclusion for hard sources remains OPEN.

# Quantifier audit

Q1 uses only actual finite injections h0/a0 and original event identities.
The number of components, their lengths and the size of R are unrestricted.
Q2/Q3 use finite matching/alternating paths without a numerical support or
owner cutoff. The minimal-obstruction deficiency-one proof applies to every
finite nonempty X. The bank merge is induction over any finite trace.

Consequently these STRUCTURAL implications are uniform over r>=7, arbitrary
private BSTs, anonymous A ancestors, both orientations, changing charts,
repeated/shared recoveries and any finite source period, provided their
explicit source-license premises hold. No source-preserving contraction,
fixed nine/ten anchor syntax or one-helper assumption is used.

They do NOT establish a uniform source policy L, its factorization F,
source-forced FH, a complete type A/B law, or a legal hard seed trace.
That source-existence implication remains unlifted because it has not
been proved on the original arbitrary-support domain in the first place.
No bounded example certificate is used as a substitute for this lift.

The lower bound r-4 on the inherited unchanged Gamma packet carry is
preserved, not replaced by six-key width/storage constants. The new merge
bound is conditional on a real seed trace; it cannot erase that lower bound.

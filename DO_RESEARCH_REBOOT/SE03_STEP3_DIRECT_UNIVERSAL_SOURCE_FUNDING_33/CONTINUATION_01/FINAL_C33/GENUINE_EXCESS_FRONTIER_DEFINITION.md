# Genuine residuals and exact natural-excess ports

Quantifiers throughout: fix ANY finite original reachable, exactly
paired-closed source at r>=7 and a legitimate Gate U14 input, on its ONE
aligned actual interval I. No bound on support, helpers, depth, private
population, source length, or owner count is imposed. A splays on EVERY
original access. Full B splays on EVERY original KEEP (root identities
included); DELETE is A-only, never deletion of a BST key. Erasure skips the
declared B accesses only. Splays are ordinary bottom-up.

Let D be all full double demands `(t,k)`, U all actual A bits `(t,j,b)`,
with `a_t=ceil(d_A/2)`, `q_t=floor(d_B/2)`. Use the original natural
macro partition and original `source_funding.py/own_map` to define h0:
first `min(2H_m,Q_m)` demand ordinals receive the first same number of
actual bit ordinals of macro m. Set

    R = D \ dom h0;          S = U \ im h0.

R consists exactly of `(t_m,k)` with `2H_m<k<=Q_m`. S consists exactly of
unused natural suffix bits of macros with `2H_m>Q_m`. Their sizes are
`sum (Q_m-2H_m)_+` and `sum (2H_m-Q_m)_+`. Equality macros contribute neither.
These are INDEPENDENT-COMPARISON residuals and seeds, not necessarily unpaid
full demands or actually unowned bits.

Let fE be the actual supplied certified smaller map; E its auxiliary
demands, C=D intersect E, N=E\D. Common l=fE|C is fixed. The supplied a0
is the actual original natural/common exchange followed by its accepted
successful extensions, or another explicitly accepted parent input.
Every a0 value remains literal. Define ACTUAL unpaid demand

    P = D \ dom a0;          T = U \ im a0.

Only T can supply new physical terminal owners. A seed in S need NOT be
in T. It may be frozen-owned, or subject to an additional reservation;
those conditions remain in the source context Gamma. Additional unretired
Gamma reservations must have their original release license.

Follow h0 then inverse-a0 from every p in P, and also from each initial
bit s in S (first inverse-a0, then h0, and so on). All are actual-ID paths.
Every component is a path or an owner-only cycle. Distinct starts have
disjoint paths. Stops are exactly a hole r in R or a free bit in T.
Cycles contain no start. Proof: unique incoming/outgoing edges of the two
injective maps; a cycle entry would force an incoming edge at its start.

Write P_T for roots ending in T and B for holes reached from the other
roots. The map alpha:P\P_T->B is bijective. The literal endpoints of P_T
can be appended without moving an old owner, under the original exchange
license. This is normally already done in the canonical natural prior.

For s in S, write rho(s) for its terminal in R or T. Its paths are disjoint
from all P paths, so rho is a bijection

    rho: S -> (R\B) disjoint_union (T\terminal(P_T)).

Define the virtual fixed matching

    k(r)=rho^{-1}(r),        r in R\B.

This is compressed ownership, not a physical reassignment and not a source
comparison receipt. A value k(r) must NEVER be called an unowned bit.
The exact genuine frontier is the root family P\P_T, with its alpha image B,
all k/rho paths, Gamma eligibility and source/bank obligations. A nominal
negative price, an original-versus-erased discrepancy, or an old signed
comparison failure does not by itself define this frontier.

# Exact easy/hard composition, including cross-owner interactions

Scope and data are those of GENUINE_EXCESS_FRONTIER_DEFINITION.md. The
inherited all-easy theorem is used, not reproved. In a mixed source its
per-macro own assignments are the original h0; its WHOLE-SOURCE completion
corollary is not applied to the mixed source.

**EASY:** every natural own assignment h0(d), including all `2H` local
assignments in a hard macro. Every actual operation and event remains.

**HARD:** independent-comparison slots R, all in macros Q>2H. Physical
unpaid roots P and their reached holes B are distinct objects. A hole in
R\B may already be paid and still be needed as an intermediate comparison
port. Such a hole is not a second outstanding B demand.

**SUPPLY:** S, all natural suffix spare bits of nondeficit macros.

**INTERACTION:** the exact rho/k quotient of frozen owners, the common/N
retirement receipt, extra Gamma reservations, signed/source block licenses,
and one merged working trace. They are retained, not separated away.

## Complete comparison composition theorem

Suppose an independent, original-source-proved rule supplies an injection
b:R->S, with a compatible type A/B construction, admissible event/block
identities, legal retirement/cuts and Bank25 trace after merging natural
owners. Then h=h0 union b is a complete independent comparison: domains
partition D, images are disjoint, each actual demand appears once. The
licensed full h/a0 exchange gives one simultaneous extension of a0; unique
incoming edges give distinct actual T terminals. Nothing is assigned twice.
This supplies E_r2 and the stated E_r3 interfaces, given actual E_r1.

Conversely every complete h extending h0 uniquely restricts to b:R->S.
Thus this is an EXACT algebraic decomposition of the unchanged comparison
architecture. It does not assert that an arbitrary injection b has a source
license, or that one exists universally.

## Weaker coupled physical composition

An adequate partial b can close every reached root without filling all of
R. The exact static-policy criterion is in JOINT_FRONTIER_FEASIBILITY_MODEL.md.
Its complete physical result may preserve all a0 while still lacking a full
independent h. No promotion to E_r2 follows from that weaker conclusion.

# Arbitrary A geometry over an already closed full-B trajectory

## Frozen IAGS Box

Target: realize a prescribed synchronized A shadow and its ORIGINAL local
packet inside a reachable exactly paired-closed source, with arbitrary
finite anonymous A ancestors and the original physical key set intact.

Conditions: finite ordered key set K with N>=4; a specified closed full-B
KEEP cycle on K, with exactly the genuine marked support M, containing a
genuine access g immediately before the specified B packet segment. Write
its cyclic order as K_g, KEEP(P), V, where P is the COMPLETE original local
KEEP/DELETE packet, including all nongenuine/root-repeat KEEPs. Let Bprev
be the B state just before K_g, Bs its state just afterward, and Bt its
state after P. Thus S_g(Bprev)=Bs, root(Bprev)!=g, root(Bs)=g, and V sends
Bt back to Bprev. The chosen full A tree As has the same physical keys and
root g. The B closed-cycle hypothesis is mandatory; no arbitrary B mask or
abstract B edge is asserted recurrent or source-valid.

Inheritance: ordinary bottom-up splay, original A-only DELETE semantics,
actual finite-state recurrence/cut transport. Published transformation
dependency: Levy--Tarjan, arXiv:1907.06310v3, Theorem3.4 and its proof on
pp7--8. For any two BSTs on the same set of at least four keys, ordinary
bottom-up access sequences can transform one into the other. Their proof
uses restricted rotations and four-node rooted hulls with rigid hanging
terms. Only existence and the exact endpoint transformation are imported.
Their path-node cost convention and numerical cost bound are NOT imported
as original StepEv credits. Primary source:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.06310v3 . The proof and ordinary-splay definition
were inspected. The downloaded version and hash are pinned separately.

Ambiguities excluded from the conclusion: no guarantee for an unproved B
closed-cycle premise; no specified smaller-support fE allocation; no donor
ownership claim; no minimum preparation cost; no assertion that an existing
history must use the constructed preparation or reset.

Bridge: apply the published transformation ONLY in A, while B remains on
its given closed cycle, then use exact recurrence for the erased execution.

## Uniform theorem

Under these conditions, there is a reachable original source history with
genuine KEEP support exactly M and a finite exactly closed source period
containing P with exactly its prescribed A/B endpoints, original operations,
and local H,Q. The full and erased B trees and their actual finite recent-
key charts close at the same legal source period. The result is uniform
in N, |M|, the finite local word P and all anonymous/private A populations.
It is a SOURCE REALIZATION theorem, not a funding or repayment theorem.

## Proof

Denote by Gamma(T,U) a finite ordinary-splay access word transforming T to
U, supplied by the cited theorem. Mark EVERY operation in Gamma as DELETE
when used below, so that it is processed in A and omitted from both B
executions. It never physically removes any node. Starting from the common
diagonal A=B=BE=Bprev, execute the following actual word W:

    DELETE(Gamma(Bprev,As)); KEEP(g); P; KEEP(V);
    DELETE(Gamma(Av,Bprev)),

where Ap is A's actual state after running P from As, and Av is its actual
state after then running the full KEEP segment V. These are finite BSTs
on exactly K, so the second transformation is applicable.

The first transformation puts A at As and leaves full B at Bprev. Since
As has root g, KEEP(g) has zero A StepEv events, and its FULL-B action is
the given genuine access returning Bs. This access ends the preparation's
natural macro. Consequently the immediately following P begins at exactly
(As,Bs); neither preparation nor reset enters its H count. Execute EVERY
operation of P unchanged. Its actual source trajectory, endpoints and H,Q
therefore remain the prescribed ones. Full B follows its given segment to
Bt, and KEEP(V) returns it to Bprev. A reaches Av by its actual accesses,
not by an inferred quotient transformation. The final A-only Gamma returns
A to Bprev and leaves B there. Thus full A/B close exactly after W.

The only extra operations are actual DELETEs outside P. They create no B
demand or new genuine KEEP key. All existing KEEP occurrences, including
the zero-B root repeats inside P, remain original operations. Full B has
the genuine support of the GIVEN closed B cycle, namely M. Every extra
A StepEv exists physically and is charged by the original ceil(edge-depth/2)
definition. No numeric credit is taken from the external cost convention.

Now repeat this same ORIGINAL word W from the common initial diagonal.
At every copy boundary A and full B again equal Bprev. The erased B is a
deterministic BST execution on the same finite key set. Include its actual
finite recent-key chart, and the full chart if necessary, in its boundary
state. Finiteness gives an actual preperiod u and period L>0. Choose u
large enough to warm the genuine recent-key records. The source interval
of copies u,...,u+L-1 is exactly closed in A, full B, erased B and charts.
Each occurrence has its own actual absolute time and physical event IDs.
P occurs inside that interval with its unchanged local trajectory. No
donor is imported from the reaching prefix or another symbolic copy.

This argument is uniform for all finite K with N>=4. In particular arbitrary
anonymous A ancestors are permitted by Gamma's domain; they need not be
off-path or belong to a fixed ten-anchor backbone. No finite tree search
stands in for this quantifier. QED using the stated published dependency.

## Exact limitation for C33

This corollary makes arbitrary A shadows admissible over an ALREADY SOURCE-
CLOSED B skeleton. It cannot make a transient B transition recurrent, or
infer that a candidate B shape satisfies marked-core closure conditions.
It is useful for original-source refutations and admissibility audits.

It is not a theorem that every existing source performed Gamma, and it
does not force its preparation credits to exist in a source under study.
Using the constructed extra events as universal donors would be invalid.
It neither supplies E_r2 nor discharges any unprovided E_r3 ownership or
block-eligibility license. CD6 and all frozen owner maps remain unchanged;
unrestricted Gate U, C33, Universal CD and Dynamic Optimality stay OPEN.

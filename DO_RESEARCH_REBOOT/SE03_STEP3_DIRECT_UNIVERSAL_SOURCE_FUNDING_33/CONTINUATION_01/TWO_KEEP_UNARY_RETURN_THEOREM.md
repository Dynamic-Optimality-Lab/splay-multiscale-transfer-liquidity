# Uniform two-H1 genuine-packet unary return

This is a SOURCE GEOMETRY theorem for arbitrary finite support. It does
not depend on a numerical D10 coefficient or a bounded population test.
Independent verification status is recorded separately.

## Exact statement

Fix any finite ordered designated anchor set V with at least two keys.
Consider two consecutive actual natural genuine KEEP packets, each having
whole actual A cost H=1. Let g be the synchronized starting anchor, y the
first genuine target and x the second. Thus y!=g and x!=y; x=g is allowed.
Keep every original DELETE, nongenuine KEEP and zero-event operation.
At the start and the final boundary assume every anonymous A ancestor of
V has at most one V-containing child. Allow arbitrary anonymous branching
at the shared boundary, arbitrary finite private populations and depths,
and every ordered private subtree and reflected orientation.

Let rho prune mark-free terms and contract anonymous unary ancestors.
If A0 and A2 are the actual starting/final A trees, then

    rho(A2) = S_x(S_y(rho(A0))).

Both ordinary anchor splays on the right have EXACTLY one StepEv. The
comparison uses the actual ordered targets y,x and their actual KEEP order.
It does not choose a chart representative after the fact.

## Complete symbolic proof

A whole-H1 genuine packet has only one positive A access. Its target is
the terminal anchor; it may be a DELETE followed by a zero-event KEEP of
that same key. A private positive target followed only by zeros cannot
end at a different anchor. Therefore the two packets' positive actions
are precisely S_y then S_x. Other original operations have zero A cost;
their root-repeat states and kinds remain explicit in the original word.

Each positive path has at most two edges. Exposing their union uses at
most five physical vertices. This union is initially ancestor-closed:
the first rooted path is; the second only exposes additional roots inside
previously rigid hangers after the first rotations. All remaining subtrees
are arbitrary ordered free terms. Inactive anonymous unary chains inside
those terms commute with both event frames; no physical key is deleted.

List every ordered rooted skeleton on at most five exposed vertices.
Require the initial root and both actual targets marked, and every other
vertex marked or anonymous. A projected hanger is either empty E or an
arbitrary nonempty ordered anchor subtree X_i. Impose the unary condition
on each exposed anonymous vertex at the two ENDPOINTS only. The actual
middle tree is unrestricted. Retain only skeletons whose exposed vertices
are exactly the two actual path unions. This is exhaustive by the path
bound and sorted-key order for EVERY source instance in the theorem.

There are 36 source word/skeleton templates and 392 colored free-term
schemas. Substitution of the four ordinary bottom-up double identities
and two zigs in every schema gives exactly S_x(S_y(rho(A0))) after endpoint
contraction. Each old access has at most one event; its target differs
from the previous anchor root, so each has exactly one event. The formal
source equations are stored in two_KEEP_H1_unary_return_source_forms.json.
No numerical debt table, specific private population, or sample source
history is used. The distinct zipper audit regenerates all skeletons via
all insertion permutations and checks every free-term equation separately.
Thus this is a complete symbolic source proof at arbitrary support.

## Signed and physical interface

Any already established POINTWISE ordinary-anchor signed certificate can
be applied along the actual old comparison y,x and its real B executions.
Summing its two inequalities gives the same total original B demand and
the debt difference between the two actual unary endpoints. The shared
comparison state is generated by the first ordinary splay; its debt is
never inferred by contracting the branching actual middle state.

The actual four A-event bits remain the two original event pairs, at
their original positive-access occurrences (t_y,1,b),(t_x,1,b). The old
comparison also has one event at each occurrence. This is an ordinal
correspondence, not four additional physical credits. No previously frozen
owner is reassigned. No B access is erased, and no donor is borrowed from
another cyclic copy. The statement itself requires no cut or Bank increase.
Zero-event operations may be reinstated with their exact original kinds
and occurrences because both comparison roots agree after each positive
anchor access. Full and erased B executions, if present, retain their
original KEEP subsequences and costs; additional inherited hypotheses of
any particular comparison certificate must still be checked.

This lemma closes one uniform two-packet branch-return interface. It does
NOT handle arbitrary longer branch episodes, H2/HIGH return pairs, erasing
a genuine KEEP from B, a whole-period frozen-owner matching, an all-support
source funding license, or Gate U. Bank25 and R1--R4 are unchanged.

# U33-BURST — a universal necessary endpoint-space bound

Status: symbolic theorem; exact Gamma55 replay and independent audit to follow.
Scope: ANY original finite source interval I, with arbitrary private trees,
support and a complete injection f:D_F(I)->C_A(I). No owner reassignment,
eligibility weakening or counting inequality is assumed. Additional frozen
owners and restrictions can only reduce the allowed injections.

Use the endpoint storage convention of C28 W199_EXACT_ELIGIBILITY_AND_BANK25
and C30 NORMALIZED_SHALLOW_OWNER_INTERFACE: at each actual source cut count
every payment whose earlier endpoint has occurred and later endpoint has
not, retaining the actual bit if it was earlier or the actual demand if it
was earlier. A endpoints precede B endpoints at one original KEEP. This is
a STRONGER ALL-EVENT-CUT profile than GateU14's independent block-construction
working queue, not a new necessary axiom for CD or the accepted transfer.

Theorem. For any consecutive original event interval J containing q selected
full demand endpoints and u original A-bit endpoints, EVERY such injection
has endpoint bank at least ceil((q-u)_+/2) at one of the cuts just before
or just after J. More precisely B_before+B_after>=q-u.

Proof. At most u of these q demands can use donors inside J by injectivity.
Partition the remaining donors into those before J (p) and after J (f).
Then p+f>=q-u. Every past donor is a distinct open bit at the first cut;
every future donor gives a distinct open demand at the second cut. Hence
B_before>=p, B_after>=f and their maximum is at least ceil((q-u)/2).
Other payments can only add open records. This proves the claim for ALL
matchings, including future/nonlocal donors, not only a selected algorithm.

For one KEEP of actual A cost a and full B cost q, take its entire A/B replay:
u=2a. A common-demand subfamily of size q_C gives the same lower bound
ceil((q_C-2a)_+/2) for every injection preserving those common demands.

The source-uniform inherited Gamma_r has a genuine occurrence with a=1,
q_F=r-1. After erasing3 its recurrent q_E=r-2, so q_C=r-2 there. Therefore

    every FULL matching: max endpoint bank >= ceil((r-3)/2),
    every COMMON owner injection: max endpoint bank >= ceil((r-4)/2).

For r=55 both lower bounds are26. Thus a uniform25 ALL-ENDPOINT schedule
is impossible even if all donors are eligible, no earlier successes are
frozen, and arbitrary source grouping/noncausal payments are permitted.
No uniform constant endpoint profile (in particular9 or25) can hold at
arbitrary support. This is stronger than the old r-4 causal carry obstruction:
it is independent of packetization, comparison formula and matching choice.

Any legal cyclic cut leaves the original occurrence intact, so the same
before/after argument applies in its one actual selected period. Neither
reflection nor opaque gap substitution changes the counts. No outside
copy can be used as extra donor supply. The theorem also applies to unions
of event intervals if their actual boundary inventories are counted once.

This does NOT refute GateU14's different working-buffer contract. A source
block may fund an earlier demand by later source units and output a static
exchange without maintaining this stronger all-cut endpoint queue. Whether
such a SOURCE-DERIVED all-r license exists is a separate principal U33 Box.

# Stronger source-shaped negative-target separator

Leaf count alone is insufficient for this new language:720 represented
frames have four BF leaves. The main theorem does not rely on excluding
them or on calling an arbitrary four-leaf frame an actual NEG2 target.

For every GENUINE cold NEG2-S target, the old q4 target is a marked leaf
by actual recency. Its four proper marked ancestors become children of
the new root. The one remaining mark was not below that old cold leaf;
it stays under one old ancestor. Thus its target has

                  (marked leaves,marked-root degree)=(4,4).

For every GENUINE NEG2-P target, the actual q5 macro visits the whole
six-mark chain and creates a five-child marked fan. Its actual q1 successor
accesses one of those old marked leaves. The target has the previous root
as its sole marked child and four marked leaves below it. Thus its shape is

                              (4,1).

These are exact marked-parent source identities with all original A/BE,
support, erasure, event timing, chart and period constraints retained. They
do not identify a q5/q1 packet with a synthetic equal-cost transition.
The accepted06 cold-target theorem supplies L4; its same source compression
also gives the stated degrees. The independent Campaign08 source audit
checks all128 cold singleton contexts and62 actual paired contexts against
these identities, with arbitrary gaps and no extreme-successor restriction.

Every four-leaf record of L instead has degree2. Every other record has
fewer than four leaves. The SOURCE-DERIVED predicate

              I(B)=[L(B)=4 and marked_root_degree(B) in {1,4}]

is therefore true on all actual starting NEG2 targets and false throughout
the complete new backward language. This proves

                      T11^-2 intersect gamma(L) = empty.

The conclusion is stronger than testing negative A preimages: even the
larger BF shape classes (4,4)/(4,1) are excluded irrespective of their
actual A/BE completion or recent state. All actual NEG2-S/P targets are
subsets of those excluded classes, so their complete original provenance
is preserved without inventing new restrictions.

The four-leaf degree2 diagrams can occur in legitimate other source
histories; they are not globally banned. The joint A/BF/recent predecessor
calculus is what closes this PARTICULAR language without admitting a fan
negative target. BF degree alone is not asserted to forbid every forward
zero from a negative target. No abstract intersection is promoted to a
source counterexample, and no width-two debt or donor injection is defined.

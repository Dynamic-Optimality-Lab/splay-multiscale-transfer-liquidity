# Universal final neutral-zero inverse

Let s be an actual cold q4 source. Its cold marked key x is a marked leaf
by the actual recency heap; BF-depth(x)=8 in the closed core11 domain.
Its marked path contains five marks, leaving one off-path mark. Therefore
BF has exactly two marked leaves, and marked root degree1 or2.

All original zero types are H1/Q2, H2/Q4 and paired H3/Q6. A q4 output has
at least four marked children/leaves. A q5/q1 pair output has four leaves.
Neither can enter s. Every genuine final zero is consequently H1/Q2.
It must enter a Fork41 shape (one marked leaf branch, one four-mark chain),
because q2 creates two marked-root children and cannot enter a degree1 Stem.

Write the physical BF path before that zero as

                        v,p,h,q,y,

with v,h,y marked and p,q unmarked. Each of its two actual doubles is SS;
both choices per double remain, including mixed directions between pairs.
The actual target y becomes root and v,h become its marked children.
Since the destination degree is exactly2, y had no old marked child.
One new child is a marked leaf and the other owns the remaining three-mark
off-path chain, ending in x. Thus precisely one old ancestor had no
off-path marked branch. The old source and new destination both have two
marked leaves: this is the exact2->2 neutral zero, not a general zero edge.

The two and only marked-parent source forms are:

* J2: root v with a two-mark chain h->y and a three-mark chain ending x.
* K2: root v with sole marked child h; h has leaf y and a three-mark chain.

In J2 the outer ancestor owns the extra chain; in K2 the inner ancestor
owns it. These follow by reversing the actual marked-parent SS identities.
They are nearest-marked-parent trees, not assertions about physical binary
root children or opposite sides. All physical separator placements and
arbitrary ordered gap subtrees remain free.

In particular the source u of the final zero has marked-root degree<=2.
The cold x is outside the v,h,y path and remains the chain leaf. This
classification is necessary, not a realizability assertion for every diagram.

The A macro has exactly one positive access, to y at depth1/2. It occurs
at terminal KEEP y or at an earlier DELETE y. Before it all A-root identity
accesses target v; afterwards only DELETE y can precede terminal KEEP y.
The exact macro language is the inherited H1 normal form, with Q2 rather
than Q1. All six A zig/double cases and private parents are included.
Shallow cold exposure after the zero is exactly the one-event A word v,y,x.

Recent order changes only at the terminal KEEP y, moving y to the front;
DELETE y does not refresh it. The actual BE retained subsequence is kept,
with no qE/qF identification. The actual BF/recent order determines the
recent-five chart; erasure and chart identity are different notions.

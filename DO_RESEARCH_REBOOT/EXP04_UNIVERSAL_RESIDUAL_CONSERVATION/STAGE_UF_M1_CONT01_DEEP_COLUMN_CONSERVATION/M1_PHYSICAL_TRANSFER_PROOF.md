# Physical transfer: no completed X

A full M1 transfer theorem is not established. This file records the strongest structural relation and the precise missing bridge.

## Birth graph is physical, but is not a priced transfer

Every U consumer has a canonical actual Gamma and creator double r, with strict whole-frame descent. For a C consumer the G/O dichotomy holds. An O birth has p(r)=g(v) and an LR/RL creator: the physical old-parent component is demoted, rather than non-target-promoted. Therefore it is not automatically an M2 P obligation. G chains need not meet any B non-target promotion internally.

There is a canonical finite ancestry of birth outputs ending at a creator outside U. The 72A law pays the immediate U successors of that layer. It does not prove a bound on the number or weight of deeper successors. A graph path from a bounded layer is not a payment bridge.

## Candidate transfer box

Target: assign the remaining silent deep C gaps to physical non-target restoration batches with bounded original A load.

Conditions needed: an independently defined restoration event set; lifetimes at actual B primitive boundaries; a map from each original remaining rho to such an event; global absolute congestion including shared chains; an independent downstream event budget.

Inheritance: M0 P-predecessors represent actual non-target B promotions, while C intervals have none. Exact B ancestry can be destroyed and recreated during one hit without intervening A work. Original EXP-01 W examples reject the unit/load-two root-entry incidences.

Ambiguity: no proved implication forces a silent G history to reach a chargeable promotion/restoration event with bounded total weight. Its frame can shrink many times while A's g component is fixed. Paired closure says the trees return; it supplies no bound on the cost or sharing of a chosen inverse obligation.

Bridge status: OPEN. No independent quantity meeting all these conditions was constructed. S_UU and all deep C mass are explicitly rejected as definitions of X.

## Preserved M2/M3 interface

M2 may work on the unchanged literal R_P. M3 may investigate a genuinely defined mixed-history conservation law. Neither receives an authorized new restoration debt from this continuation. Any future use of the birth graph must prove its own bridge and account for U-to-U chains, opposite outputs, cyclic lifetimes, and duplicate physical resources.

All earlier restricted exact-return, local and periodic comparison theorems remain valid with their own eligibility conditions. They are not converted into a universal transfer. No B work is relabelled A work and no virtual A bit is introduced.

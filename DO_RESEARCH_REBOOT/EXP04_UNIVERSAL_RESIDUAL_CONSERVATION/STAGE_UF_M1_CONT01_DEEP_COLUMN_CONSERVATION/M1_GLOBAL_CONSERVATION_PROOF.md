# Arbitrary-depth scoped payment; full conservation OPEN

The following is a source-uniform theorem for a defined subset of literal original deep gaps. It leaves a named unpriced complement. It is not full M1.

## B0: frame creators outside U

For rho in the original prescribed R_C,>2, let v be its original consuming double, Gamma_v its whole-frame birth bit, and r(v) the original containing creator double. Define B0={rho:r(v) not in U}.

The birth lemmas prove that each creator has at most two U frame consumers, across all columns, branches and seam lifts. Each consumer carries at most three original residual gaps. Therefore
\[
|B0|\le6|V\setminus U|
 \le6(4R_A+4K_B)\le72A.
\]
All quantities are defined on the original event word. This is an aggregate load bound, not an injective root-entry rule.

## B1: an acquired sibling of an opposite output

After B0, define B1 to contain rho for which r(v) is in U, v is the O alternative, and J_{r(v)} is A-acquired from its actual birth through its original first inside hit (including the post-A boundary).

For any r in U, J_r is A-absent at birth: its old-grandparent bit is neutral and its lost child label contains the current A-root target. If J_r is later A-acquired, select its first real A SET creation in this interval. Different J labels cannot share a creation bit. Runs for the same J are disjoint, because a new B SET creation requires an intervening B loss, and that loss is at its first inside KEEP. Thus these r inject into actual A creations and number at most R_A.

An acquired J_r cannot have a G U successor: that successor's whole-frame Pi would require J_r to be A-blind throughout this very run. The only B1 successor is the unique opposite old-parent output consumer. It carries at most three gaps. Hence
\[
|B1|\le3R_A\le6A.
\]
This is a separate additional A charge, even if its A event was also used by an inherited or B0 mechanism.

## Exact accounting identity and complement

B0 and B1 are disjoint, by their creator predicates. Let
\[
S_{UU}=\mathcal R_{C,>2}\setminus(B0\cup B1).
\]
Every original gap occurs once, and
\[
\boxed{R_{C,>2}\le78A+|S_{UU}|.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
R_C\le114A+|S_{UU}|.
\]
There is no radius restriction on B0 or B1; ell>2 only keeps this addition disjoint from the inherited near assignment.

For every S_UU consumer, its creator is U and either a G chosen-output birth with that output unacquired, or an O parent-output birth with both outputs unacquired. This is an exact original selector description. It is not an independent payment theorem.

## Composition across the full source

The non-U bound counts all original doubles, not selected residual vertices, so common M0 ancestors do not add capacity. Creator output runs are source-scoped and have unique first hits; repeating a component creates a fresh run and must satisfy the same physical accounting again. A creation at the cyclic seam is counted once by its actual modulo-period primitive ID. Signed A flux is telescoped on the full closed states. Full A component changes, B ancestry recreations, private carriers and cross-column use are retained.

The proof never charges a B promotion as A work. The O alternative is included explicitly and is not silently assigned to the preceding same-g J output. M0 overlapping siblings remain allowed; only the two completed outputs of one opposite birth creator are disjoint.

\[
|S_{UU}|\le c_{\rm silent}A

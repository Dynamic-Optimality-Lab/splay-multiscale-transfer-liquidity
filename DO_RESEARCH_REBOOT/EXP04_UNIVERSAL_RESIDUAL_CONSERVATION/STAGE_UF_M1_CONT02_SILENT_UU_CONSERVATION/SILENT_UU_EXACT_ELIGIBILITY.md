## Necessary quantitative and chronological consequences

A B double is a genuine KEEP, so a_h>=1 by the original cyclic A-root-change theorem. A surviving parent has
\[
j>2a_h,\quad b_h(D)\ge2a_h+1\ge3,\quad q_h>2a_h.
\]
The first follows because at most j-1 original parents precede it. The last follows because b_h(D)<=q_h; a cheap packet cannot overflow. Thus the original index and the distinct-parent quota rank are different necessary conditions.

A positive prescribed survivor also forces **absence of every applicable positive full-period periodic certificate**, not merely failure of raw at one computational cut: otherwise H_all pays the noncheap occurrence just proved. It must evade every eligible local comparison as well. This does not imply that no eligible comparison can be found; that implication is precisely part of universal elimination.

For E_old exclusion there are two alternatives. Either g has no actual A demotion anywhere in the period, or its current epoch contains an earlier original C_old parent. An arbitrary later same-g consumer cannot claim a fresh epoch donor. The old577 shared-epoch source and EXP-01 Hall obstructions forbid that shortcut.

A=0 gives no genuine B KEEP/double and no gap, as established originally. No ratio is used at zero.

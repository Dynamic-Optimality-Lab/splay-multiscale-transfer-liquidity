# Late-chain target and stopping gate

The sealed577 control already has an isolated original R_* silent U-to-U deep C consumer with j>2a_h. Therefore 'all silent consumers are prefix-early' is already false and is not a new research result. The new padding mechanism targets the stronger still-useful sufficient premise H-LATE:

> There is an absolute bound on the number of consecutive C renewals that are simultaneously original U/R6, silent U-to-U, physically deep, and beyond the original index prefix j>2a_h.

A bounded-height lemma of this form would support a root/chain charging route. Its negation requires a parameterized complete source, not another isolated late consumer. Odd padding gives a candidate with chain length k-1, physical distances3i, j=L+1 and a_h=1, allowing both k and odd L to grow. The older unbounded qualified chain was prefix-early and did not refute H-LATE. Even padding failed for the separate exact receiving-cohort reason preserved in PADDING_PARITY_FAILURE.md.

H-LATE concerns a necessary geometric superset of S_UU. It does not include all frozen hybrid/quota exclusions. Refuting it does not refute conservation for S_UU. A large original index is necessary for overflow but is not sufficient: the actual Q(D) counts distinct D parents, not skipped original doubles. Audit those ranks explicitly on the small native R_* instances before claiming anything about survival. A valid old periodic certificate stays active.

If the odd-padding theorem and the native selector audit establish H-LATE's failure, close that physical obstruction branch. Do not silently claim an S_UU survivor, a new paid class, or a full M1 theorem.

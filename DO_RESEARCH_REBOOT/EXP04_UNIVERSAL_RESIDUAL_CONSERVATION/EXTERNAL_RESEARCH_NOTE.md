# Primary literature checked; no new dependency promoted

The July2026 preprint by Chmel,Haeupler,Hladik,Koucky,Roeyskoe,Rozhon,
Sladky and Tarjan states a splay competitive bound with factor
O(log log n * (log log log n)^2), plus initialization, in Theorem1.
Its Sections5.5–5.6 control internal pairings using a parameter that grows
with population. This factor is not an absolute K for the present target.
Its internal-pairing classes are not identified with original SET-neutral
R6 parents; such a bridge would require another proof. No such bridge or
new theorem dependency is assumed in Continuation02.

Primary source:[version1,Theorem1 and Sections5.5–5.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18498v1).
This is a checked theorem statement and research lead, not an independent
audit of that paper's complete proof or a substitute for the requested
constant-factor residual conservation theorem.

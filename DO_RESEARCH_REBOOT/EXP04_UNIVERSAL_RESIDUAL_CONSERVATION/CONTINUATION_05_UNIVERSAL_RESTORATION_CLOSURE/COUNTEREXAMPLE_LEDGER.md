# Exact obstructions and preserved controls

**Principal outcome C.** The new closed, genuinely large-support
opposite-side family refutes the orientation-blind restoration

## C05-O: local span capacity and orientation-blind gain count

Candidate O1: k repeated old grandparents of any earlier
orientation require k−1 intervening B ancestry gains. False:
the complete alternating-path family in
OPPOSITE_SIDE_CONSERVATION has \(\lfloor k/2\rfloor\) reused
old grandparents and zero gains in the gap.

Candidate O2 (the local forest funding test): all predecessor
edges may be paid by any actual A StepEv within their own
causal spans, with a uniform finite aggregate load, even
allowing fractional allocation. False: the reused edges
above all have just the one StepEv A(2,1). Therefore the
required capacity grows at least as \(\lfloor k/2\rfloor\).
This refutes all constants for this local donor graph, not
a payment allowed to use original work outside these spans.

At k=257 the complete n=516 source has A=15,061, Q_B=2,771,
and 128 such edges. It has genuine S=M=all 516 keys. Exact
raw periodic cost 19,121 ≤ 150,610 pays all its noncheap
original residuals, with cheap parents fitting the quota.
Original Pi was not run for this large instance, so its
R_* count is unknown. The independently Pi-checked k=4 and
k=32 instances validate the smaller exact realizations.
The asymptotic local obstruction is the explicit physical
argument, not extrapolation from these three instances.

## C05-M: raw marked persistent gain mass is not O(A)

Candidate M: total persistent marked descendant gains of
non-target parent promotions at fully neutral original U
doubles are uniformly \(O(A)\). False even with all added
keys genuinely visited in the measured period.

Use the complete 65-key original G_4_1_L31_r1_z1 period
(A=323, 290 operations). Its original LL double (132,2)
has old grandparent 18, old parent 10, signs (0,0), and
whole frame [3,33]. Insert m physical keys in the open
inorder gap (18,19), using an actual attached gap tree,
and relabel all old keys in order. Repeat the unchanged
active word m+1 times. Every copy returns the complete
labelled pair to the same common diagonal, including the
rigid gap tree. Use a measured all-KEEP support visitation
loop, with an exact full-tree return, to visit every key.

At the first bit of the specified LL step, 10 gains 18
and the old right component of 18. The m added keys are
in that component. They remain descendants of 10 after
the second bit and the entire access. C02 gap-extension
invariance preserves the original full SET comparisons:
active subtree labels expand injectively, and labels
entirely inside the fixed gap are common static labels.
It also preserves the whole-frame Pi check for copies
after the first: their defining preceding history lies
in the unchanged periodic active body. Hence at least m
interior copies each give m persistent genuine marked
gains at the indicated fully neutral U double.

All repairs are measured. Sequential access, one wrap
KEEP and exact coercion give \(O(65+m)\) A for the
support loop, so
\[
F_{\rm raw}\ge m^2,\qquad
A=323(m+1)+O(65+m).
\]
No absolute ratio bound for this mass exists. This mass
does NOT filter by future overflow uses; a sharper
occurrence count with proved usefulness is not refuted.
The old guarded periodic comparison can cut before the
diagonal support loop and erase it, leaving a comparison
on the 65 active keys (C02 G1). Thus these are not genuine
post-hybrid escapes. The m=7, n=72 instance checks the

## C05-F: two shortcuts for a forest bound

* Uniform pointwise height is false for the all-double
  forest. For any k≥2 let n=2k+1, \(g=1,p_i=2i,x_i=2i+1\).
  Put B at g with right child p_k; p_i has right child x_i
  and left child p_(i-1), if any. Access x_k,…,x_1.
  Each first double has old grandparent g. Inductively
  its completed right subtree is the remaining p-chain;
  subsequent higher doubles leave that component intact.
  Between hits g has no B promotion. A has the x_i as its
  left spine, with gap keys hanging off it, so it never
  demotes g on this segment. These k vertices form a
  strict no-promotion chain of length k−1. Coercion and
  actual support visits complete the source. Saved k=16
  has exact forest height 15. Each fixed column can still
  be paid globally by its real KEEP gaps.
* Sibling frames need not be disjoint. In the 72-key
  complete mass source, forest parent (140,2) has children
  (142,1) and (148,2) with overlapping original S frames
  on keys 19 through 72. One predecessor can be used in
  its same-column role and its old-parent promotion role.
  This blocks a disjoint-sibling mass telescope.

## C05-S: exposure is not automatic for a neutral restorer

The complete 24-key source in SAME_SIDE_CONSERVATION has
two selected column-10 parents, a real no-reset B gain
between them, and both restoration and consumer are in
original U, independently verified. The gain comes from
the third double of KEEP 4, beyond \(2a_h=2\) for that
restoring KEEP. Its whole frame and completed parent
output meet neither exposure payment. This rejects any
claim that these exposures are automatic at an upper
neutral restorer. The old protected hybrid transfer
does pay it. There are no original R6 gaps on this
source, so it is not an actual frozen-tail receiver.

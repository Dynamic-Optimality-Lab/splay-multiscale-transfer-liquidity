# Packet overflow: proved reduction and open step

Let T consist only of the distinct consuming parents of the fixed
literal overflow gaps. No original quota is refilled. At KEEP h it
has exactly \(e_h=(b_h(D)-2a_h)_+\) vertices. If its kth parent has
original double ordinal j, then \(j\ge 2a_h+k\); hence pre-B depth
is at least \(4a_h+2e_h\) when \(e_h>0\), and the sum of original
g-to-target path lengths of its tail is at least
\(4a_h e_h+e_h(e_h+1)\). This is C04 P5 geometry, not A credit.
The earlier selected D parents need not share that geometry.

Run the new last-promotion selector on this T, with actual lifted
cyclic column intervals. The previously budgeted E_old selection
does not change. The interval for a column can contain a demotion
already used elsewhere; the new reset budget explicitly counts
its additional bounded reuse. Distinct old grandparents in a
single packet do not automatically have distinct A donors.

After the paid cases in GLOBAL_A_WORK_ACCOUNTING, let
\(T_N\) be no-promotion retention and \(T_S\) the remaining
silent-restoration receivers. Their union is the exact complement
among the original overflow parents, with original gap weights
unchanged. In particular every receiver in \(T_S\) has a distinct
last non-target B promotion at a same-side restoring double
which is beyond its own paid prefix, not in the protected paid
operation class, fully neutral, with whole frame unexposed and
completed parent output not A-acquired during its B membership
run. A no-promotion receiver has continuous ancestry for its
particular target, not necessarily unchanging B subtree.

The proved bound is
\[
\sum_h e_h\le(8+10C_{\rm DF})A+|T_N|+|T_S|.
\]
It uses an independently paid subset, not a claim that the last
two quantities are bounded. The exact missing implication is
\[
 |T_N|+|T_S|\le K_5 A
\]
for an absolute constant on all admissible W, or a substitute
direct all-double theorem. A result here would give a valid K
in the frozen target, namely \(8+10C_{\rm DF}+K_5\). Each paid
parent can have up to three original gap slots, so the correct
full coefficient includes that factor, with no extra prefix
or C04 local-return credit.

**IAGS proof box: hard restoration.** Target: bound the selected
silent B promotions jointly over original sources. Conditions
that suffice: source-derived price for the actual non-target
promotions not covered by old comparison, prefix or SET
exposure. Inheritance: unique promotion donor per cyclic
column interval, completed parent output, signed SET closure.
Ambiguity: many marked descendant gains may be created in one
silent event; mass is not its cost. The 24-key complete source
shows genuine silent, upper, neutral restoration with both
involved doubles in U before hybrid payment. Bridge: count
distinct donors, then transfer one unit to each receiver.
No absolute price on the stated hard subset is proved.

**IAGS proof box: retained columns.** Target: bound \(T_N\)
jointly, not the cost of each single g. Inheritance: strictly
shrinking frames in each no-promotion interval and exact mixed
output structure. Ambiguity: many columns can reuse the same
target without gains; finite physical cases refute local span
capacity, and an exact chain refutes a size-independent
pointwise depth bound. The fixed-column 3A law still holds.
Bridge required: aggregate original A work across overlapping
retargeting branches, or a comparison that covers them in the
fixed residual domain. No such bound is proved.

All tested new sources are old-periodic paid. Empty measured
post-hybrid inventories and nonempty diagnostic restoration
forests are deliberately separate statements.

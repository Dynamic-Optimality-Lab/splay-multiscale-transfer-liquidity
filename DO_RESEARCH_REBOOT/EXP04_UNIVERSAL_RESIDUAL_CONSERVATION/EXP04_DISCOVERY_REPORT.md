## Cycles4–6: skipped ranks, rank traffic, and actual cost-certified spans

The consecutive-rank comparison restriction can be relaxed using an added
established theorem. Cole's dynamic-finger theorem has a linear initialization
term, allowing a fixed absolute C_DF in the bound
`C_DF[N+m+sum log2(1+rank distance)]`. Its primary records are the
[published theorem](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/S009753979732699X)
and [author-institution report](https://cs.nyu.edu/dynamic/reports/?year=1995).
This external result was consulted; its full long proof was not independently
reproved or formally audited here.

For a monotone shadow initialized from actual complete B, fix J as the union
of its initial physical target paths. J is ancestor-closed; all real search
paths and rotations stay inside it, and outside components keep their internal
structure. On the actual J-tree, distinct target rank gaps telescope to at
most |J|-1. Zero-cost repeated targets are collapsed only in this comparison.
Thus `Q_shadow<=3 C_DF |J|`, including skipped ranks and interior anchors.

Two deterministic selectors were tested: increasing/decreasing record targets,
and the earliest feasible long monotone subsequence, chosen using target-order
suffix lengths. They use no residual or matching success to choose windows.
Each accepted domain satisfies `|J|<=2(ell-1)`. Successive retained roots give
distinct mandatory original A StepEv intervals. One system has `sum|J|<=2A`;
four systems have witness load at most4 and give `R_mono<=72 C_DF A`, through
the same-labelled-grandparent injection at the same original KEEP and three
original gaps per parent. Pruning outside components was separately checked
against the immutable tuple implementation on the critical witnesses.

These comparisons cover the inherited residual finitely, but testing continues.
Scrambled continuations leave new original consumers without any such monotone
representative. An independently computable whole-cycle rank certificate helps:
the inherited closed-core theorem gives `|S|<=2|M|-1<=2A`; the cyclic number of
genuine KEEP blocks is at most A. If integer log-rank travel L is at most2A,
dynamic finger gives `Q_B<=5 C_DF A`, hence `R_*<=15 C_DF A` by three slots.
There is no universal L<=2A premise. Cheap extreme-target padding violates it;
an explicit diagonal family and weighted-access argument refute every fixed
constant version of that premise. It has no difficult residual and does not
refute DO.

Composing the two adversaries defeats both comparison strategies and the
whole-cycle certificate. In composed_p4_alternating_inside_m256, A932/Q_B732,
gap `(645,(72,3),(80,1))`, parent(645,2), remains unpaid and has
`L/A=3995/932>2`. This is a new strict original obstruction, not a failed
unclosed fragment.

The repair uses **actual operation spans**. From the next unowned original
operation, choose the latest prefix whose fixed initial path union J and
actual target sequence satisfy

\[
D=|J|+m+L_J\le4A(\text{span}).
\]

Here L_J is integer log-rank travel in J, m counts B target blocks, and every
actual KEEP is retained. If no eligible prefix exists, advance one operation.
The rule uses no B cost or demand test. Fixed-J physical isolation gives
`Q_B(span)<=C_DF D`. Selected actual spans are disjoint, including DELETE
preparation and closing work. Therefore

\[
\boxed{R_{\rm density}\le3\sum Q_B(\text{span})
\le12C_{\rm DF}A.}
\]

This pays the composed witness and52 gaps in the final successive partition.
It does not prove that all remaining consumers universally belong to accepted
spans. An accepted span need not itself be a paired-closed source; the original
source is fully closed and supplies all of its real A work.

## Cycles7–10: sparse lifecycles and a direct potential

Warmed mapped H words and leaf-scan words test private vertices without
inventing restoration. Each retains its actual common-diagonal prefix and

The run includes18 new sources with no complete A/B equality boundary. No

82->116 at `B(325,4,0)` and recreates it at `B(348,3,0)` before consuming at

original whole-frame Pi and first-hit conditions remain valid. A causal theory
must accommodate B retirement/recreation, not only uninterrupted old depth.

A local physical subclass is also proved: when a consumer's old g lies on
its PRE-A target path, distinct such parents inject into actual A rotations
with pivot g at that access. This gives `R_own<=6A`, strictly weaker than
requiring identical complete A/B paths. It is recorded as an optional partial
class; no free6A term is silently added to the main reconstruction.

Finally a direct potential was investigated beyond comparison matching:
`w_A(v)=4^(-depth_A(v))` and
`Phi=log2 product_v [W_A(B-subtree(v))/w_A(v)]`.
Its B-side access-lemma bound is constant because the target has weight1
and total A weight<=2. Its A-side law fails parametrically. In the ascending
DELETE preparation, one rotation raises Phi by more than k at A root k.
Even signed total A drift is not O(A): the first B access to n=2m+1 drops Phi
by at least `2m(m+1)-1`, whereas other B accesses increase it by at most4 each.
Paired closure forces signed A drift at least that drop minus4A. The omitted
family has A=4n-1. Thus this potential's drift is quadratic for linear A work.
Exact Fraction/hex certificates through513 and full complete witnesses are
saved. The weighted Access Lemma's fixed-weight convention was checked in
[Sleator–Tarjan, Lemma1](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sleator/papers/self-adjusting.pdf).
Failure of this potential does not exclude other potentials or prove failure
of residual conservation.

One theorem: certify the historical global all-B-double forest, its actual
A-bit root injection, and the exact frozen residual partition. No descendant
conservation or Dynamic Optimality work is in scope.

The historical priority R, then P, then C will be frozen explicitly. The
mathematical audit independently reconstructs completed-output descent and
cyclic interval disjointness. A separately implemented streaming two-lap
classifier will be compared to the historical retrospective construction.
Both are checked against independently derived immutable tuple paths,
frames and complete operation states.

An additional proposed consequence will be checked without changing the
partition: an overflow parent cannot be a global forest root, because a
root outside the original prefix is a first old-pool use in its A-demotion
epoch and is already removed by E_old. The required conservative6A bound
will remain stated even if this sharper zero-weight property is proved.

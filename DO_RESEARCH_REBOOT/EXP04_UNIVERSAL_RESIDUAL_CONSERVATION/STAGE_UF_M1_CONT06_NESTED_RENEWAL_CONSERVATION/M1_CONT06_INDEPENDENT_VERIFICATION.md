This is same-agent mathematical work with separately implemented computational checks, not external review or formal-kernel certification. The universal structural arguments use primitive component identities and induction; finite audits are implementation evidence only.

These deliberately reuse the inherited padded FAMILY, including two previously known physical parameter controls; they are fresh original replays and classification records, not six newly discovered families.

It stores all original primitives, double identities, pre/post step states and full-state pool. prefix_check verifies the common-diagonal reaching word separately and excludes its work from measured A.
2. independent0 constructs/checks the all-double forest. physical1's separate edge updater checks literal deleted/added edges and complete states. birthc separately reconstructs original SET/Pi/U/cohort/pair/R6 predicates; native6/adapterc runs the original unchanged predicates and requires exact U/all-pair agreement. Its original C_old predecessor check uses offline cyclic event distances rather than the inherited two-lap streaming implementation. Its full_physical recheck independently replays EVERY fresh primitive through edge/SET/sign/full-boundary checks at seal.

The two stored long expansion cases are explicitly skipped in this OPTIONAL check; their complete sealed physical/guard/predicate scopes remain preserved. This is not a test of every historical PF transition.

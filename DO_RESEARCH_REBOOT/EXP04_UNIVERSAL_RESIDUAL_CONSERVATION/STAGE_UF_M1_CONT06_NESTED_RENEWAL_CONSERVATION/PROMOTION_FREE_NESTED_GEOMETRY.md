## Composition, not just pairwise descent

Let w_0,...,w_r be a maximal chain of PF canonical C_old edges. All F_i and J_i refer to the same physical g and fixed labelled universe at the specified timestamps. For each adjacent pair,

    J_(i+1) proper-subset F_(i+1) subseteq J_i proper-subset F_i.

Induction therefore proves strict nesting of BOTH the F sequence and the J sequence. Targets of successive vertices are distinct; a retired target cannot return below g without a promotion. Orientation changes do not break this composition: they cut the other side of the same interval rooted at g. This proves no laminarity across different g columns or sibling branches. ## Equality at the inter-access inclusion

F_v=J_u is possible. Each intervening B pivot at g would strictly remove a nonempty physical subtree. No promotion can undo it. Equality thus excludes EVERY intervening demotion of g. Conversely, a nonidentity B access strictly inside its current component must rotate g, hence shrink it in this PF interval. Consequently equality makes v the first subsequent inside hit of J_u, with the whole component unchanged internally up to that hit. External attachment changes are allowed. There is then no intervening ALL-double old-g use; u is also the immediate all-double predecessor, and an original U consumer's inherited creator law gives the chosen-output G birth at u.

Strict F_v subset J_u means at least one intervening inside hit/demotion at g. It may be cheap or absent from C_old; an opposite old-parent output may be the eventual U birth. It supplies no new A reset. It is therefore invalid to identify filtered u with the all-double predecessor or creator in general.

## Order restriction stronger than finite height

Throughout a PF block, all full g-components are physical key intervals containing g. At any inside right hit x>g, g loses every remaining descendant >=x, possibly more. Thus later inside right hits strictly decrease. Left inside hits strictly increase. The entire hit word can interleave these two sequences and need not itself be monotone.

## G0. A-demotion-free keys are permanently untouched components — PROVED

Fix an oldg of an actual consuming B double in a nontrivial exactly
paired-closed source. If no measured A rotation demotes g, then no original
A access can have g as strict ancestor: ordinary splay would demote it.
No A access can target g either. Such an access leaves A rooted at g;

subtree at g, whose full labelled internal structure stays fixed throughout

connector. Reaching-prefix rotations are not measured credit.

not a characterization by root identity.

# Source-uniform separator and intact-component compensation

These are sufficient conditions for the EXISTING frozen systems. They are not a proof of all-source compensation and create no extra budget.

## Separator-window theorem

Fix an original positive-Psi episode and decreasing records (reflect for increasing). Let c be a genuine KEEP block that is first in its episode or whose immediately preceding KEEP target is min S. Assume the next min-S KEEP after c is in the same episode. Let T be the decreasing records from c through that first minimum; compute J from their actual pre-c B paths. If |J|<=2(|T|-1), the frozen records selector opens and accepts precisely this window at c.

Proof: an earlier accepted records window stops at the first minimum it encounters and cannot cross the preceding minimum to consume c. Rejected openings advance by one original block. A minimum opening cannot retain the larger c. Thus the scan reaches c. The proposed record list ends at its first minimum, since there is no smaller rank. The stated domain test is exactly the frozen physical eligibility predicate, so acceptance follows. No demand or gap predicate was used to define this source-derived condition. Original repeats belong to their collapsed blocks, and all actual DELETE A work stays in the original transition intervals.

If all KEEPs through some h in the prefix of this accepted window are retained, original and shadow complete B states agree there, by identical initial state and identical splay word. Consequently every original old-grandparent parent at h matches. The full source need not have small raw/Lambda cost.

## Intact-component first-hit theorem

Fix any accepted frozen shadow, and any retained operation r at which original B and shadow have the same COMPLETE rooted component T at the same physical key g after that access. Suppose h is the first later original KEEP with target inside T, and h is retained in this same window. Then the full T component stays equal in both executions until h, even if arbitrary intervening original KEEPs were omitted by the shadow.

An access outside a rooted component never enters it: all rotations take place on the target path, and transfer whole off-path components without changing their internal children. DELETE freezes original B. Every intervening shadow access is one of those original outside requests. Hence both copies preserve T internally. At h they have the same search suffix from g to the common target.

Any ORIGINAL B double with old grandparent u in that suffix and with both lower path edges inside T occurs identically in shadow. Indeed its distance to the target is the same even integer2j in both trees; bottom-up double j is determined by that suffix. External attachments may differ, but its internal old grandparent/parent/target/case/full frame and completed outputs agree. This applies in LL/RR/LR/RL, with arbitrary intact private-key hangers. It is not a claim that a boundary ancestor ABOVE T matches.

For an original U whole-frame first-hit consumer, this theorem applies whenever its creator's relevant complete output is coupled at a retained anchor and its consumer is retained. It covers both a grandparent output and an opposite old-parent output. It uses the ORIGINAL first-hit property, not a new cohort after omissions.

## Exact failure alternatives

For such a source-defined proposed anchor, failure of its particular certificate implies at least one of: the frozen episode/opening/domain conditions fail; the anchor is not retained; the physical output is not equal at the anchor; the consumer is not retained in the same window; or it is not the first original inside hit of that equal component. If all hypotheses hold, mismatch is impossible by the primitive proof.

This is a logical necessary alternative, not a globally priced defect class. Window nonretention/domain failure is not itself an A event. An earlier inside hit can change the original U/birth classification rather than create a new residual. Existence of a useful coupled anchor for EVERY original S_UU demand, and bounded multiplicity of any resulting physical defects, remain unproved.

# Primitive rank law and guarded compensation

The original C3 law remains
\[
B_{\rm raw}\le4A+\Lambda_A.
\]
Lambda_A counts bitlength of marked middle-subtree size only on actual A bits moving the LCA of the two original S extremes. It is neither virtual A work nor an approved transfer X.

## Necessary support and traffic

Let d be a nonzero cyclic KEEP rank displacement. Its raw cost is1+bitlength(d). The number of nonzero cyclic KEEP transitions is K_B<=A, using disjoint original incoming A gaps. Hence
\[
B_{\rm raw}\le[1+\operatorname{bitlength}(|S|-1)]A.
\]
For |S|<=512, raw<=10A. A genuine positive z therefore requires |S|>=513, as well as Lambda_A>6A and failure of all G/H/local systems. Empty/single-target/zero-A sources have no B-double demand and need no division.

This derived barrier motivated the518-key attempt; tests confined to small support could not possibly supply a raw-negative escape.

## Exact replication formula

For the theorem's existing paired two-zig block, let d>=1 be its fixed S-rank distance. Its middle subtree contains d-1 marked keys. Every EXTRA copy has two real A zigs and contributes
\[
\Delta A=2,\quad
\Delta B_{\rm raw}=2[1+\operatorname{bitlength}(d)],\quad
\Delta\Lambda_A=2\operatorname{bitlength}(d-1).
\]
The signed rank change is zero. The G/H normalized cyclic cost at the block cut is unchanged. Thus unbounded unpriced Lambda traffic can be erased, and raw failure does not force a surviving packet.

## Missing coupling

No residual-sensitive subset of Lambda is known with a source-uniform A price. To defeat the replication theorem one must alter a retained physical trajectory/domain or the applicable guard/support/certificate assumptions. A proof that every such alteration covering actual Z_h has an absolute global A cost is still missing. A large Lambda alone is not that proof.

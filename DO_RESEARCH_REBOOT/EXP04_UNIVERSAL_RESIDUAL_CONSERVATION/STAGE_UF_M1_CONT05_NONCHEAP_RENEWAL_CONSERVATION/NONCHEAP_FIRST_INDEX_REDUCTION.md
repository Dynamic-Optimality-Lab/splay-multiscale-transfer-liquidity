## No-reset physical exception

Any nonempty all-double old-g column with no A demotion must have a B non-target promotion of g somewhere in its cycle. Otherwise M0 assigns every use type C and strict finite frame descent gives a cyclic strict subset, impossible. Targeting g is impossible in such a nontrivial column: the next different original request, at latest an old-g consumer, would demote g in A. Non-target B promotions are bit0 of LL/RR with old parent g. Thus reset-free filtered cycles are physically promotion-mediated; they are not a counterexample to M0 acyclicity. The FILTERED L_g predecessor graph is not the M0 forest.

A=0 has no B-double demand by exact paired closure. Empty columns and epochs make no edge. No virtual A bit or excluded prefix is used.

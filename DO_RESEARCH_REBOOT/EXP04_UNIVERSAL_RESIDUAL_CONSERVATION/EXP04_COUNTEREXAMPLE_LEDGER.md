## C4. Whole-cycle rank travel has an absolute A bound — REFUTED

The c=2 version already fails on osc_p4_m256_inside_off0, with A832 and
integer cyclic log-rank travel3738. Its genuine R_* event is still preserved.
The m1024 version has A2368 and travel14490. Monotone comparisons happen to
pay their residuals; this does not make the failed rank premise true.

An all-constant refutation uses a diagonal-start source: KEEP1..n, alternate
KEEP1/KEEPn m times, then KEEP n..1. Complete sorted restoration closes both
trees, and A=B throughout. All n keys are genuine support. Rank travel is at
least `2(m-1)ceil(log2 n)`. In the alternating block assign weight1 to each
extreme and weight1/n^2 to the other keys. The classical weighted access lemma
bounds its total A cost by `C0 m+C1(n)`, C0 absolute, since each requested
weight is1 and total weight<3. Preparation/restoration and the finite potential
range are absorbed in C1(n), independent of m. Taking m sufficiently large,
then n large, makes rank travel/A unbounded. R_*=0 in this diagonal family:
rank travel's pessimism is not a cost-ratio or DO counterexample.

## C8. Adaptive weighted access-lemma potential is A-Lipschitz — REFUTED PARAMETRICALLY

Let `w_A(v)=4^(-depth_A(v))` and
`Phi=log2 product_{v in B}(sum_{u in B-subtree(v)}w_A(u))/w_A(v)`.
It is a finite nonnegative physical-state potential. With A frozen at its
access target, that target has weight1 and total weight<=2, so the standard
weighted access lemma supplies a constant B amortized bound. This B-side
success does not bound A reweighting.

During original DELETE2..n preparation, A at root k has a descending left
chain and ascending right chain; B is the original right vine. The one real
rotation for DELETE(k+1) gives the exact product ratio

`4^k product_(i=1..k) [(L_i/4+R)/(L_i+R/4)]`,

where `1<=L_i,R<=4/3`. Every factor is at least16/19; the ratio is at least
`(64/19)^k>2^k`. Thus one original A StepEv raises Phi by more than k.
Exact rational certificates at n65,129,257,513 use hex numerators/denominators,
not floating logarithms. The events are prefixes of archived complete sources.

## C9. The same potential's signed A drift is O(A) on cycles — REFUTED PARAMETRICALLY

Before the first B access to n=2m+1, A is the left vine and B the right vine.
For this fixed A, the potential product before B is at least
`4^(n(n-1)/2)`. Splaying n in B produces root n with an even-key right spine
under it and odd leaves. Its product is at most `(4/3)4^(m^2)`. The potential
drop is at least `2m(m+1)-1` in log2 units.

The original weighted Access Lemma1 bounds each genuine B-phase potential
increase by4: its target's weight is1, its pre-splay subtree mass is at least1,
and total mass is at most2. There are at most A genuine KEEP events; B root
repetitions change no potential.
Complete paired closure gives

`signed A drift >=2m(m+1)-1-4A`.

In the explicit omitted-hit source A=4n-1: n-1 ascending preparation, n-1
descending preparation, then KEEP1,2,5 and KEEP3..n cost n+2 in total, and
n-1 closing work. Thus signed drift is quadratic while A is linear. The
mixed archived family also has linear A and the same initial large drop.
Exact complete mixed witnesses and their actual costs are saved in
EXP04_WEIGHTED_SIGNED_DRIFT_REFUTATION.json. This rejects even the aggregate
A budget for this particular potential, not every potential or the target.

Weighted-access-lemma dependency: [original Sleator–Tarjan paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sleator/papers/self-adjusting.pdf).
Its external theorem is imported, not newly formally audited here; all new
ratio identities and inequalities are proved above and checked with Fractions.

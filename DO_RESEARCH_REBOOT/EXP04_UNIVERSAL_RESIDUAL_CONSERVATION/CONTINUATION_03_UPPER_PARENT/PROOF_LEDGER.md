## N. Necessary physical configuration

Every surviving parent(h,j,g) has j>2a_h, hence q(h)>2a_h. It has g absent
from the strict original pre-A path; indeed g!=target, so it is absent from
the entire path. It lacks every frozen same-operation/same-g comparison
match. Its actual consuming operation remains H-protected. Every local
raw/G/H arc through it fails5A and every full-period cut/variant has
m_cyc+L_cyc>10A, hence L_cyc>9A. No small rank-traffic premise is inferred.

Its old B g is nevertheless a strict physical ancestor of the target just
before that double; after the double it is not. This is a B-side physical
loss. At pre-A time its relation to the target is absent; no A loss at
this operation can be assigned to that already absent relation. Earlier
A losses can be shared by several targets and are not automatically
injective. Later B uses can arise by restoring an old relation OR by
requesting a different target still under g. Destruction/recreation and
retargeting are distinct chronological possibilities, not free A work.

## E. First residual use in an A-demotion epoch — PROVED, source-uniform

Fix ANY original demand set C, with its original consuming B parent IDs
and at most3 original gaps per parent. For each labelled physical key g,
mark all actual original A primitive rotation occurrences that demote g
(pivot g). If there are none in the measured cyclic period, do not provide
a donor from a reaching prefix or an imaginary earlier period.

Otherwise these marks partition cyclic chronology into half-open epochs:
after a mark through just before the next mark. A and B phases retain their
actual order. In each epoch select only the FIRST distinct C-consuming B
parent whose old grandparent is g, if one exists. Pay all original gaps
of that selected parent. E(C) is this source-defined selected occurrence
set. Choice is chronological and deterministic, not existence of a flow.

Each selected parent has a unique actual A demotion bit opening its epoch.
One bit has a single pivot g and opens one epoch. It is assigned at most
one original parent and thus at most3 original gaps. A genuine A StepEv
has at most2 physical rotations. Therefore on every admissible original
source, independent of support, histories, epoch lengths and overlaps,

    |E(C)| <=3 R_A(W)<=6A(W).

This is not the refuted last-loss rule. It limits allocation to one parent
per actual demotion epoch and leaves subsequent parents unpaid. It does
not assert all consumers are first, or that a physical g demotion loses
the relation to every future target. Its direct bounded-load proof needs
no such ancestral interpretation. Epochs for different g may overlap in
time; their donors are DIFFERENT actual bits. Two bits in one StepEv are
budgeted through R_A<=2A, not treated as two cost-one StepEv resources.

Take C to be the original remainder AFTER F_C,F_D,P and BEFORE O,H.
Then the original O class is contained in E(C). If g is a strict pre-A
ancestor at h, the actual A splay of that target demotes g before B begins.
Choose the last such g demotion in the operation. There is no intervening
B operation; within this B splay old grandparents are distinct. Thus its
original parent is the first C parent in that epoch. This proves containment
for arbitrary sources, not just tested ones.

The same argument shows E(C) contains every C parent whose g was demoted
at ANY actual A rotation in its incoming original KEEP gap: no B access
occurs between the last such bit and h. This strictly generalizes the
current-operation test structurally and permits donors across arbitrary
numbers of KEEP gaps for other first-use parents.

Replace O by E(C), with the SAME6A budget. Define

    R_renew = C \ (E(C) union H) = R_upper \ E(C).

Both equalities use O subset E(C); no original predicate is recomputed on
a comparison. The improved partial interface is

    Q_B <=(47+48 C_seq+102 C_DF)A+|R_renew|,
    |R_upper| <=6A+|R_renew|.

The second is a conservative target inequality. It is NOT added to the
old47 coefficient: O and E(C) together cost6A, rather than12A.
Adding the separately preserved C2 small-return class costs42A and gives

    |R_upper| <=48A+|R_renew,wide|,
    Q_B <=(89+48 C_seq+102 C_DF)A+|R_renew,wide|.

Here R_renew,wide is the literal remainder after removing all C2 <=8-key
actual B-return consumers. Its uniform A bound is not proved.

## E2. Exact repeat-debt alternative — PROVED identity, OPEN conservation

In each A-demotion epoch list the C parents with oldg in chronological
order. R_renew contains only later parents (and keys with NO A demotion
in the entire measured period), after H exclusions. The selected first
parent can itself be H-paid; that does not permit selecting a second for
free. A universally broader E pool may be defined once, but not changed
retrospectively to fit a witness.

Writing n_e for each epoch's distinct C-parent count and Z for parents
at A-demotion-free keys, the total C-parent count decomposes exactly into
nonempty epochs plus sum_e(n_e-1)_+ plus |Z|. At the gap level retain actual
per-parent slot multiplicities rather than pretending all have weight1.
The first term has the6A payment above. No bound on either later term is
proved by this identity. Cyclic closure supplies epoch seams, not new bits.

The first missing implication is a source-uniform O(A) bound for the
HYBRID-ESCAPING later-use and A-demotion-free original demands. Neither
signed SET ownership transfers nor B ancestry recreation is assumed to
have bounded total multiplicity. The proposed universal statement that all
original R6 are first uses is separately tested and must be rejected upon
one original repeated-use witness.

## G. Rigid A-subtree packets and full-gap avoidance — PROVED geometry

A demand in R_renew is not the first C parent of its g epoch, or g has
no A demotion in the entire measured cycle. In either case g has NO
actual A demotion anywhere in its consumer's incoming KEEP gap. If it did,
the last such bit would precede the first and only B access in that gap;
old-g uniqueness would make this parent the first C parent of that epoch.

Consequently g is absent from EVERY actual A access path in that gap,
including DELETE helpers and target endpoints. A strict ancestor is
demoted during its own access. An access targeting g leaves A rooted at g;
the next non-g access, at latest the actual consumer with target!=g, would
demote g inside the same gap. Both contradict the preceding fact. Thus
all these accesses stay outside the complete A subtree rooted at g.
Every rotation changes only their paths and reattaches that complete
subtree rigidly. Its full internal labelled structure and key set stay
fixed through the entire incoming gap, not just the last A operation.
External attachment may change. No node or hanger is contracted.

There is a related packet statement. From the A bit opening an epoch
through its LAST C parent, the completed demoted A subtree at g persists
unchanged. After its opening loss, the rest of the opening splay leaves
that component rigid (inherited completed-output induction). A later
access strictly below g would demote it, ending the epoch before B begins.
An access to g would promote it to the root, and the subsequent non-g
consumer would again force a new demotion before that parent. Therefore
no intervening complete A access can enter this subtree before the last
parent. A promotion of a non-target g during an access is followed by its
demotion in that same access, so it cannot bypass this argument.

Each later parent in this packet has a target OUTSIDE the fixed A-subtree
key interval, yet below g on its actual B path. B can remove/recreate such
ancestry or retarget another descendant. These are precise physical
alternatives; they do not show the packet has bounded demand. Many g
packets can overlap one actual A access, and no absolute overlap bound is
proved. Original signed SET ownership transfers remain in their original
histories; they do not create additional A resources.

The DW5 exact repeated-use packet demonstrates retargeting explicitly:
g34's fixed A subtree has keys[33,34,35], while its two original R6 parents
consume targets11 and19. The B relation g34->19 is not recreated between
those parents; it already persists until its own loss at the second one.
Thus neither an A repair nor a B recreation of that particular relation
is automatically forced between different-target consumers.

Finally, in the ORIGINAL pre-B path for KEEP h, the j-th B double's oldg
is exactly2j original path edges above the target. Bottom-up splay removes
two original ancestors per double, in all four orientations. A surviving
j>2a_h therefore gives original distance(g,x)=2j>4a_h>=2R_A(G_h).
This is a long B route beside a rigid off-path A component. Its impossibility
or an aggregate budget does not follow from that numerical disparity.

## F1. Immediate off-path A-fringe roots — PROVED additional scoped payment

For a residual parent with g off the pre-A path P_h to x, accept it if
the actual A parent of g lies ON P_h. This is an original complete physical
fringe root, not a contracted component or a comparison node. It need not
be a first epoch use. At each strict ancestor u of x on P_h, there is at
most one such off-path child g; at the target x there are at most two.

For the first kind, charge the actual A bit demoting u during operation h.
There is at most one relevant g per u and at most3 original gaps per B
parent; distinct oldg parents in h are distinct. Different operations use
different original A bits. Thus this part costs at most3R_A<=6A.

For the at-most-two child roots of x, use the first ACTUAL A StepEv in the
original incoming KEEP gap. It exists: a residual double is a nonidentity
B access, and distinct consecutive B roots force A work in that very gap.
The cyclic KEEP gaps partition original operations; each selected StepEv
receives at most2*3=6 gaps from this part. Its budget is6A. Therefore

    |F_fringe|<=12A.

These two parts may reuse an A occurrence, with their coefficients added;
no virtual unit is created. This is a structural eligibility predicate,
not an increased rank or density threshold. There is no claim that every
off-path g is an immediate fringe root. At arbitrary depth a fixed-width
version would require its own explicit population factor; it is not free.

Removing F_fringe after E(C) and the unchanged old hybrid, define the
literal complement R_deep=R_upper\(E(C) union F_fringe). Then

    |R_upper|<=18A+|R_deep|,
    Q_B<=(59+48 C_seq+102 C_DF)A+|R_deep|.

The scalar59 is35+6(prefix)+6(E)+12(fringe). It does NOT include a second
6 for the old own-path class, which E contains. If the preserved42A

    |R_upper|<=60A+|R_deep,wide|,
    Q_B<=(101+48 C_seq+102 C_DF)A+|R_deep,wide|.

Every remaining oldg has A-distance at least2 from the common-prefix
attachment point on its consumer's pre-A path. Together with G this gives

budget for that literal complement is proved.

## Degenerate sources, seams and ownership audit

No KEEP means no residual. One KEEP in a B-closed period is B-identity;
zero total A work similarly forces zero genuine B work. Empty demand
sets require no epoch construction. At a cyclic seam, the last actual
g demotion of the designated period opens the wrapped epoch; its original
bit is counted once. A reaching prefix is never a resource. No initial
potential or imaginary prior-lap bit is added.

All donor and demand IDs include their actual source. Several original
gaps at one B parent are retained as separate slots, at most3; distinct
parents are not merged because they share g. F_C,F_D,P,O,H and their
replacement E are assigned by successive literal set difference. These
paid sets are disjoint even when their B histories overlap. Actual A
resources may be used by different proved mechanisms, with the complete
scalar costs explicitly summed. No signed SET label is treated as an
extra physical rotation. SET births, losses, exposure, first hits, cohorts
and mixed ownership stay exactly in their original event timelines.

The E bit is not required to be a root-entry resource inside the old Pi
shared-life window. It is a different, explicitly scoped global physical
predicate. Its earlier position in the g epoch and one-parent allocation
prove the budget. It does not assert feasibility in the old root-entry
incidence graph or erase that graph's Hall obstructions. Later parents
and A-demotion-free keys remain debt even if their labels are renewed.

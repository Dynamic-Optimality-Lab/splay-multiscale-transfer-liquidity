## P7. Recycling a simultaneous same-side packet forces a B batch — PROVED

At ANY ordinary complete physical B access, select any k of its LL/RR
parents. After this access the complete rooted subtrees at their old
grandparents are PAIRWISE DISJOINT. This concerns complete physical
subtrees, including every hanger, not just S labels.

Proof: just before a same-side double, the current target-rooted subtree
X contains all completed outputs of earlier doubles. In LL, write
G.left=P, P.left=X, P.right=M. The first right rotation demotes G to the
subtree made of M,G and G's old right component; it excludes ALL of X.
The second right rotation promotes x without changing this completed G
subtree. RR is reflected. Every later splay rotation is on x's remaining
path above it and preserves each completed off-path subtree internally.
Thus each later same-side G output is disjoint from every earlier
completed G output; induction proves the assertion even with interspersed
opposite-side doubles. It does not assert disjoint outputs for an
arbitrary ALL-opposite-side packet.

Fix a later KEEP of y at which ALL these k keys are again old consuming
grandparents. At the earlier post-B boundary, y belongs to at most ONE
of their disjoint subtrees. At the later pre-B boundary each g is a strict
ancestor of y, by original-path grandparent identity. At least k-1
DISTINCT relations (g,y) must therefore be gained by intervening real B
rotations. A rotation changes descendant sets only at its pivot u and
promoted v: u loses descendants, v gains them. Thus these gains require
distinct actual B primitive promotions, each promoting its specified g.
Choosing another already retained descendant cannot reuse all k columns.
This distinguishes simultaneous width from single-column retargeting.

SAME-SIDE B double with old parent g. If instead a required gain is at a
KEEP targeting g, its genuine A access leaves root g; before the later
KEEP y!=g there must be an actual A demotion of g. The required batch
therefore splits into keys with genuine intervening A demotions, and
actual non-target B parent promotions. Distinct keys select distinct
primitive occurrences. This is event existence, not a new disjoint A

Ambiguity old-parent losses and edge rebirth need not preserve a whole B output. Bridge only descendant-set monotonicity plus full A outside-target rigidity is required. CLOSED by G1–G2.
2. **Volume account.** Target full C payment from a cyclic state potential. Conditions a constant A-flux price plus a meaningful B-restoration remainder. Inheritance exact primitive descendant changes. Ambiguity a bit can change many ancestry pairs. Bridge F_A<=K A is REFUTED by W(n,m); do not retain it under a new name.
3. **Saturated account.** Target bounded A transitions and unit C debits. Conditions count every physical C from an own-column drop. Inheritance long-chain geometry. Ambiguity saturation persists while targets are retired. Bridge fails exactly on the unbounded chain. Eligibility-localized alternatives remain unproved rather than refuted.
4. **Lifetime transfer.** Target canonical original-source restoration ownership. Conditions pair lifetimes and first closers. Inheritance exact B closure. Ambiguity one opener creates many marked pairs and chains can share it. Bridge proves volume capacity only; the absolute A/usable-M2 price is OPEN.

Inheritance all proved scoped/physical facts above. Ambiguity unbounded A component depth, gain volume and cross-chain reuse remain.

## 3. Integer normal form, and exactly what it does not solve

For integer w, D(s)<=w+D(t) implies

    floor(D(s)) <= floor(w+D(t)) = w+floor(D(t)).

Since psi is integer, rounding down preserves its floor and the range.
Thus any feasible REAL H1 debt in this certificate class can be replaced by

    D*(s) = max(floor(D(s)),c(s)),

an integer feasible H1 debt, with Lambda<=D*<=U at every admissible source.
The same statement is true on other valid projections for the lower bound;
the necessary upper bound retains its source-admissibility qualification.
This is an explicit safe operation on a given debt, not a construction of
the missing debt from an unproved global-existence premise.

When Lambda=U the normalized value is fixed. Otherwise the only integer
intervals are[0,1],[0,2],[1,2]. FIXING A SOURCE VALUE DOES NOT DISCHARGE ITS
INCIDENT INEQUALITIES. In particular, psi(s)=2 can force source debt2 even
when c(s)<2; its weight0/1 exits still impose destination requirements.

one aligned period, original source receipts and legal block/cyclic cut

Lemma. Let h:D->U be an independently source-certified complete injection
on the actual period I. Let a:D minus{p}->U be the already source-certified
injective paid map. There is a constructive free terminal u in U minus
image(a), obtained through actual h/a ownership edges. The extension
a union{p->u} keeps EVERY previous a value fixed and has the permitted
same-period block/cyclic receipt.

Algorithm. Start at d0=p. At step i inspect ui=h(di). If ui is unassigned
under a, stop. Otherwise let d_(i+1) be its unique actual owner under a
and continue. All labels are original demand/event/bit IDs in I.

Termination proof. For i>0, di is in domain(a), so never p. If an actual
demand repeats, injectivity of a and h forces its predecessors to repeat.
Reversing to the first entry eventually makes p a member of domain(a),
a contradiction. A reached common-only cycle cannot have an outside
incoming path since each vertex/unit has at most one predecessor. Because
D is finite and h is total, the walk ends at a free actual unit u, after
at most |D| demand steps. No fixed path-length bound is assumed.

Unused/owner proof. The stopping test gives u outside image(a). Every
previously paid demand remains at a(d); set only p->u. The final map is
injective and total. In particular all frozen common fE assignments are
unchanged, including ones not encountered in the walk. If u had been a
locked effective full owner, the walk would continue rather than stopping.
Any E-only owner was already retired under the inherited full/aux ordinal
interface, not newly erased to manufacture this extension.

Source eligibility proof. Each h edge has the independent original-event
stream receipt in POSITIVE_SURPLUS_PROOF.md. Each a edge is an existing
accepted physical assignment. The walk and endpoint operation are exactly
the inherited injective fixed-owner exchange, with its fixed set enlarged
to ALL already-paid demands. Its proof uses only actual injectivity and
the same-period receipt, so this stronger freezing introduces no new
matching premise. Transport BOTH endpoints of h to I before walking; a
and the Step1 cut stay fixed. Every walked unit is in I, original event
and bit identities remain unchanged, and no source copy is added.

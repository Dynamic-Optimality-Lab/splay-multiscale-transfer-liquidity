# Constructive source-period one-slot extension

Input: the complete actual source period Pi, its physical events, original
full/aux states, certified frozen fE and the accepted Campaign10 partial
map a. Let D,U,C,P,N,R,Pstar,Wstar be the unchanged inherited definitions.
One may equivalently supply the source-certified a directly; the theorem
quantifies EVERY such paid injection and does not guess fE values.

The public single_slot_allocator.py gives independent_full, extend_one
and from_frozen_auxiliary. Actual source membership, erased replay, legal
alignment and the frozen auxiliary certificate remain source premises;
no arbitrary numeric input is promoted to a certified history.

1. Keep all original macro occurrence lists and actual A event/bit order.
   Construct h0 from the first min(Q,2H) demands/own units. Recover unique
   r=(t*,3), and the exact existing partial map a/anchor from frozen fE.
2. The source-Hamiltonian theorem gives a positive macro, with no anonymous-
   helper assumption. Select the first positive macro in the fixed cyclic
   order and its first h0-unused actual bit v. Build complete independent
   h=h0 union{r->v}. Certify its original-event stream and period transport.
3. If Pstar empty, return a unchanged. Otherwise p is its exact actual
   demand, either r or a blocked root anchored at common r. Freeze a on
   ALL D minus{p}. Walk h through a owners until the free terminal u.
4. Return f'=a union{p->u}, retaining the full event/owner/period path.

For every nonempty case, u is an actual original A-event bit in the one
legal interval, free under a and outside every effective common fE owner.
Every old a assignment is unchanged, the final map is injective and all
actual BF demands are covered. Native source replay and shared endpoints
are never altered. B terminal zigs are retained in states but are not
extra DOUBLE demands. No padded/erased-run event contributes capacity.

Chart corrections are covered through actual physical demands, not an
unproved potential. Full D contains EVERY QF demand; C/P/N uses actual
QE at every retained occurrence, including full-root repeats. The exact
identity QF-QE=|P|-|N|=D_erased+J_retained retains signed interference.
Any extra full double represented by a chart-switch correction is already
in D and funded once by f'. A chart potential difference is not a second
physical demand and is neither assumed nonnegative nor counted as credit.
No global chart debt is proved by this construction.

Period validity: build h at a genuine full cut before its chosen positive
macro, carry ONE actual bit until r, then transport both endpoint IDs to
the fixed Step1 interval I. Each source position occurs once. Keep the
actual fE cut and all a values in I unchanged during the terminal walk.
Later-in-I donors are permitted by the inherited cyclic receipt; no prior/
future copy or reaching prefix is spent. |R| is physical before dividing
by an alignment factor, so repeated periods are never collapsed.

This proves C7-ONE-SLOT-FUNDING at its exact scope, and QF<=2A for this
subclass follows from |D|<=|U|. It does not claim unrestricted core7,
multi-slot funding, full CD6, Step2 or arbitrary-support induction.

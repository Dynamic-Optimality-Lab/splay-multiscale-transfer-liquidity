# Exact zero-surplus necessary rigidity

This is a NECESSARY reduction of the candidate regime Splus=0. It does
not by itself assert that such a source exists or that it has an available
bit. ZERO_SURPLUS_DONOR.md proves the candidate impossible.

Each natural genuine full macro starts with synchronized marked root g
and ends at another marked target x. Thus H>=1. At core7, Q<=3. Exactly
one excess slot gives one macro H1/Q3. If Splus=0, every other macro has
2H=Q. Since H>=2 would force Q>=4, impossible, every other macro is H1/Q2.
No H-positive Q0 excursion is silently discarded; it would create surplus
or raise its enclosing H and contradict this candidate regime.

For any H1 macro the entire unique positive A event must target its final
root x. Before that event, all A root accesses cost0 and target g. After
it, all zero-cost A accesses target x. A nonterminal KEEP x would already
be the genuine full macro end, so all intervening post-event operations
are DELETE x. The exact regular word is

    {Dg,Kg}* Kx,
    or {Dg,Kg}* Dx (Dx)* Kx,

with x at actual A depth1 or2 when its sole positive event occurs. In the
first word that event is at Kx; in the second it is the first Dx. There
is NO positive anonymous-helper event and no hidden second positive A
access. Root repeats and their BE effects remain timestamped occurrences.

At genuine boundaries, A therefore performs exactly one ordinary splay
of x from its preceding actual A tree. BF performs its own ordinary splay
of x from its own preceding tree. Both boundary SHADOWS execute the same
genuine target word. This does NOT identify their trees, actual interior
operation schedules or costs; BF Q is2 or3 while A H is1. It also does
NOT identify BE with BF: erasure may skip x and root repeats can still
change the auxiliary tree. All physical event IDs are the original ones,
including an earlier Dx event in the second normal form.

The characterization is uniform over period length, sparse key labels,
arbitrary private populations and both orientations. It is not a proof
that all same-word pairs are reachable or periodic, and it does not apply
the scalar five-key theorem to a six-key comparison. We retain all actual
source reachability, exact full paired closure and legal period hypotheses.

There is no extra positive A helper to mine in this candidate. The final
argument instead contradicts its BF transitions: the unique Q3 needs
a Hamiltonian input, but no preceding Q2/Q3 splay can end there. No A/B
convergence theorem or unproved common-word uniqueness is needed.

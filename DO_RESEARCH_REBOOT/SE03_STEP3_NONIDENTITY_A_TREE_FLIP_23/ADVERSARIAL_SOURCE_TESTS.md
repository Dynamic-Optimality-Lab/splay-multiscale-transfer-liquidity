## Positive cost despite inverse physical flips

Let D=P(D0,D1), C<P<R and

    T=G(L,R(C(X,P(D0,D1)),E));
    U=G(L,C(X,R(P(D0,D1),E))).

Original A accesses C,G send T to U with2 events. The A-only word

    P,R,G,C,R,G

sends U back to T. Its exact intermediate shapes are

    P(G(L,C(X,D0)),R(D1,E));
    R(P(G(L,C(X,D0)),D1),E);
    G(L,P(C(X,D0),R(D1,E)));
    C(G(L,X),P(D0,R(D1,E)));
    R(P(C(G(L,X),D0),D1),E);
    G(L,R(C(X,P(D0,D1)),E)).

endpoints unchanged, preserving source closure and critical helper profiles.

refutes only cancellation of costs from net A-state identity.

## Refutation distinctions

The source controls refute ZERO-CORRECTION/identity lifting of a deleted
nonidentity helper. They do NOT refute corrected compression: the main
theorem proves that source valid after the explicit repair. The outer
tree calculation refutes extending the x-join lemma to arbitrary outer
deletions; it is not reported as a new independently replayed closed
counterexample. No control refutes CD6, Coarse-Double, the corrected FLIP23
theorem, or a future owner-protection theorem.

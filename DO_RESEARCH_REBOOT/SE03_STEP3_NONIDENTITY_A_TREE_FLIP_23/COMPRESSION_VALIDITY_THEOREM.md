# FLIP23-D — valid, corrected and rejected compression modes

Status: mathematical derivation, independent audit pending.

## Mode0: exact identity excision (C22, unchanged)

If an interval returns BOTH original A and actual B_E, deleting it from
the auxiliary proof word preserves every surviving state/event. Reachable
closed six-key source and actual coordinate transport must still hold.
Those are C22's hypotheses, not consequences of raw costs.

## Mode1: nonidentity canonical inner excision (new)

The extended D_G or D_R returns B_E but sends T to U in A. Naive deletion
therefore first disagrees at the corresponding post-D boundary: shadow
A is T, original A is U. Both roots are G; equal roots DO NOT mean equal
trees. Root agreement at a later retained KEEP is satisfied by ordinary
splaying in each execution, but its A depth must be computed separately.

The next source x access has depth2 in the original and3 in the shadow,
cost1 versus2. Their final A trees are exactly the same. Thus deletion
is a LEGITIMATE closed-source projection only with this proved rejoining
and the two-bit unit transport. It is INVALID to claim identical future
A histories or map shadow(tau_e,2,b) to a nonexistent original event.

All KEEPs outside D remain original in the auxiliary model; B_E states
and double IDs there agree. Original deleted full B demands stay in the
ledger, and original deleted A bits are partitioned into repair/protected
units. SOURCE_VALIDITY gives closed original source controls whose naive
zero-discrepancy assertion fails at a later DELETE x catcher.

The full projection theorem and six-key/cut checks are in
CD6_LEGAL_TRANSFER_INTERFACE. This is a PROVED sufficient condition,
not a claim that every erased-B identity can be compressed this way.

## Mode2: retained nonidentity interval (valid direct route)

Keep every actual operation of a noncanonical or outer component. The
auxiliary source A is then the original A on that component (when outside
a selected correction corridor). Its actual state change and positive
cost remain. CD6 on the whole reachable closed erased source is legal.
The component supplies no guaranteed excision reserve. This is a complete
source comparison interface without a hypothesized rejoining condition.

## Rejected: deleting an anonymous outer flip with zero correction

For G3 let T_outer=G(L,P(R(C(X,D),E),F)), P>R. The actual h,g pair sends
it to T=G(L,R(C(X,D),P(E,F))). Subsequent canonical s,t,v,p send A to
U with E'=P(E,F). If the OUTER prefix as well is naively deleted, the
shadow x result is

    x(G(L,B0),P(C(B1,R(D,E)),F)),

whereas actual x result is

    x(G(L,B0),C(B1,R(D,P(E,F)))).

Their right-child keys P and C differ. Thus the inner x-rejoining lemma
does NOT justify deleting the outer flip. This is an exact tree-identity
refutation of that overextension, not a newly replayed full-source
counterexample. The outer prefix is retained (or its whole overlap
component retained), so the legal C23 theorem has no such false premise.

No endpoint-only scalar potential, sign assumption or whole-period
telescoping is used to excuse a missing source state. Applying CD6 to a
shadow without proved six-key reachability/closure and actual bit mapping
remains forbidden; CD6 itself is unchanged.

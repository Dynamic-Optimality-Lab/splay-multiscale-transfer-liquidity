# Public-debt construction: the remaining unresolved gap

No universal public debt function has been constructed in this campaign.
The erased-helper reduction in HELPER_REDUCTION.md is a valid implication
of the already necessary original H1/range inequalities; it does not supply
an H1 potential or establish M3. Both gaps are therefore not marked closed.

An apparent inventory-independent construction would be

    D_max(s)=sup{ delta(C(w)) : w compatible with the shared state s }.

For a specified bounded delta and nonempty compatibility fibre this is a
function of the shared state, independent of which witness was selected.
However no universal signed-preserving delta on the required private image
has been constructed, and taking a supremum does not by itself transport
its source extremizer along EVERY original shared edge.

This is a logical obstruction, not a new finite-population experiment.
Let 0<=q<p<=2 be arbitrary symbolic numbers. Suppose one shared source s
has compatible private objects a,b; shared destination t has c; another
destination u has d. Let private edges be a->c and b->d of weight0, with

    delta(a)=delta(c)=q,       delta(b)=delta(d)=p.

Both private inequalities have residual0. The shared edge s->t nevertheless
has supremum-debt residual q-p<0. Thus the proposed universal implication
"private inequalities imply public supremum inequalities" is false. This
is an order-theoretic proof for arbitrary q<p, not a claim that these
particular fibres are an original M3 graph or a counterexample to M3.

A minimum has the corresponding destination-extremizer issue. A proven
coupling/matching theorem for the ACTUAL private fibres, or a debt constant
on every fibre, would be needed. Assuming such a theorem merely renames the
remaining compatibility burden. Independently chosen original witnesses
cannot be replaced by a persistent private history; the frozen state and
midpoint sharing must remain exactly as specified.

The reused GAPMULTI01 witnesses already show that the private Score split
is not determined by the shared state. They do NOT prove that no public
debt exists: the3761 sector certificates demonstrate valid public debts
on their domains. The unproved task is their universal structural extension,
including H1 signed preservation and the remaining Score-bearing-helper
families. No five-population envelope or numerical fit is offered here.

The completed new helper theorem reduces an earlier difficulty: initially
Score-empty Gamma1 helpers add no independent inequalities beyond H1 and
range. Its non-range cases have a lower-weight H1 edge on the SAME FULL
shared endpoints. Therefore that reduction does not require resolving the
private projection or assuming any erasure invariance of the debt.

RESULT: a universal erased-helper redundancy proof and independent symbolic
audit; public shared-state H1 debt still OPEN. AFTER: no M3 promotion and
no claim that both gaps are solved. No finite tests, new source constraints,

No original signature is newly certified, and full M3/Dynamic Optimality

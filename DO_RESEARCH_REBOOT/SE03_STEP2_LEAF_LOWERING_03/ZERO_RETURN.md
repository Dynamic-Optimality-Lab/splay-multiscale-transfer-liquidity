# R0 — partial universal exclusion, general case open

Target: no actual zero-only T11->F11 return. Conditions: original full
core11 domain, exact states and packetization. Inheritance: F11 has<=2
marked leaves, T11>=4, local leaf-loss classification and first-entry
theorem. Ambiguities: general all-H1 neutral-prefix accessibility is
unproved. Bridge: a return must first realize a leaf-lowering zero.

PROVED universally: a five-leaf T11 start has no zero successor, since
its marked-parent tree is a star and all genuine B accesses have q1.
For a four-leaf start, every packet before the first leaf loss must be
a leaf-neutral H1/Q2 macro. Neither H2/Q4 restoration nor a paired zero
nor a positive anonymous helper can intervene before that first loss.
The first loss must have the exact source groups and A geometry in
LEAF_LOWERING_CLASSIFICATION.md. None of these statements asserts that
all such first-loss sources are impossible.

## Restricted source families now sealed for arbitrary zero-path length

All known forced-zero source families (Omega+, Sigma and modified Gamma)
have an extreme BF root, unary unmarked separators and at most2 marked
leaves. The inherited exclusion gives no final zero edge into them.
This immediately excludes any zero path ending in those actual terms,
irrespective of its starting T11, BE, recent order or gap populations.

There is also a new FORWARD theorem: no zero/+1 path of cost<=1 starting
from ANY of the three exact known T11 A/B term families reaches ANY F11.
The proof is not bounded path search. The source-complete inductive
closure certificate and its arbitrary-fringe lifting are explained in
SOURCE_REPLAY.md. It has6,2,4 term/cost cells respectively, no missing
H2 or paired action, and no cell with A-one-event q4/q5 negative geometry.
Its reflected certificate is independently checked. Full BE and chart
variation are permitted: projection is used only for source exclusion,
with every full path proved to map into the displayed closure.

## Remaining general R0 obligation

A hypothetical return must therefore start outside the known T families
and end outside the known F families. It must first pass through a
four-leaf, leaf-neutral H1/Q2 prefix and then realize the local loss
configuration. Arbitrarily many neutral steps are not excluded by the
local consecutive-loss bound. After two losses, zero H2/Q4 restoration
may again become available in other source geometries, with anonymous
helpers and actual A costs; the explicit family only closes its own
restoration branch. No general exclusion or R0 countercertificate exists
in this campaign. R0 remains OPEN at its full declared scope.

## Extension C3, independent of A/BE/recent

Let C3 have extreme marked BF root, all unmarked core vertices unary,
and at most THREE marked leaves. No singleton with Q>=2 enters it:
the extreme target has an all-left/right source path; its second double
creates a branching unmarked vertex retained by outer frames. A q5/q1

price grammar then excludes every zero or negative incoming packet.

proof; it is not a changed definition of F11.

## Restricted R1 theorem

No zero packet enters any of the known F terms, so the sole +1 of a
cheap path ending there must be the LAST packet. Its source is Z or J
above (or impossible for Gamma). Every zero predecessor of Z/J has
just been classified, with depth at most1 and marked-leaf count<=3.
Since T11 has at least4 leaves, it cannot start that zero prefix. This
PROVES R1 exclusion from ANY actual T11 to any of these parameterized
known F families, arbitrary gaps/BE/recent contexts included.

In particular T11 cannot reach the actual Sigma +1 predecessor by a
zero chain. The reflected statement follows from separate frame identities,
not an assumed symmetry of a previous debt formula. The erroneous old

argument still closes.

## General R1 remains open

The +1 may lie before or after the first zero leaf-loss in other F/T
geometries. If before it, the universal first-loss prefix theorem proves
it is H1/Q1, not H2/Q3; if after, the first-loss prefix has only zeros.
All preceding zero macros are H1/Q2 and no positive anonymous helper
can occur in that prefix. Existence of the first lowering configuration
under these joint A/B restrictions remains unproved. We do not relocate
the sole +1 to the end for the GENERAL target, nor infer it from Sigma.

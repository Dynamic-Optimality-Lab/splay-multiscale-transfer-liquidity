## Two source bounds on creator-group size

First, g_p<=2H_p: each StepEv creates at most two jointly qualified
watched-edge births, and one edge lifetime has at most one selected death.

Second, g_p<=r-1. A creator and its H1 frontier cannot belong to the
SAME natural macro: that frontier's unique positive access destroys
the watched edge, whereas a different positive creator would make H>1.
Every chosen creator edge from p must therefore still be present at
the END of p; if it had died earlier, the frontier would pair with a
later birth. Different such exported lifetimes have different marked
child keys, since a node has only one actual immediate parent at p's
end. Its final A root is the genuine marked target and has no incoming
edge. Thus there are at most r-1 exported claims. These arguments hold
for the marked-parent OUTER watched choice as well as anonymous edges.

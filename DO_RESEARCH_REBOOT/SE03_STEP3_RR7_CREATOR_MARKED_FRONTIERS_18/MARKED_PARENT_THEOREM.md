## 1. The inner-edge barrier

Suppose a completed-access boundary contains the directed left edge
p->x. If p has a lower ancestor b<p, then b is outside the proper
x subtree X. While this edge remains true at subsequent completed
access boundaries, there is ALWAYS some present key below p outside X.

Proof by original accesses. If the next target z is outside X, X is an
off-search-path hanger and its entire labeled subtree stays rigid under
ordinary splaying. The old witness b therefore remains outside it.
If z is inside X and the edge p->x is still present afterward, z is
the new root and z<p, whereas p and X are proper descendants of z.
Thus z is a new witness outside the new X. These two cases exhaust
every actual access, including DELETEs. At a boundary with p root and
the edge still present, ALL present keys below p must belong to X,
contradicting the invariant. Therefore this inner-born edge cannot
persist until p is root. Mirror: a right edge born with an upper
ancestor cannot persist to root exposure.

This is a Boolean edge-lifetime statement at actual access boundaries;
it does not assume unobserved intermediate trees are source boundaries.
Even an access which destroys/recreates an edge internally obeys the
two-case proof. Arbitrary private BST populations are included.

## 3. Joint event capacity is still TWO, not two per class

Consider the union of (i) new anonymous-parent/marked-child edges and
(ii) new marked-parent/marked-child edges which are root-exposable.
For LL, with source G(P(v(A,B),C),D), possible surviving births are:

    v->P if P marked (either type of v);
    P->G if G marked (either type of P);
    P->root(B) only if P unmarked and root(B) marked;
    G->root(C) only if G unmarked and root(C) marked.

Marked middle-hanger edges in the last two positions are INNER: global
creator root v lies below their parent in key order. Part1 excludes
them from the root-exposable class. If P marked, the first birth and
at most one of the second/fourth qualify. If P unmarked, at most two
of the second/third/fourth qualify, by the marked/unmarked type of G.
Total at most TWO. RR is the reflected argument.

For LR, each side contributes at most one: v->P if P marked, otherwise
P->root(B) if that root is marked; analogously v->G or G->root(C).
Marked middle-hanger cases are inner by the global root's order. RL
is reflected. A terminal zig has at most ONE qualifying birth. The
outside attachment to v is excluded since v is the accessed target
and has no parent at the completed creator boundary.

A non-target consumed operand's child links are rigid in later outer
frames. New direct children of the accessed target are consumed ancestor
operands. Thus surviving births are attributed to their unique original
creating StepEv. Assign its bit0/1 in canonical edge order. This gives
one JOINT globally injective raw candidate map for all the watched
H1 zero-hole classes, across arbitrary finite support/period/private
populations and both orientations. Marked and anonymous claims do not
each get a separate two-bit budget.

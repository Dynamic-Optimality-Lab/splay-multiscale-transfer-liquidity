from old auxiliary root R, with all subsequent retained accesses (before

condition that a donor/matching exists.

This guard follows in the hardest seventh-predecessor spine of C21:

auxiliary root while s:C is erased; t:G is its first change to G. All

## Ordinary-splay top-root lemma, without a maximal auxiliary core

After ANY ordinary access G whose old root was R>G, that R is either
G.right (zig/mixed final top event) or G.right.right through a middle P
(LL final top event). Earlier deeper events do not change this final
root placement. Thus the actual subsequent R,G pair uses zig/zig or
RR/LL, and returns the WHOLE auxiliary BST exactly, including all private
physical keys and hangers. Its B-double total is respectively0 or2.

The original A pair is RR/LL and returns U exactly by C21 RG21. The H1
macro word grammar retains only G/R root repeats and its one positive
access, so deleting the ENTIRE original v,p interval in an AUXILIARY
proof execution preserves both A and B_E endpoints. It removes exactly

It does not assume B_F returns: full B changes, and its original three
double demands remain obligations in the original full history.

## Disjoint collective excision

Take any finite collection L of actual such return intervals with the
SAME erased C and orientation, in one legal original period. They are
disjoint because v,p windows of distinct deep accesses are disjoint.
For ANY subset T of L, remove its intervals only from the auxiliary proof
word. Each is an exact (A,B_E) identity, so the other states/costs are
unchanged and the compressed auxiliary word remains exactly closed.

The critical-spine anchors h/g/t are outside their own v,p interval and
outside every other selected interval: they lie in their own deep-free
gap, whereas another interval lies in its different gap. G and R therefore
still have retained occurrences after excision. Every other nonerased
marked key was never removed, since these intervals contain only G,R
accesses. Hence the compressed auxiliary support is STILL six.
Here one uses the inherited closed-source fact: in a nontrivial B-closed
period every retained key occurs genuinely somewhere. Retaining the six
distinct labels therefore retains six genuine support keys, even if a
particular anchor occurrence happens to be an auxiliary root repeat.

Original diagonal reachability is preserved: use an actual reachable
boundary outside the excised intervals and omit only identity intervals
after that boundary. There is such a boundary at a retained deep e. If
an interval wraps the displayed cut, rotate the proof word to a retained
boundary, then transport BOTH endpoints back to the original surviving
occurrence IDs. No extra physical copy or neighboring-period unit is used.
The fixed fE and its original legal cut are NEVER changed. An independent
compressed-source comparison can use its own certified cut and then be

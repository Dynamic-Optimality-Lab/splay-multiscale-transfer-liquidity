# MANDATORY IAGS RECURSIVE CLOSURE AND RESOURCE-DISCOVERY DIRECTIVE

The primary research objective is to establish a mathematically valid proof or an exact source-level refutation of the frozen target.

**UNRESOLVED is a reporting status, not a proof-search strategy.**

Do not terminate merely because the first proposed sufficient lemma, donor construction, invariant, matching reduction or ownership exchange is unavailable.

## 1. Recursive resource discovery

Whenever progress is blocked, identify precisely which mathematical resource is missing.

Examples:

- A source-derived donor.
- An ownership-preserving exchange.
- A signed correction identity.
- A comparison-safe matching.
- A reachability invariant.
- A noncollision theorem.
- A universal source-lifting argument.
- A missing induction bridge.

Convert the missing resource into a new IAGS Box:

**Target — Conditions — Inheritance — Ambiguities — Exact Bridge.**

Decompose the Box into smaller independently meaningful obligations.

Continue recursively.

A decomposition is useful only if it creates a genuine reduction in mathematical difficulty, exposes new source structure, or identifies a falsifiable sufficient condition.

Do not recursively rename the original theorem.

## 2. Explore alternate sufficient mechanisms

If one proposed condition fails, investigate mathematically different routes to the same frozen theorem.

For a missing physical donor, investigate:

- Additional original A-event supply.
- Licensed nonlocal ownership reassignment.
- Collective rather than individual donor expansion.
- Source-forced cancellation or compensation.
- Alternative comparison correspondences.
- New structural invariants.
- A valid source partition into simpler classes.

Do not insist that every proof must use the first proposed donor mechanism.

**The target theorem is immutable. Its sufficient proof conditions may be replaced.**

## 3. Construct exact refutations

If a candidate lemma is false, construct an exact source-valid countercertificate.

Determine whether the witness refutes:

- Only the candidate proof mechanism.
- A proposed sufficient condition.
- A supporting source lemma.
- Or the actual frozen theorem.

Never confuse these levels.

Use the counterexample to discover a corrected sufficient condition whenever possible.

## 4. Reconstruct upward continuously

Whenever a child Box is proved, immediately propagate its conclusion to its parent.

Reassess every remaining sibling obligation.

A stronger child theorem may discharge multiple parent requirements simultaneously.

Seal proved intermediate lemmas at their legitimate mathematical and verification scopes.

Preserve all historical accepted results.

## 5. Prevent circular recursion

Every recursion must accomplish at least one of:

- Produce a strictly simpler proof obligation under a justified measure.
- Derive a new usable lemma.
- Eliminate a source configuration.
- Establish a new source-valid invariant.
- Generate an exact counterexample.
- Discover a genuinely different sufficient proof architecture.

Do not generate infinite chains of restated obligations.

## 6. Continue research while legitimately possible

Do not use UNRESOLVED as an early exit merely because the current approach is difficult.

Before reporting an unresolved mathematical obstacle, document:

1. The exact missing implication.
2. The attempted source-derived mechanisms.
3. Their proofs or counterexamples.
4. The remaining viable alternatives.
5. Why the current recursion cannot be completed under the authorized resources.
6. The smallest next mathematical Box.

Continue through other viable routes while authorized computation and verification constraints permit.

## 7. Preserve hard resource constraints

Mathematical resource discovery does not authorize increasing computational budgets.

Do not reset, bypass or extend CPU, symbolic or independent-audit caps.

An exhausted audit allowance does not prove a mathematical lemma false.

A complete mathematical derivation may be preserved as AUDIT-PENDING when independent verification is unavailable.

Do not manufacture evidence or declare a theorem independently certified without completing its required audit.

## 8. Terminal outcomes

**OUTCOME A:** The frozen mathematical theorem is proved with every required implication established. Report its exact audit status.

**OUTCOME E:** An exact valid countercertificate refutes the frozen theorem.

**UNRESOLVED:** Authorized work cannot establish either conclusion. Preserve all genuine progress and supply the smallest exact continuation obligation.

A terminal unresolved report must not be presented as a mathematical impossibility result.

## FINAL RULE

**WHEN A PROOF RESOURCE IS MISSING, FIRST INVESTIGATE HOW THE ORIGINAL MATHEMATICAL SYSTEM CAN PRODUCE IT.**

**IF THAT FAILS, DECOMPOSE THE REQUIREMENT OR CONSTRUCT A DIFFERENT SUFFICIENT MECHANISM.**

**IF THAT FAILS, INVESTIGATE AN EXACT SOURCE COUNTEREXAMPLE.**

**RECONSTRUCT UPWARD THROUGH EVERY PROVED BRIDGE.**

**DO NOT STOP AT THE FIRST UNPROVED LEMMA.**

**DO NOT FORCE A FALSE PROOF OR FALSE REFUTATION.**

## Actual operations and event location

Every zero-event A access targets its current root. If the unique positive
A access targeted anything other than y, A could not subsequently finish
rooted at y using only zero-event accesses. Thus it targets y, at actual
A-depth1/2. Before it, every access targets g. Before the terminal genuine
KEEP, every KEEP must target the unchanged BF root g. After a DELETE y
makes A root y, a KEEP g would be positive in A, and an earlier KEEP y
would already end the macro. Therefore, with Zg in {DELETE g,KEEP g}, the
COMPLETE physical word forms are

    Zg* ; KEEP y,
    Zg* ; DELETE y ; (DELETE y)* ; KEEP y.

The unique A event is at the displayed KEEP in the first form and the first
DELETE y in the second. This is not an assumption that the event occurs
at the terminal KEEP. Every original identity occurrence is retained. The
description is valid for arbitrarily long identity blocks by root identity
induction. It is also sufficient when the stated source paths/costs hold.

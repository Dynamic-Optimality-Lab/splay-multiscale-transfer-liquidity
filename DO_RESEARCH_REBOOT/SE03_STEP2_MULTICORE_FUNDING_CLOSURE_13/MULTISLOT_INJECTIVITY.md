# Arbitrary-cardinality physical nonoverlap

Core7 seed r_c->v_c is injective on all R because c->c-1 is injective
on a cyclic list, each predecessor is noncritical and original macro
occurrence inventories are disjoint. Distinct bit IDs mean different
(absolute occurrence,event ordinal,bit), not different chart descriptions.

Core8 uses a single bounded actual carry stream, starting/ending empty
at a full max-D cut. A minted own-unused unit enters once, is either
spent on one excess slot or released, and never reminted. The stream
constructs h extending h0, proving arbitrary-set seed injectivity without
assuming pairwise or general Hall. Actual counts may be arbitrarily large.

For ANY finite complete injective h and fixed partial paid injection a,
walk from all missing roots through h and inverse a. Unique predecessors
exclude entered cycles and merging paths; missing roots are outside dom(a).
Thus all walks end at distinct actual free units. Adding all root/terminal
pairs simultaneously keeps every old value. Paths need not be short.

Both source endpoints are transported by one actual-period bijection;
copies/aliases cannot create resources. A single event may supply bits0
and1, but their different bit positions are counted explicitly. Chart
changes do not relabel physical identity. Locked seeds route through
owners; a locked unit is never stolen as a terminal.

No arbitrary-core9..11 seed map is proved. Anchor distinctness alone
does not imply supply. The generic finite exchange proves no global
capacity inequality until an independent source h is supplied.

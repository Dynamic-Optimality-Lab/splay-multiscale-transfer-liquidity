# Source-exact signed interfaces at a core11 chart miss

This derives the residual comparison without assuming it. It does not
prove the missing inequality, and it is not a Step2 completion certificate.

Use RECENT_SUPPORT_COVER.md. Before the actual miss z, let d_old be the
sealed nine-Score debt in the old chart M minus {z}. Let d_new be its value
at the actual endpoint in the new chart M minus {v}. Actual A operations
and their H source StepEvs are unchanged in every comparison below.

## Two disjoint cold twigs

The new chart omitting v is already a valid source chart before the miss:
its nine Scores include the actual target z. Full splaying z is exactly
its ordinary nine-Score splay, with unchanged q. If d_alt is the debt in
that chart at the source, M3 gives, for the clipped chart price w_c,

    w_c+d_new-d_alt>=0.

Thus the actual full signed residual with the OLD source chart decomposes
as this nonnegative certificate, plus d_alt-d_old, plus the nonnegative
actual A tail2(H-3)_+. Equality of actual full source trees does not make
different charts' debts equal. That comparison remains a real obligation.

## One cold four-node chain

Perform only the FIRST actual B double of the miss, at v,u1,z. It is
same-side and internal, since v is outside the common seven-node core
and its current marked root. It preserves the full eleven-node core and
leaves the global B root unchanged. Let Btilde be this intermediate tree.
The remaining full B frames are exactly the ordinary splay of marked z
in the new nine-Score tree of Btilde; their double count is q-1.

Use the actual A SOURCE tree with Btilde to form a hypothetical ordinary
vertex in the unchanged nine-Score source relation. Its root is still
the actual common marked root. The same original A word is legal: every
nonterminal KEEP is a root repeat and every other operation is DELETE.
Omitted v is simply an anonymous key for this chart. No key is deleted.
All order gaps and populations are actual and admissible for its new
Score labels. No reachability or closed-walk IH is invoked for this
hypothetical pair; only the already universal local M3 edge theorem is used.

Write d_virtual for its debt and w_c=2min(H,3)-(q-1). The local certificate
and the exact ACTUAL cost w=2H-q satisfy

    w_c+d_new-d_virtual>=0,
    w+d_new-d_old
      = [w_c+d_new-d_virtual]
        +[d_virtual-d_old-1]+2(H-3)_+.                 (1)

The -1 is the actual first B DOUBLE, not a rounding correction or an
unpriced virtual operation. The A trajectory has not lost an event.
Discarding this -1, or identifying d_virtual with d_old, would manufacture
the missing repayment. Equation(1) explicitly preserves its sign.

## Paired q5/1 edges and the actual remaining gap

A q5 miss has its forced q1 successor from the completed source theorem.
That successor may be a chart hit OR another miss. The source partition
must retain both cases and every original A event. Their Hsum2 negative
and Hsum3 zero cases still require a signed old-to-final-chart comparison.
Summing separate charts' inequalities without paying their boundary
differences is not a proof. H1/q3, H1/q4, zero/+1 releases, and paired
q5/1 transfers at these misses are the central unproved obligations.

The core10 deficiency-one cold configurations require their own chart
comparison as well. The fixed nine-Score hit proof covers core10 hits,
including true-core9->8, but supplies no miss comparison by assertion.

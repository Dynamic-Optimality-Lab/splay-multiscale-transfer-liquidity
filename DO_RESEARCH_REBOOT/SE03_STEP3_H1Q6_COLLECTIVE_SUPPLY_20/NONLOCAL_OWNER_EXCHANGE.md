# What the new comparison edges prove about original owners

C16/C17's finite partial/full exchange is reused at its stated scope and
audit status. Maintain the literal licensed old injective paid map a,
including required common ordinals, previous successful full assignments
and the inherited E-only retirement policy. A source comparison h may
use a-owned units. Such units are not free endpoints by definition.

G20 inserts distinct fresh-image comparison edges for guarded debts and
keeps every old h_B value. This is an actual source derivation of those
escape edges, not the assumption that an available donor can be chosen.
Original owners are unchanged. Following h then inverse a from an unpaid
root either reaches a real unit outside im(a) or a still-undefined
comparison frontier. Finite injectivity excludes entered rooted cycles
and mergers. A blocked frontier is NOT an effective payment.

When the new source-class corollary makes h total, the undefined outcome
is impossible. The inherited complete-map proof yields distinct actual
free endpoints for all roots and appends only their assignments. Every
old a/common value stays literal. This supports arbitrary finite root
count/path length; no fixed lookback is assumed.

When unguarded or other holes remain, a root whose original debt was
guarded can still reach one of them through old owners. G20 therefore
does not by itself prove effective repayment of every guarded ROOT in an
otherwise incomplete source. The source-complete endpoint premise and
the original eligibility/Bank25 receipt remain essential. No producer
calculation or static matching is promoted into those missing claims.

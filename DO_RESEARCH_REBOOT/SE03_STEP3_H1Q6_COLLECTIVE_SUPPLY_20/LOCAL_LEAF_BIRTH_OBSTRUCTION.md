# Distinct births do not create enough local credit

Original A80 selected zero holes(9,2),(13,2) become unary births(45,4),
(45,2) in one H1/Q6 macro. Its only comparison tail unit is(45,1,1).
Original B82 has the corresponding(47,4),(47,2) versus(47,1,1).
The H1/Q6 macro gains5 marked B leaves but contributes only2 physical A
bits, one comparison-reserved and one comparison-unused. Two transported
demands therefore cannot both receive new local comparison values.

Effective availability is stricter still. In qualified actual A80,
frozen a uses BOTH local units (LOCAL_SPARE_LEDGER). The C19 own-tail
comparison step fills an entry already agreeing with old a. It does not
free a bit for the remaining anchor. A donor cannot be inferred from
mere presence in the source, a distinct leaf birth, or B-leaf gain.

G20 uses original predecessor A events outside the recovery, derives their
comparison-unused status AFTER all own-tail payments, and proves global
noncollision. It does not reassign a frozen effective unit locally.
The remaining saturated two-predecessor class has no such proved donor;
more distant source preparation or legal exchange remains necessary.

leaf death. Per-key alternating Boolean status pairs it to its unique next
actual unary leaf-birth demand. Distinct deaths have distinct such demands;
two descriptions of one demand are deduplicated before any payment.

and removes its old unary-demand value. The ENTIRE physical image stays
the same. It is an alternative source proof comparison before effective
exchange, not a mutation of original a/common/fE or an immutable comparison
receipt during an ongoing extension.

Group the resulting unary arrivals by their actual B-birth natural macro.
The old image occupies its first min(2H,beta+z) original units. Pay arrivals
in demand order from the FIRST available own-tail units. There are k<=u
actual arrivals and T=2H-min(2H,beta+z) such units. Remaining debt is(k-T)_+.

an actual previously comparison-unused A event bit.

two arrivals therefore leave one actual missing birth demand. G20's
incoming-chain lemma further excludes the old root's last double from

not assumptions selecting only independently fundable debts.

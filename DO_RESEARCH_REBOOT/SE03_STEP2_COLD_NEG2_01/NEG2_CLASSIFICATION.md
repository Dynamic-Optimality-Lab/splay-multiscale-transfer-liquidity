six genuine KEEP keys. Genuine means its target is not the current full
B root. Support normalization and fixed labeled marked-core constancy
are inherited, giving6<=c<=11. Every full B search path stays in this core,
so Q_single<=floor((c-1)/2)<=5.

Use natural FULL macros ending at genuine full KEEPs, not auxiliary
root-change boundaries. A starts at the preceding genuine B root g and
ends at the next genuine target x!=g. H=0 would leave A rooted at g,
contradiction. Therefore EVERY natural full macro has H>=1, even with
DELETE helpers, nongenuine KEEPs and auxiliary/full disagreement.

## Both directions and source H1 geometry

The classification depends only on the true depths and event counts,
not key direction. Each sole positive A event is an actual ZIG_L/ZIG_R,
LL,RR,LR or RL. If that event is not the terminal KEEP itself, it must
target the terminal key x; every later zero event preserves its root.
This permits an actual DELETE x followed by an A-root KEEP x. No fake
H0 genuine full macro is introduced. The q5 packet has one such event
in EACH of its two actual macros.

The original maximal core11 source theorem further forces every full B
double to be same-side with marked G and unmarked P. It is not asserted
for core10, where terminal zigs and mixed frames remain. Reflecting an
actual source preserves costs, but is not assumed to reflect M3 debt.

## Root gaps and chart changes

Every nongenuine full KEEP has qF0, but may have nonzero A cost. All are
retained inside their natural full macro. A positive A excursion returning
to the same synchronized root needs at least two events; H0 is an exact
state identity. Neither creates an extra price-2 packet. Added root-identity
operations remain actual occurrences with separate absolute timestamps.

An auxiliary qE0 repeat does NOT imply qF0. Erased B disagreement changes
neither the definition of genuine FULL macro nor its cost. A q5 target
cannot be a hit of its old five-key chart: its eleven-node ancestor path
would not fit in nine Scores. Its q1 successor may be a hit OR a further
miss; BOTH chart changes belong to the packet if present. A singleton is
cold exactly when its target is absent from the actual old recent-five set.

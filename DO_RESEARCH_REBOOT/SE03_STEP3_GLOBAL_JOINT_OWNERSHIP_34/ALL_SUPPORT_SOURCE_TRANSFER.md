# Support quantifiers and lifting

OR34 is a theorem for every ordinary A splay, any finite tree, operation
length and key order, including arbitrary anonymous ancestors/private
terms. LPR and EX-PR34 additionally require the inherited closed maximal
full core2r-1 and its all-SS/path-to-star source language. Their r>=7
quantifier is uniform; the proof does not enumerate support sizes.

The MR pool theorem is uniform over r and arbitrary A/helper populations
on its declared disjoint MR/own-solvent source partition. The already
accepted MR h0-preserving license and working2 trace remain its funding
interface. No proof says every arbitrary full B source has this partition.

The two new closed controls from core40/verify81 have seven genuine keys
but full core7. They are legitimate smaller-core histories, not invalid
BSTs. Their PR scope is OUTSIDE MAXIMAL CORE. The declared policy did
not silently invoke Q=marked-ancestor-count there. No new actual fE/a0
was supplied for either input. They are neither shortage witnesses nor
universal core7 certificates.

There is no cost-free contraction of anonymous A ancestors, no hidden
seven-key assumption in OR34, no arbitrary-support extension of fixed
six-key storage caps, and no chart replacement. Genuine B subtrees keep
their original keys; DELETE remains A-only. Reflection follows from the
actual formulas in both source lemmas, not numerical symmetry alone.

The strong I(r-1)->I(r) induction has NOT been established for all r>=7.
Maximal-core coalition compatibility and smaller-core source licenses
are separate unresolved interfaces of the same principal global theorem.

# Campaign34 source integrity

Current local branch/HEAD and cumulative resource state are frozen in
entry.json; hashes and dependency status are in dependency_manifest.json.
The current accepted controller remains the unmodified C33
CONTINUATION_01/run_phase_1440.py. New phase wrappers there launch only
campaign34 scripts, keeping every old charge and failure cumulative.
No controller or cap is replaced/reset. Metadata-only entry/publication
follows the inherited exclusion and records its CPU separately.

All new mathematical artifacts are in this C34 directory. Historical
graphs, costs, raw states, source words, owners and certificates remain
unchanged. R1–R4 keep their actual original meanings: exact source/target;
original operations, costs, sorts and physical IDs; frozen owners/contracts/
resources; and proved/conditional/refuted/open evidence distinctions.

A splays every original access. Full B processes EVERY original KEEP;
genuine means that the target differs from its current root. Nongenuine
root-repeat KEEPs are retained identities in B and may cost A events.
The directive's phrase 'genuine ... including root repeats' is resolved
using this original definition, not by changing which operations execute.
DELETE is A-only, never physical deletion. All splaying is bottom-up.

Costs are a_t=ceil(edge_depth_A/2), q_t=floor(edge_depth_B/2). Bits(t,j,b)
and demands(t,k) belong to ONE actual aligned period. All existing fE,
common owners, natural h0 and later successful effective values remain
fixed. No signed chart correction, retirement, actual recurrence or legal
Step1 cut is removed. No proof-only shadow supplies a physical bit.

The source34 tests reuse accepted source/primitive receipts rather than
replaying them without purpose. A source pool graph is explicitly a NEW
candidate counting scheme; it is not silently equated with the complete
source-license/working-bank system. No finite passing test proves JIO34.

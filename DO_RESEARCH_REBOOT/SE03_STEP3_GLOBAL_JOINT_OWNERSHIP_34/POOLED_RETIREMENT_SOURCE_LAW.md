# A source-derived pooled-retirement law and the unproved coalition bound

## Box PR34

Target: eliminate fixed first-ticket collisions by deriving ONE joint
source-capacity system for every demand, before any interior/extreme
priority is imposed. Conditions: original closed maximal full core2r-1,
r>=7, actual A/B operations, and inherited C15 marked path-to-star theorem.
Inheritance: C33 AR1 and actual cyclic event identities. Ambiguity: the

Bridge: an independently source-proved full pool allocation would be a

## Operation pools, not repeated tickets

For each original occurrence t let C_t be ALL its actual A units(t,j,b),
size2a_t. A zero-event operation has an EMPTY pool. Pools at different
original occurrences are disjoint. They are not necessarily free under a0.

For a full natural macro m, write x_m for its genuine target and U_m for
all its actual A units. Use the actual marked ancestors g of x_m in B;
there are Q_m of them at the inherited maximal-core/all-SS scope.

For each such g, start immediately AFTER its most recent genuine B KEEP
and stop after the current original A access to x_m, cyclically in the
one actual source period. At the start A is rooted at g; at the end it
is rooted at x_m. Include EVERY original A operation t in that interval
with g an ancestor of x_m BEFORE t and not an ancestor AFTER t.
Let R_m be the union of these retiring-operation occurrences over all g.
Define

    T_m = union_{t in R_m} C_t,
    N_m = U_m union T_m.                                    (PR)

This rule does NOT pick one first-removal bit. A shared retiring operation
is represented ONCE with its actual capacity2a_t, even if many marked
relations and many later targets refer to it. Current and retirement
contributions are unions of actual IDs, never summed with duplication.

## The source theorem actually proved

For EVERY macro m at this scope,

    |T_m| >= Q_m, hence |N_m| >= Q_m.                        (LPR)

Proof: for each old marked ancestor g, at least one whole A operation
removes its ancestry between its last g KEEP and current x_m. Select the
first such operation only for this proof. If several g select operation t,
they are distinct ancestors lost across t. AR1 implies that at most one
marked ancestor is lost per actual rotation, hence at most2a_t in t.
Assign those g distinct original bit ordinals in C_t. Different t have
disjoint pools. This gives an explicit per-macro injection of its Q_m
demands into T_m. No initial A shape/private-inventory bound is used.

This is a uniform source-local result including INTERIOR targets. It
does not prove that these per-macro injections coexist across macros.

## The proposed GLOBAL source theorem — OPEN

For the pooled scheme, each demand of m may use any unit of N_m at the
counting layer. Full independent pool feasibility is exactly

    for EVERY set S of full natural macros:
    |union_{m in S} N_m| >= sum_{m in S} Q_m.                (GPR)

All slots of a macro have the same pool. A deficient set of slots can be
enlarged to include every demand of each represented macro without adding
any pool neighbors, so testing all macro coalitions is exact for THIS
scheme. In particular LPR rules out single-macro obstructions. It does
not rule out a coalition of two or more macros.

GPR is a candidate SOURCE theorem, not a consequence of the matching
theorem. Its source truth is attacked separately. A failed coalition
would refute this pooled comparison rule, not the whole original source
eligibility system or JIO34.

## Exact overlap accounting

For a macro coalition S, put U_S=union U_m, T_S=union T_m and

    sigma(S)=sum_m (|T_m|-Q_m),
    overlap(S)=sum_m |T_m| - |T_S|,
    local_unused(S)=|U_S\T_S|.

Then the literal set identity is

    |union N_m|-sum Q_m = sigma-overlap+local_unused.         (OI)

The first and third terms are nonnegative, but overlap can be large.
The missing source compensation law is
`overlap <= sigma+local_unused` for ALL coalitions. Exact closure alone
has not supplied a proof. For S the whole source, OI includes the original
total-credit obstruction; writing OI is not a new proof of that bound.

Cycles/cuts use actual periodic source states and original occurrence IDs.
No neighboring symbolic copy supplies an extra pool. Nongenuine KEEPs,
DELETEs, zero-event accesses and chart transitions remain in their true
natural macro. Smaller cores lack the inherited Q=marked-ancestor identity;
LPR/GPR are not extrapolated there.

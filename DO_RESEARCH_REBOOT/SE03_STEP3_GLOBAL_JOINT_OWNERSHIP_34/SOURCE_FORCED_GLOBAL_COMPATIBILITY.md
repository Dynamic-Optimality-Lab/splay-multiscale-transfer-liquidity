# Source compatibility: what is proved and what is not

The following form ONE proposed global policy, not an expanding list of
helper repairs. PR assigns every demand of m its operation pool N_m,
with actual capacity unions and virtual a0 stays at the physical layer.

New proved interfaces are LPR (individual source capacity), OR34
(operation/rotation retirement exactness), EX-PR34 (all exterior coalitions),
the MR-partition pool theorem, and exact obstruction localization. The
proofs are in POOLED_RETIREMENT_SOURCE_LAW.md,
OPERATION_RETIREMENT_EXACTNESS.md, JOINT_INTERIOR_EXTERIOR_ALLOCATION.md
and MINIMAL_GLOBAL_OWNER_OBSTRUCTION.md. These are symbolic source or
finite-set theorems, not universal conclusions inferred from controls.

## The attempted universal bridge — NOT PROVED

For every coalition S of genuine macros in a closed maximal core, source
geometry would need to prove

    overlap(S) <= sigma(S)+local_unused(S),                 GPR

with the exact definitions in PR. This would produce a raw pool cover by
the specified finite matching algorithm. With owner stays, the weaker
physical-layer requirement adds

    |a0(D_S intersect dom(a0)) \ N(D_S)|

on the right. That term counts already-paid stays, not extra free units.

Attempt1: sum individual LPR inequalities. Failure: the sum counts
shared operations repeatedly. OR34 gives an exact retirement description
but no bound on how many different target demands refer to one operation.
The C33 collision realizes precisely that sharing.

Attempt2: union the inherited exterior injection with independent interior
LPR injections. Failure: raw images can intersect, and frozen owners can
occupy either image. Individual injection does not prove joint expansion.

Attempt3: use MR to eliminate overlap. It succeeds on a disjoint MR/
own-solvent partition by the new explicit pool allocation. No source
proof supplies such a partition for arbitrary B trajectories. Enlarging
an interval until it is solvent assumes the property under investigation.

Attempt4: infer the law from period closure. Closure provides physical
identities and cyclic births/deaths. It does not give the sign of OI.
For S all macros, N(D_S)=U, so GPR contains Q_F<=2A itself. A proof cannot
substitute that desired sign or the refuted KEEP-count bound here.

The smallest structural candidate not eliminated is a connected coalition
with at least two macros, an interior target, and an own-excess macro.
Neither its source impossibility nor a source-valid negative cut is
established. Smaller cores additionally need a source bridge replacing
Q=number of old marked ancestors. No universal funding or architecture
refutation follows from the present analysis.

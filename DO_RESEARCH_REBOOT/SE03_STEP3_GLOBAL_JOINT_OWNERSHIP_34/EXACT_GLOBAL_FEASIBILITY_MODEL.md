# Exact full model and a separate exact pooled counting scheme

## Original Gate U14 route

The exact accepted route is a proof-carrying source/construction system.
A witness consists of original E_r1 erasure/domain/actual-period evidence;
an independent ORIGINAL-SOURCE type A/B full credit license producing h
and containing natural h0; its actual selected/admitted/assigned/released
trace with working<=25 and correct closing cut; original common pairing,
E-only retirement, chart/source-block eligibility and both-endpoint
transport; and licensed h/inverse-a0 paths ending at actual free units.

Such a witness gives the required extension, and an execution of that
UNCHANGED route contains these witness fields. This is the inherited
C33 exact-model statement. No equivalence to naive static matching or
time-expanded flow has been established for all original source licenses.
An arbitrary candidate pool graph is not substituted for this system.

## The pooled scheme's physical layer

Freeze a fixed actual source and a0. Let N(d)=N_m from PR for d in m.
Use all physical units permitted in the alternative full comparison ledger;
additional unretired Gamma reservations still need their actual license.
For the counting layer of THIS fixed policy put

    J(d)=N(d) union {a0(d) if d already paid}.

The second edge is a VIRTUAL STAY, not permission to move the actual
owner or to call that occupied unit free. Then the following are equivalent:

1. There are disjoint pool/inverse-a0 augmenting paths for all unpaid roots.
2. J has a matching covering every full demand.
3. For all X subset D,
   |N(X)\a0(X intersect dom a0)| >= |X\dom a0|.              (OP)

Proof: a covering matching compared with a0 decomposes into alternating
paths, stays and cycles. The paths starting at unpaid roots end at distinct
units outside im a0. Every non-stay matching edge belongs to N. Append
ONLY root-to-terminal pairs, retaining every original a0 edge. Conversely
such disjoint paths toggle the virtual matching to cover all D. The finite
matching argument gives3; subtract the distinct paid stay images to obtain
OP. Cycles can be discarded and supply no free unit.

This relation includes occupied intermediate bits, so it does not exclude
legitimate pool-owner relays by deleting im a0 from every comparison edge.
It gives actual free endpoints after the paths. It does not declare a
pool comparison to have a complete original source/Bank25 license.

## Macro-complete obstruction theorem

Any deficient X in OP can be enlarged to the union D_S of ALL demand slots
of the macros represented in X, without reducing its Hall deficiency.
Adding a demand from an already represented macro adds no pool neighbor.
An unpaid one adds no stay; a paid one adds at most one new stay and one
left vertex. Therefore deficiency cannot decrease. Exact feasibility of
this physical layer is equivalent to

    for all macro coalitions S:
    |N(D_S) union a0(D_S intersect dom a0)| >= |D_S|.        (MOP)

By LPR, no single macro can violate this. Minimal pooled obstructions
therefore involve at least two genuine macro inventories, including their
actual owner stays. A minimal SLOT obstruction has deficiency exactly1;
a macro-complete/minimal-macro obstruction need not have deficiency1.

Using OI, the deficit margin in MOP is exactly

    sigma-overlap+local_unused
       + |a0(D_S intersect dom a0)\N(D_S)|.

The last term counts already-paid stays in the alternative comparison;
it is NOT a supply of new free tokens. These are uniform set/matching
theorems. A SOURCE proof that every coalition has nonnegative margin is
still required, as are the original full license and working trace.

## Principal distinction

Even a successful owner-dependent J matching may leave no independent
cover using N alone. Thus it may prove only a physical extension, not
E_r2. Matching also supplies no unconditional Bank25 trace. The complete
original proof system above remains the exact JIO34 parent interface.

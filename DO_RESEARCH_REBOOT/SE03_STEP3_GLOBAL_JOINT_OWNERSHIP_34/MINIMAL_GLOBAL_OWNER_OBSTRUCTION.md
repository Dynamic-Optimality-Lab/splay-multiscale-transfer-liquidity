## Structural localization of every hypothetical PR obstruction

Let S be an inclusion-minimal macro-complete deficient coalition under
MOP. Then:

1. S has at least two macros (LPR individually funds every macro).
2. Some m in S has Qm>2Hm. Otherwise disjoint own inventories alone
   cover S, even before adding retirement pools or old-owner stays.
3. The overlap graph of S is connected, where two vertices are adjacent
   exactly when their FULL candidate neighborhoods (including stays)
   intersect. If disconnected, its Hall deficits add by components;
   a deficient component contradicts inclusion-minimality.
4. At least one macro in S has an interior target. OR34/EX-PR34 gives
   an injection for every exterior-only coalition; stays only enlarge it.
5. Its exact margin is

       sigma-overlap+local_unused+owner_stays_outside_N < 0.

All five statements are uniform NECESSARY conditions. They do not prove
that a coalition with these properties is source-realizable. None of the
accepted controls produced one. In particular they are not a CD witness.

A minimal SLOT obstruction has deficiency exactly1: delete one demand,
apply minimality and neighborhood inclusion, obtaining |J(X)|>=|X|-1.
A macro-complete obstruction can have larger deficiency. The algorithm
must not label its provisional matching owners as the original frozen a0.

## Source translation of a putative cut

For each d=(t,k) in X, recover the actual full KEEP and double ordinal,
its natural macro, and its current a0 value if any. Each pool edge is
either a physical unit in that macro or a physical unit in an operation
whose endpoint snapshots remove a specific old marked B ancestor's A
ancestry of the target after the last genuine KEEP of that ancestor.

literal old demand/unit pair. The finite alternating cut can therefore

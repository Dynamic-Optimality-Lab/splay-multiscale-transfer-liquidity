# Eligibility audit — original counting universe versus stronger licenses

The original Pair Access specification U4_02_ORIGINAL_PAIR_ACCESS_SPEC
defines CD-U as Q_B<=2A on actual reachable paired-closed source walks.
Its two A-bit counting inventory has no per-demand key compatibility,
chosen-comparison-image restriction or arbitrary-seam deadline. C15's
SOURCE_SPEC explicitly retains cyclic/block counting without a terminal-
key matching rule or new chronology condition. These are mathematical
counting units, not a redefinition of the native graph matching model.

At this PURE STATIC COUNTING level the source-derived relation is

    E_count(p)=U(I) for every full demand p.

Actual membership in U still requires its original occurrence/event/bit
and the ONE legal period. Removing effective im(a) is legitimate;
removing every auxiliary/comparison image as though all were simultaneous
physical full owners is not. Auxiliary-only demands retire only in the
alternative full ledger; archived f5/f6 maps stay unchanged.

The campaign's actual F(W) is STRONGER: it additionally preserves its
particular comparison/source-block/period/working Bank25 contracts. Any
extra target eligibility or reservation must come from the actual
required receipt, not be defined as 'the units my current partial h
happens to use'. Bank25 is also a global schedule condition, not in
general expressible by a static per-demand neighborhood alone.

For every proposed donor record:

* actual unit(tau,j,b), its source birth and period membership;
* auxiliary raw h6 retention/payment and normalized f6 inverse owner;
* full common l6 and actual effective a inverse owner;
* original target source eligibility and binding reservation if any;
* comparison image membership separately from physical occupancy;
* required cyclic cut and physical schedule receipt, if claimed.

For the disputed R-v bit, RV26-O proves it is already full a0-owned:
either common or local v-second. The bit itself is not a new free donor.
An independent comparison may USE an occupied bit only to construct an
owner path; a new effective payment requires its eligible free terminal.

No complete stronger eligibility/schedule certificate for EVERY legitimate
two-arrival source has been constructed. Uniform E_count is not silently
substituted for that missing full F(W) contract. Conversely no hypothetical
target-only-u1 restriction is attributed to original CD just because an
abstract matching example has it. The distinction is essential to the
architecture test and prevents a local pool from posing as the universe.

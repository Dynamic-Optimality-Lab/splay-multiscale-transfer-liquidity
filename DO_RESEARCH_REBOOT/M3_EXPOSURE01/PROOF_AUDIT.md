## Universal lemma established, with its actual quantifiers

For any proper private port-bearing prefix T, let C erase every subtree
with empty Score support (support includes PRIVATE port inventories).
For any present target z whose initial subtree has nonempty Score support,

    C(Splay_z(T)) = C(Splay_z(C(T))).

Target depth and hence A charge are preserved; B's unchanged word retains
its actual q and signed weights. This holds for arbitrary anonymous counts
and repeated Score-bearing accesses. ERASURE_LEMMA.md proves support
preservation/idempotence, both rotation orientations, context congruence
and the deterministic access-path induction. The independent symbolic
auditor verifies the universal local identities with arbitrary subtree
variables. It instantiates NO population/tree/label assignments and reads
NO potential labels. Its scope is equational term algebra under explicit
path-support premises, not formal verification of the entire original
compiler or the complete M3 theorem.

The important postcondition is conditional pruning: an old parent can
become Score-empty after rotation. The proof's smart constructor erases
it on BOTH sides. Assuming it remains Score-bearing would be incorrect.

## Why this is not the required public invariant

The original state contains(mu,b,alpha7,sigma). C reads private Score
allocations that this state does not contain. The already certified
GAPMULTI01 pair has the SAME complete shared source and destination
and sigma, but hidden port Score sets (empty,{0,1}) versus({0},{1}).
Our C retains a nonempty port's Score data and collapses an empty port.
Thus those PRIVATE projected objects differ at the same shared state.
No new counterexample experiment is needed or was performed.

This establishes that C ITSELF cannot be treated as a shared observable.
It does not rule out a potential f(C(T)) that is constant on all compatible
private projections. Proving precisely that insensitivity, together with
signed inequalities, is the essential missing descent theorem. We have
not established it for the total-five functions or arbitrary signatures.

Taking a max/min over compatible private projections does not automatically
solve this. A source extremizer can differ from the private witness of the
current edge; a destination extremizer can differ as well. There is no
proved coupling transporting the two extrema. Replacing that coupling by
"a common valid potential exists" would restate the open feasibility burden.
Independent private witnesses on successive original edges cannot be made
globally persistent to justify telescoping; that would change R2/R3.

The projection lemma also does not simulate an access to an anonymous
target whose own subtree is Score-empty: it erases the target. Relevant
helper accounting must therefore be reconciled with the proposed projected
potential. We do not omit such helpers, assert an unproved cheap-edge bound
or replace actual preterminal debt by macro-entry debt.

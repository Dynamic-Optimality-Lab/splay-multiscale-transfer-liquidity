# Correction: frozen R1-R4 and the research-quality rules are separate

Some new campaign notes abbreviate the one-prompt stop as "R3" and label
the four pre-execution research-quality gates "R1-R4". That naming is
incorrect. It does not change their mathematical arguments, but the
authoritative rules must be distinguished explicitly.

The ORIGINAL FROZEN contract is:

* R1: exact mathematical target; no narrowed target or altered closure.
* R2: exact weighted product/graph semantics and full witness scopes.
* R3: frozen architecture, without new coordinates/observables/solver,
  graph modification or unapproved experimental pivot/cap increase.
* R4: evidence discipline; no premature promotion or UNKNOWN-as-PASS.

The SEPARATE permanent research-quality rules are in
../RESEARCH_RULES.md. Its rule3, "One-prompt scope", says:

> If a second independent open theorem is necessary, stop.

That is the stop used here. It is not the definition of frozen R3.
The PRE_EXECUTION.md four numbered research gates refer to this second
set. Every campaign "R3 stop" / R3_stop_reason reference must be read as
RESEARCH-QUALITY RULE3. The original frozen R1-R4 contract remains fully
in force. The target is still complete original Step4/M3; the partial
frontier theorem is a lemma toward that target, not a narrowed replacement
or a claim that the requested completion has been achieved.

Authority: ../RESEARCH_RULES.md and the user's original reconstruction
directive, section5 (frozen R1-R4), section25 (supplementary four research-
quality rules). This correction is append-only; all earlier artifacts

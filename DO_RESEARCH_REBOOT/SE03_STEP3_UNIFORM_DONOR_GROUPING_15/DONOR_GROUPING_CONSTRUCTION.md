# Source grouping, raw donor ownership and its limit

Canonical candidate group G_i starts just after the preceding ACTUAL deep/
q1 pair end and ends at the current pair end. This is a cyclic partition
of original natural macros, not a synthetic B transition or deletion of
intervening operations. Each group retains actual A/B/BE endpoints, all
H,Q,qE costs, current smaller-support locks and support/period identities.

Assign every raw deep double to its marked-grandparent leaf-birth channel.
For each marked key, its immediately preceding actual leaf-death macro
uniquely owns the channel's original first A bit. Record original demand
and donor (t,j,b), macro/group indices and whether that arc crosses the
chosen seam. The q1 successor uses its own nonreserved branch bit. This
is a uniform SOURCE rule and all raw deep demands are represented.

Groups may share channel evidence but no channel donor: a birth/key owns
one demand, cyclic key-lifecycle pairing owns one death, and a death macro
reserves one unit only. The global proof covers arbitrary group families,
not pairwise testing. Raw channels need not be AVAILABLE under fE/a.

Run the exact partial owner augmentation. A group root either acquires a
distinct free terminal or is assigned a distinct explicit branch frontier.
The latter is retained as an OPEN obligation, never silently called funded.
It can belong to a different group or to an ordinary already-paid demand.

For the explicit repeated-block family, donor group j additionally owns
the first<=4 units of its ACTUAL final reset. Those reset events are disjoint
over ALL blocks; their supplier macro can overlap the next block in evidence
only. The full family map and fixed-owner extension are proved separately.
An arbitrary history need not contain such a reset. No universal reset
availability or desired matching is assumed in the principal theorem.

# Complete parameterized checkpoint deep domain

Normalize nonempty closed genuine support M; all KEEP targets are in M.
Full-core source monotonicity and closure fix S by label, with r<=|S|<=2r-1.
A B charge q=r-1 needs depth>=2r-2 and hence |S|=2r-1 and exact depth2r-2.
Conversely that depth has q=r-1. Thus this is the checkpoint's maximal
depth class, not an invented cutoff for 'large' q.

Its full source path contains every S node. Density forces alternating
marked/unmarked extreme-removal pairs, each LL/RR, ending at a marked leaf.
For a target m_k, the resulting actual B core is the inherited two-sided
fan. Paths to other marks m_i are L R^(i-1) L for i<k and R L^(r-i) R
for i>k. Only an extreme nonroot mark has even depth/allowed same-side
outer pair in the actual closed maximal core. The next genuine KEEP is
therefore an ACTUAL extreme q1. It includes all intervening DELETEs,
nongenuine KEEPs, zero accesses and real chart/erasure behavior in Hnext.

Pair those two genuine natural macros, disjointly including the seam.
Their original Q is r, H=Hfirst+Hnext>=2. If2H>=r their RAW packet capacity
suffices; if2H<r the raw deficit is r-2H. Original natural h0 may leave
more slots than this net deficit (the successor's own spare is not erased).
All H splits, orientations, arbitrary A shadows/private helpers and chart
changes remain. No reverse realization of arbitrary symbolic edges is used.

UNIFORM_LEAF_CHANNEL_THEOREM proves path-to-star, leaf counts1 -> r-1 ->
r-2 and at least r-2 intervening genuine accesses before the next deep
occurrence. There is no assumption of bounded run length/group count.
Between deep pairs arbitrary ordinary source macros are allowed, and may
carry branch-excess deficits. Erasure changes their B states/costs and
need not preserve the deep sequence; both actual executions are retained.

No maximum-depth packets exist in smaller full cores. This does not fund
those cores or their lesser-depth deficits. If there is no deep packet,
the deep-family target is vacuous but the full uniform transfer is not.

and at most three tagged roles for a fixed original A-entry/B-decrease pair. It does not give distinct available A units for U.

Identify the actual physical events and their multiplicities. Distinguish counted work from availability under an existing comparison.

Do not turn pair injectivity into individual-unit injectivity, count role tags as extra decrease slots, assume a selected positive head is free, or claim the desired residual-capacity inequality as a new lemma.

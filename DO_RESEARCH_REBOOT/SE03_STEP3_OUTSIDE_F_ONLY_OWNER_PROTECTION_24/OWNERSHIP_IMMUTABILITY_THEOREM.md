# O24-I — what can change, and what cannot

Status: manuscript derivation of the recovered contract; audit pending.

The C15 FROZEN_OWNER_EXCHANGE, C19 FROZEN_OWNER_PRESERVATION, C21
EXTREME_OWNER_EXCHANGE, C22 PAIRED_LEDGER_FUNDING and C23 physical
transport all leave EVERY earlier successful effective value literal.
Thus O_fixed is the graph of the supplied a, together with independently
required fixed common values (already in a when a extends l). No theorem
in these inspected records licenses moving an old effective F-only value.
O_movable for previously successful effective a entries is EMPTY under
this recovered contract. If a future policy supplies another license it
must be an additional explicit theorem, not an inferred convenience.

THEOREM. Every injective extension a' of a has

    im(a' restricted to new demands) subset U\im(a).

Proof: if a'(p)=a(d) for p new and d old, injectivity and extension force
p=d, contradiction. Conversely any injective new map into U\im(a) can
be appended without changing old values, PROVIDED its source eligibility
and period/storage contract have been established. Set disjointness alone
does not prove that last source requirement.

Changing independent h/phi before its use is allowed where its source
construction permits. T19 is such an alternative comparison, with the
SAME bit image as its input. It neither releases an effective bit nor
authorizes modifying a separately fixed comparison during an ongoing
extension. C23 psi maps shadow cost units to actual original bits; it is
also not an effective-owner reassignment.

The inherited h followed by inverse-a walk is NON-DISPLACING. If a(q)=u,
the walk can consult h(q), but q still owns u. Only its eventual actual
free terminal can be given to the initiating unpaid root. A walk ending
at an undefined comparison demand gives NO extension. The word 'exchange'
in those records must not be interpreted as shifting immutable values
along the path. This distinction rules out the proposed swap p->u,q->v
whenever q->u was frozen, even if v is otherwise suitable.

Already paid creator/first-own-tail full assignments are included in a
with no special mobility exception. A comparison-only commitment constrains
that proof comparison; it is a physical occupied unit only when an actual
effective or separately binding physical reservation receipt says so.
Do not remove every comparison image from U as though it were a second
physical allocation, or declare comparison-unused units free under a.

# One original period for source units, owners and proof paths

Status: manuscript source-transport theorem; audit pending.

Use a single legally aligned original I for A/B_F/B_E and supplied fE/a.
Every donor keeps its absolute occurrence t, event j and bit b. Every
demand keeps its KEEP occurrence kappa and double ordinal k. They are
not replaced by a block-local clock unless a bijection carries BOTH
endpoints back to I. A's positive tau can be an earlier DELETE; assigning
its donor identity to kappa instead would change the physical bit.

C23's corrected comparison may use a different valid cut. Compose its
source demand/unit lift with psi FIRST, producing actual original IDs.
Only then take fE/a inverses. Those inverses refer to the fixed maps on
I, not their copies at t plus/minus |I|. The resulting finite entry and
root walks are relations between actual units/demands in I, so all their
terminals remain in I. Reflection and a legal cyclic reindexing preserve
the maps when all endpoints and block memberships are carried together.

A crossing channel can use a source-valid later occurrence only under
the inherited static cyclic/block license. No new terminal-causality
restriction is introduced; no license to borrow an event from another
copy is inferred. An old paid value crossing a displayed seam remains
one fixed value, not two owners. All KEEP/DELETE root repeats remain
in the actual history even if they create zero events.

This theorem preserves signed original qF-qE per occurrence, E-only
retirement only in an alternative full ledger, and the extra shadow
event's two-bit debit. It does not reset the legal Step1 cut, extend a
fixed map to another recurrence interval, or give a new full Bank25
execution schedule. Those are separate inherited/remaining requirements.

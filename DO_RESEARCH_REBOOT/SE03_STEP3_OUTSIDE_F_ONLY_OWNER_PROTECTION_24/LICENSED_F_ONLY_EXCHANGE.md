# Non-displacing augmentation and its exact failure boundary

All old effective values are immutable by O24-I. Therefore a swap which
changes a paid F-only donor is forbidden, including at a creator payment,
an own-tail payment or an inconvenient exterior interceptor.

The valid inherited augmentation uses an independent source comparison
and consults inverse a. GLOBAL_OWNER_FRONTIER_THEOREM constructs one
such H_X and proves finite free termination OR its exact paid frontier
type. Only a source-eligible free terminal can fund the initiating root.
No intermediate a value moves. A new independent comparison edge can
repair a hole if its actual unit is outside the ENTIRE comparison image
and the source/license proves its eligibility; it need not be a-free.
If it is a-owned, continuing the path can reach another undefined demand.
The old root remains unpaid until a genuinely free terminal is obtained.

## Complete conditional theorem, not assumed endpoint existence

If H_X is total on every owner reachable from the covered unpaid roots,
then every such path terminates free. The proof is finite injectivity,
not a Hall/cardinality premise. Distinct roots get distinct endpoints,
and appending them preserves all frozen values. The corrected-R/internal
receipt subclass in GLOBAL_OWNER_FRONTIER_THEOREM proves this closure
from its explicit source-domain condition, so it is a complete smaller
funding theorem. General exterior closure is NOT proved by finiteness.

The comparison-origin receipt of an outside paid demand can be inspected,
but cannot automatically be pasted into H_X. Its old edge can collide
with im(H_X), require a separately frozen comparison choice or lead to
another uncovered frontier. Replaying a receipt without proving fresh
image/source compatibility is not a licensed repair. This is the exact
unclosed branch for OWNER24, not a failure of the smaller finite-path
theorem or of Coarse-Double.

particular constructed pool can be saturated under actual immutable

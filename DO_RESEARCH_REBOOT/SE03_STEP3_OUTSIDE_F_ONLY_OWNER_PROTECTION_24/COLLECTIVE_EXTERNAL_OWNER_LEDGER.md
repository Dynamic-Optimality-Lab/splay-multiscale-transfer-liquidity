# Arbitrary finite collection: one source comparison, one physical inventory

Status: manuscript composition theorem for the specified maps/ledger;
audit pending. This is not universal exterior-owner frontier closure.

1. Deduplicate every original occurrence, demand ID and unit ID. Select
   at most one source-excision option per actual overlapping window.
   Use the inherited disjoint deep windows for the selected inner blocks;
   retain other/outer components exactly. Repeated isomorphic trees at
   different source occurrences remain different physical inventories.
2. Apply identity excisions and C23 corrected excisions in their actual
   source order. Each selected window has proved equal A/B_E endpoints,
   either immediately or at its catcher. Induction proves whole-source
   closure/support/physical unit transport for any finite mixed collection.
3. Obtain ONE phi for that union and run inverse-fE entry paths from the
   UNION V. Paths are disjoint, even across source block boundaries.
   Hence R and B are single distinct-terminal sets. Independently chosen
   singleton comparisons could produce overlapping endpoint lists; their
   separate counts cannot simply be added.
4. Evaluate actual a inverse on that single R. One outside paid demand
   has one physical value; it can be counted at most once. An owner which
   is outside one block but charged in another is internal to the UNION's
   demand ledger. Recompute x_in,x_out,p for that union; do not sum
   singleton 'outside' classifications and double-charge cross-block owners.
5. Apply the exact O24 identity on the resulting sets. Its proof contains
   no multiplicity bound or pairwise-only assumption. All corrections,
   old values and repair units remain counted once.

Shared boundary macros and shared ancestors are not new credits. A bit
belonging to several descriptions has one ID. Source-overlapping blocks
must be retained or resolved into the permitted disjoint option; no new
neutral union theorem is inferred from their overlapping names. A union
of ordinary atomic records is always a valid ledger, but not automatically
a new legal excision. These are distinct operations.

For the full comparison H_X, different covered unpaid roots have distinct
free terminals OR distinct paid missing-frontier IDs in the same snapshot.
Changing an independent comparison to repair one frontier can change
other paths; it needs a fresh global injectivity/eligibility proof. No
independent successful singleton repair implies collective success.
Frozen a itself is never changed except by appending eligible free-endpoint
payments. Long chains, mixed block types and cyclic crossings require no
new bounded lookback in these static statements.

Everything here is uniform over arbitrary finite source length/collection
size and permitted private trees, with reflected source/maps transported
together. It supplies exact simultaneous accounting. A theorem ensuring
all the remaining exterior frontiers have compatible simultaneous escapes
is still missing and must not be inferred from this composition result.

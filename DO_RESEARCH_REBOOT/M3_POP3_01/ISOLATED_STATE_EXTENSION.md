# Totalization outside incident production states

Every original LOW/HIGH endpoint and high M midpoint satisfies the original
full B-tree predicate. All9 cores are present and connected to its root;
endpoint gaps are childless. Erasing non-real side refs and childless gaps
therefore gives a valid ordered9-core tree. All incident vertices also have
a legal five-bit mask. These are source-imposed facts, not new constraints.

At typed vertices with an invalid B projection or invalid mask, there is
no incident original production leg. Extend J by0 there. Otherwise use the
stated table/debt default. This makes W a total bounded potential on the
covered typed sectors without changing any edge inequality. The combined
decoder checks SIGNATURE COVERAGE FIRST and rejects uncovered valid
signatures; isolation is never used to silently accept an open M3 sector.

Static signature dispatch is legitimate because every original leg conserves
mu and sigma. The old single-gap, unit-pair and new population3 domains are
disjoint except for explicitly excluded subcases. They require no cross-
sector compatibility. Shared state coordinates/sorts and every original
closure/midpoint equality remain frozen. At most one table decoder is
resident; no old finite matrix or production predicate is replayed when
checking the new dispatch against saved values.

Use the immutable frozen obligation and C20's actual maximal-core marked
path-to-star theorem. G,R,C are marked B ancestors ROOT-first, whereas
G<x<C<R is key order. G is the least mark, R the greatest, C the next

## Exhaustive B predecessor split at this inherited scope

The G20 guard supplies p or v unless

    p:H1,Q1,u0,beta1,z1;
    v:H1,Q2,u1,beta1,z1.

After v, B is R with children G (leaf) and C->K->...->x (chain).
Before v its two old marked ancestors are G (unary) and C (branching).
There are exactly two ancestor orders:

    unary:  G -> C, with C children R(leaf), K->...->x;
    branch: C has children G->R(leaf), K->...->x.

Root degree Q+c forces the immediately preceding t:

    unary: Q1,u0,beta1,z1;
    branch: Q1,z0 OR Q2,u0,beta2,z1.

The Q1,z0 branch case has strict surplus. Any t with H>=2 in the remaining
small-Q cases has strict surplus. These are comparison donors under C20's
post-ALL-own-tail rule. Only the displayed H1 saturations remain below.

## Necessary A grammar — no global realization asserted from syntax

Write x0=x(B0,B1). Ordinary A formulas, H_v=H_p=H_e=1 and x<C force

    after v: R(C(G(A,x0),D),E),
    after p: U=G(A,C(x0,R(D,E))),
    after e: x(G(A,B0),C(B1,R(D,E))).

Proof in the remaining unary H_t=1 case: t starts at C and targets G.
Its new G.right is C (zig/LR), or a middle P<C with P.right=C (LL).
The latter puts R>C at least3 deep and contradicts H_v=1. Thus G.right=C,
and R must be C.right for the next R access to have one event. The R,G
pair is consequently RR/LL. Keeping x within2 edges forces C.left=x.
From old root C, an RR access R leaves C two levels below R, making G at
least3 deep. Therefore it is zig or RL; p must be LL through C,G and
its C.left/G.right operand must be x itself. This is the same post-v form.

In the UNARY saturation, H_t=1 with old root C and target G. Inverting
the single zig or LR that yields U gives exactly

    V1=C(G(A,x0),R(D,E));
    V2=C(P(A0,G(A1,x0)),R(D,E)), P<G.

LL cannot yield U: it would leave an additional right child below G
before the old C. V2's P is necessarily anonymous. These V1,V2 are the
entire family V used in the new exposure lemma.

In the BRANCH saturation, the pre-v tree is exactly

    W1=C(G(A,x0),R(D,E));
    W2=C(G(A,x0),P(R(D,E),F)), P>R.

These are the zig/RL preimages of the post-v tree. Inverting H_t=1 access
C will be done in EXTREME_NONLOCAL_SUPPLY. The source does not permit us
to erase the possible anonymous P>R in W2.

## Original operations and scope

Each H1 macro has one changing access to its final genuine target, possibly
an earlier DELETE. Before it all operations target the old A root; after
it all operations target the new A root. Every original root-repeat KEEP
remains in the actual erased execution. No operation/chart is discarded.
The displayed terms are actual A frames with arbitrary ordered, globally
consistent hangers; private descendants may be marked as permitted by
their intervals. In particular K remains in the operand containing x.

Every source-valid recovery in the frozen scope is in a guard, the unary
V family or branch W family by these actual source identities. This does
not assert every formal frame has a reachable paired-closed realization.
Global source reachability/closure and actual owner receipts remain
hypotheses. Order reversal swaps children and proves the reflected family.

The literal H1/Q6 deep case has r7. The same grammar is source-uniform
for Q=r-1, maximal core2r-1, r>=7, exactly two selected arrivals. It does
not classify nondeep fixed-Q6 cases or larger arrival loads.

# J21 — arbitrary repeated/shared comparison supply without collisions

Status: mathematical derivation complete, independent audit pending.
Use the fixed post-ALL-own-tail h_B, and E21-COMP's deterministic source
rule. Recovery descriptions referring to the SAME actual macro are grouped;
the two arrivals are distinct birth demands, not duplicate descriptions.
There is ONE remaining demand d_e per qualifying recovery after own payment.

## 1. Why seven-window noncollision does NOT follow from spacing alone

C15 gives at least r-2 genuine macros between deep accesses. At r7 this
is five intervening macros, enough for C20's five-predecessor windows but
NOT for two arbitrary seven-predecessor windows. A proof which replaced
five by seven in that old spacing sentence would be invalid.

The new source rule has stronger information. Every macro between its
chosen supplier and its recovery, supplier included and recovery excluded,
has Q in {1,2,3}:

| Last remaining unary case, backward from e | Q |
|---|---:|
| p | 1 |
| v | 2 |
| t | 1 |
| s | 3 |
| g | 1 |
| h | 2 |
| j (chosen supplier) | 1 |

Earlier supplier cases truncate this list or use the branch list
p1,v2,t2,s1-or2,g1. All are NONDEEP: r-1>=6. No actual deep access can
lie anywhere in a selected supplier-to-recovery interval.

## 2. Deep-free cyclic gap theorem

List ALL actual deep accesses of the original finite period, not merely
the ones with the selected two arrivals or the extreme order. They divide
the genuine macros into disjoint cyclic gaps. Assign to e the open gap
after its preceding deep access and before e. Every E21 supplier interval
is contained in that gap by part1. If there is only one deep occurrence,
its gap is the rest of this ONE actual period.

The recursive list cannot silently reuse a cyclic occurrence: if it wrapped
through e before reaching its supplier, that same occurrence would have
both Q>=6 and Q<=3. Thus all predecessor occurrences used here are actual
distinct macros of I. No neighboring symbolic copy supplies another bit.

Distinct recoveries have disjoint such gaps. Therefore their supplier
macros and physical LAST bits are distinct. This establishes joint supply
for an ARBITRARY finite number of repeated extreme recoveries without any
assumption about the number, distance, creator ancestry or shape of A.

## 3. Shared source data

Two arrivals at one recovery are handled together: the specified own-tail
step pays the first and E21 supplies the one remaining demand. Three or
more selected arrivals are outside this exact theorem and are not split
into overlapping pairs. Distinct recoveries cannot share a selected birth
demand, by its original (t,k) identity. Earlier zero deaths are distinct
through C19's per-key consecutive death/birth bijection.

Recoveries may share creator ancestry, charts, comparison ownership chains
or even equal SHAPES. None of that identifies physical source occurrences.
Their suppliers lie in disjoint actual macro inventories. Shared symbolic
resets do not become donors. The original R,G events at different times
are different physical units even if they restore the same whole A tree.

The rule does not inspect a's current inverse and never changes h_B values.
Adding earlier E21 donors cannot change the comparison eligibility of a
later supplier: every new bit lies in a different physical macro. This
proves arbitrary-subset capacity directly by an explicit injection, not
by assuming Hall's inequality or pairwise fundability.

Effective-owner paths can still interact with OTHER unresolved comparison
holes. Their termination is a separate bridge, in EXTREME_OWNER_EXCHANGE.
The theorem here proves global noncollision of all constructed comparison
bits, not global effective freedom or arbitrary Bank25 execution.

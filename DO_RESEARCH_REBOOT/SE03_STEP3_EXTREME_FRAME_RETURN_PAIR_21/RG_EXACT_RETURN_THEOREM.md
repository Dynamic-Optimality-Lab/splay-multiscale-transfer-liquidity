    U=G(A,C(x(B0,B1),R(D,E))).

## What returns, and what does not follow

In full marked B, before v the root is G or C, while after p it is the
chain G->R->C->...->x. Thus full B does not return with A in this frame.
The marked-leaf state and demand history change. The erased B and charts
are actual executions and may change even at root-repeat KEEPs. Neither
their return nor vanishing signed corrections is inferred. Original frozen
owners remain literal; no physical event is retired because the tree returns.

Arbitrary root-repeat operations within the H1 word forms preserve this
A identity. Repeating the two positive A accesses R,G restores U each
time as an A statement; each repetition has different occurrence/event IDs.
It does not prove repeated copies have the same B costs, selected debt
provenance, recent chart, frozen owners or a paired-closed embedding.

Reflection gives the opposite orientation exactly. Arbitrary private BST
populations in these operands are restored, including all their depths.
Arbitrary interleaved zero-weight transitions do NOT follow: weight zero
does not mean A identity. Only proven root-repeat refinements can be
inserted without recalculating this formula. This is a symbolic original
rotation witness, not a newly executed globally closed source receipt.

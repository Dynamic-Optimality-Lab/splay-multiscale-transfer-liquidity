DS35. Both labels are proper, contain the target x, and are strictly
distinct along the source path: every path vertex is itself in S.

## Exact private-population interface

Pruning S-free A hangers and contracting outside-S unary A vertices
preserves the SET J_S at a boundary: hangers contribute no nonempty label,
and unary contraction removes only a duplicate. Outside-S branchers must
be retained. This is a statement about the boundary label SET, NOT about
erasing actual helper operations, costs, exposed nodes or rotation bits.
First-loss witnesses always use the ORIGINAL full A rotation sequence.
The theorem therefore survives arbitrary private populations and newly
exposed anonymous helpers without a finite inventory cap.

The labels are intervals/subsets of the fixed source carrier S, not a
vector enumerating private inventories. Their number per macro is2q,
with q<=r-1. Dependence on the actual S-shadow geometry is retained.
No scalar potential formula or signed global preservation is asserted.

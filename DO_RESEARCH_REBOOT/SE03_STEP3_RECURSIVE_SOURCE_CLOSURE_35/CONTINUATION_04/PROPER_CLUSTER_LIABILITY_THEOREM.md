## Lemma 2 — h0-preserving injection of unpaid claims into missing labels

Let h0_e be the original own map assigning the first min(q,2H) demands
to the first min(q,2H) units of U. There is an EXPLICIT injection

    rho: (D_e minus dom h0_e) -> {Ci missing from J_M(A_start)}.

All h0_e values and all previously supplied physical owners stay literal.

Proof. If q<=2H there is no unpaid claim. Otherwise h0_e exhausts EVERY
unit of U. For each d in P define

    psi(d)=h0_e^(-1)(lambda(d)).

This is an injective partial map from P into dom h0_e, by Lemma1 and
injectivity of h0_e. Begin at an unpaid demand d0. If it is in N, output
its label. If it is in P, follow psi; continue while the current demand
is in P, and output the label of the first demand in N.

This walk must terminate. It cannot revisit d0, which is outside the
range of psi. It cannot enter any other cycle, since the entry would
then have two distinct incoming psi edges, contradicting injectivity.
The demand set is finite. Thus a terminal demand in N exists.

Walks from distinct unpaid starts are disjoint: a merge would contradict
injectivity by tracing backward, or would put an unpaid start into the
range of psi. Their terminal demands and hence their distinct Ci labels
are different. This is the required injection, derived from actual source
rotations and the already fixed h0, rather than postulated from cardinality.
No physical donor assignment is changed. QED.

An output label can belong to a demand already paid by h0. That does NOT
free its bit or fund the incoming liability. rho records which missing
cluster carries that liability. Labels are not a second credit inventory.

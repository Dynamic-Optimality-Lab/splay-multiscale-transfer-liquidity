# RT35 — exact balanced rank tokens on the original source

Status: symbolic uniform source lemma; physical FUNDING bridge OPEN.
This is a new sufficient-proof interface, not a replacement of JR35.

## Box

Target: preserve arbitrary original A depths and private populations while
describing each rank change by a bounded number of ordered intervals.
Conditions: the original finite physical key set K and full actual A/B
trees; rank rho(k)=depth_A(k) for every PHYSICAL key, including anonymous
keys. Inheritance: ordinary single rotations underlying each actual StepEv.
Ambiguity: the tokens below record signed changes; they are not additional
credits and do not establish distinct donor supply. Bridge: a future
source-licensed joint charging theorem may attach B demands to the exact
actual bit IDs carrying these balanced changes. That charging theorem
must prove eligibility, noncollision and Bank25 separately.

## Rank-update theorem and proof

For an actual right rotation at old pivot p with promoted left child x,
write the actual subtree at its current depth d as

    p(x(L,C),R) -> x(L,p(C,R)).

The depths of ALL keys in L and key x decrease by1. The depths of key p
and ALL keys in R increase by1. Every key in C or outside this subtree
retains its depth. L union {x} and {p} union R are two disjoint contiguous
ordered intervals of K. Both are nonempty. The same statements hold
under reflection: for p(L,x(C,R)), {p} union L increases by1 and {x} union
R decreases by1. This is a direct depth calculation on the original
rotation at arbitrary d, not a shallow-pivot normalization. Arbitrary
subtree internal depths and all outside ancestors are preserved.

Record ONE balanced token

    (actual t,j,b; interval_decreased, interval_increased).

A double has its two actual single rotations assigned the original bits
b=0,1, one token per bit. A terminal zig uses bit0; its real bit1 is not
used for this rank-change representation. No token is copied for its two
intervals. Thus token identities inject into the original inventory of
2A actual bits, without declaring a token free under any frozen owner.

For every original access, the sum of these signed interval indicator
vectors equals its exact full rank-vector change. This follows by
telescoping actual rotations, including every anonymous helper. Over an
exactly A-closed period their sum is zero at EVERY physical key. This
pointwise cancellation is not permission to release or spend an owned bit.
It does not say that B demand costs cancel.

## Joint source geometry

Every nonempty interval of physical key order has a unique shallowest
key in A. Indeed the LCA of its two extreme keys belongs to that interval
and is an ancestor of all its keys; any distinct minimum-depth keys would
have a strictly shallower LCA in the same interval, a contradiction.

Use a proof-only value child for each actual B node, alongside its left
and right subtree children. The unique shallowest key of a B subtree
selects exactly one of its nonempty children. This defines a heavy path
decomposition without altering any actual tree operation or cost.

Immediately after A has processed an original KEEP x, it has root x.
Thus every current B ancestor subtree containing x has unique minimum
rank0 at x, and the entire B search path to x follows these heavy choices.
DELETE creates rank tokens but never a B demand; root-repeat KEEP and
all changing charts retain their original semantics. All physical keys,
including private ancestors, are used in the rank definition.

## Why this is a different interface

The two signed intervals are retained as ONE physical-bit history object;
neither separate tree statistics nor inherited debt values recover their
joint effect on the current B decomposition. Exact-return A pairs can
cancel their rank vectors while still changing B. Therefore a valid
funding procedure must retain B's interval/owner interaction through the
return, rather than inferring a future deep A access or spending two
interval endpoints as two fresh credits.

The known lazy-interval framework of Chmel et al.,
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18498v1 , is a structural reference. Its
near-loglog result and normalized comparator do not supply the exact
constant-two donor rule or the original physical-owner contract here.
No normalizing rotations or asymptotic cost units are imported.

## Smallest new root bridge — RT35-JOINT

Construct a source transducer which covers every full B double by a
distinct actual A bit while retaining these signed interval changes and
their evolving B incidence. It must survive merges/splits, exact A
returns, repeated/shared boundaries and arbitrary support; prove global
noncollision and the original Bank25 license. Existing h0 and frozen
owners stay literal under their established exchange interface. This
bridge is OPEN. The rank representation alone is not a proof of it.

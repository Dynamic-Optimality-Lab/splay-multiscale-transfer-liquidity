## 1. One watched edge per H1/Q3 residual

Such a macro has exactly one positive A access, to its final genuine
target x, of depth1/2. The final KEEP may have A cost0 if that positive
access was an earlier original DELETE x. Its pre-root G is the
preceding genuine full root and is marked.

At depth1 watch G->x. At depth2, if the immediate parent P is
unmarked watch P->x; if P is marked watch the OUTER edge G->P.
Each selected edge has a marked child and is destroyed by the
unique positive A event. There is only one h0-unpaid original double
in this macro; use its exact demand ID (t,3).

Pair the watched edge's death with its latest surviving birth at an

There is such a birth: the edge is present before the death and absent
after it. A positive creator and this H1 death cannot be in the same
natural macro. Pairings of deaths of the same labeled edge have

## 2. Root exposure and the joint two-unit birth bound

Reconstruct the C18 inner-edge barrier. For a left edge p->x with
p marked, if some lower ancestor b<p lies outside the child subtree X,
then while the edge persists at completed-access boundaries a key<p
remains outside X. An access outside X leaves X a rigid hanger. An
access inside X that leaves p->x still present makes its target<p
the new root, hence a new outside witness. Thus the edge cannot
survive to a boundary at which p is root. Reverse all orders for
a right edge. A selected marked-parent watched edge has parent
root before death, so such an inner birth is impossible.

For LL, G(P(v(A,B),C),D) becomes v(A,P(B,G(C,D))).
Every surviving new edge with marked child that can qualify is:

    v->P if P marked;
    P->G if G marked;
    P->root(B) if P unmarked and root(B) marked;
    G->root(C) if G unmarked and root(C) marked.

The marked middle-hanger alternatives excluded by the last two
conditions are inner births by the preceding barrier. If P is
marked, the first edge plus at most one of the second/fourth
qualify. If P is unmarked, at most two of the second/third/fourth
qualify. Thus there are at most TWO jointly, not two per category.
For LR, each side has either the new root-to-operand marked-child
edge or its anonymous-operand-to-middle-root alternative: at
most one on each side. RR/RL are literal key/child reflections.
A terminal zig has at most ONE qualifying surviving birth.

These are free-subtree identities, uniform in all private populations.
Edges inside a hanger are unchanged. A temporary outside attachment
to the accessed target cannot survive the complete access, because
that target ends at root. Later outer events preserve processed
operand subtrees; final selected births are assigned to their unique
original creating event. Canonical ordering assigns bit0/1 to the
at most two exports. The raw creator map is therefore injective
over the selected H1/Q3 residuals, for every finite source.
This does not make those raw bits unused under h0.

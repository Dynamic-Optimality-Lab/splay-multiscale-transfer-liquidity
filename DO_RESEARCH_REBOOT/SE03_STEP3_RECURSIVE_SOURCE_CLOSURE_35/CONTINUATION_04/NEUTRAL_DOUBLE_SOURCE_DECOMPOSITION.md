## 2. Independently justified actual donor locations

slots as release slots. Within a B1/B2 double designate its FIRST

For every integer level ell, the upcrossings and downcrossings of ell

Its donor is the actual A +1 rotation bit recorded above.

## 3. Preserve h0 while reducing unpaid claims to neutral terminals

Take the original fixed h0. From each demand outside dom(h0):

- if neutral, stop with that actual neutral-demand liability label;
- otherwise follow its g_plus bit;
- if that bit is outside im(h0), stop at that actual bit;
- otherwise follow the reverse h0 edge to its owner and repeat.

Both matchings are injective. A walk from an h0-unmatched start cannot
return to its start or enter a cycle, since that would give two incoming
edges of the same matching. Two starts cannot merge. Hence every finite
walk ends, at either a DIFFERENT neutral-demand label or a DIFFERENT
actual h0-unowned bit.

Assign bit termini to their original unpaid starts in a SEPARATE partial
comparison, keeping every h0 value literal. The other unpaid starts

can already be h0-paid; it is not a second use of that demand's bit.
This is a constructive GLOBAL ownership reduction, not merely the count
bound in(2). No earlier successful assignment or supplied fE is replaced.

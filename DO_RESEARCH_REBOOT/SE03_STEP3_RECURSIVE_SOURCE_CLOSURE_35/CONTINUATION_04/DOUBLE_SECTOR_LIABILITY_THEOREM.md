## 1. Literal B double sectors at the macro source

Let BF's original root-to-x path be v0=g,v1,...,vd=x and let q=floor(d/2).
For the k-th ORIGINAL double, k=1,...,q, define its two labels to be the
full physical key sets of the ORIGINAL BF subtrees rooted at

    v_(d-2k+2), v_(d-2k+1).

These are proper nonempty nested subsets of K containing x. Different
doubles use disjoint pairs of labels: physical subtree sets along a path
are strictly nested, since each added ancestor contributes its own key.
For odd d, v1's label is excluded: it belongs to the terminal zig, which
creates no B-double demand. No actual event or cost is removed.

Why these are source-exact sectors: every bottom-up double consumes its
current target subtree and its original parent frame. The rebuilt target
subtree has the full label set of the source ancestor reached by prior
doubles. Each LL/RR/LR/RL formula preserves the whole consumed frame set.
Induction therefore gives exactly the two original path-subtree labels
above. In an SS double their two losses occur parent-first; in a mixed
double target-first. This changes only their order, not their identities.

Each physical subtree is an ordered interval, including its original
private hangers. These are proof labels on ACTUAL physical sets; private
keys are NOT relabeled genuine, accessed, deleted or contracted.

## 2. Present sectors force an injective local first-loss matching

Let J_phys(A) be the set of full physical key sets of ALL actual A

belongs to J_phys(A_start). Choose the deeper label if present, otherwise
its parent label. Let P be these double demands and N the remaining ones,

A single rotation p(y(L,C),R)->y(L,p(C,R)) preserves the whole frame set
and every untouched set; the only lost physical subtree set is the old

    lambda:P -> the macro's actual2H A units

## 3. Literal-owner-preserving reduction of EVERY unpaid claim

Let h0 be the frozen natural own injection onto the first min(q,2H)
demands from the first min(q,2H) actual units. If q<=2H it is already full.
If q>2H, its image is the ENTIRE local inventory U of2H units.

For a present demand d define

    psi(d)=h0^(-1)(lambda(d)).

This partial map is injective and has range inside dom(h0). From each
unpaid start d0 outside dom(h0), follow psi while the current demand is
present. Stop at the first demand in N and attach ITS doubly missing
sector as the liability label.

The finite walk cannot cycle. It cannot return to its start, which is
outside psi's range. Entry into a different cycle would give two distinct
incoming psi edges at the entry, violating injectivity. Two unpaid starts
cannot have merging walks for the same reason. They end at DIFFERENT
demands of N, whose two-label sectors are disjoint in this source path.
Thus this is an explicit source-derived injection

    unpaid B doubles -> doubly missing original B interval sectors.

It is constructed from actual rotations and the fixed h0, not postulated
from an equivalent cardinality inequality. ALL physical h0 assignments
remain literal; no bit is released or reassigned. A terminal demand may
already have an h0 owner. Its sector is a LIABILITY annotation, not an
extra copy of that owner's bit or a claim that it became free.

## 5. The exact global problem remaining

For arbitrary sources, retain each missing-sector liability with its
absolute macro and demand occurrence. The SAME interval pair can recur
in another macro, so occurrence tags do not manufacture capacity.
lambda and psi are local proof data; they do not supply nonlocal free
bits. First-loss/birth or creator bits may already be owned, and different
liabilities may reach one historical event.

Prove a source-licensed, simultaneous repayment rule for these doubly
missing sectors, including their creations, returns, changes and
overlaps, or construct a genuine original-source counterexample. Do not
infer that global source closure itself guarantees the endpoints. No
donor assignment across different macros is proved here.

## 3. The source fact that forces availability

### Lemma 1: a persistent B ancestor excludes a positive A rotation

At an actual A rotation, let a,b be distinct vertices of S such that
a is an ancestor of b in the CURRENT full B tree. Let K be the old
S-label of the promoted A child's subtree. If

    a in K,   b not in K,                              (1)

then the rotation has DeltaPsi<=0. Its OWN actual bit belongs to R1,
independently of the original cyclic crossing-map assignments.

PROOF. Every actual B subtree containing a also contains its descendant
b. Therefore K cannot be a label in J_S(B). One actual A rotation
replaces only the old promoted-child label K by the new demoted-pivot
label J; the whole-frame label persists at the new frame root. Losing
a label absent from J_S(B) cannot increase the missing-label set.
Introducing J can only leave that set unchanged or decrease it.
SET duplicates can suppress these changes, not reverse their signs.
Thus DeltaPsi is 0 or -1. Step 1 uses only +1 real A-rotation bits,
so this actual bit is outside image(g1). QED.

This is a one-sided label-exclusion consequence of actual ancestry.
No sign assumption on the new label J is needed. In particular there
is no assumed free endpoint or release of a positive bit's owner.
The same proof applies to either rotation direction.

## 5. Source-forced return rotations and their physical availability

### Lemma 2: B preserves an excluding anchor throughout the return

Immediately after B's KEEP an, a2 has a1 as its direct left child,
and its entire left subtree contains exactly the physical keys <a2.
That same left subtree stays attached to a2 throughout (2).

PROOF. B's original path has 2m edges, all right edges. Ordinary
bottom-up RR doubles leave, just before the final RR, the literal frame

    a1(V0,a2(V1,an(D,Vn))),

where D is the actual intervening subtree, with arbitrary ordered
hangers. The last two rotations are

    a2(a1(V0,V1),an(D,Vn))
      -> an(a2(a1(V0,V1),D),Vn).

Hence a2.left=a1(V0,V1), containing exactly all keys <a2. Every target
in (2) is >=a2. Its B search path never enters that whole lower
subtree, so every rotation operand is >=a2. If a2 is an operand, that
lower subtree is its untouched outer left hanger: a2 can be demoted
by a left rotation or promoted by a right rotation. The opposite two
possibilities would require an operand <a2, which is not on the path.
All other rotations leave its edge unchanged. Induction preserves the
entire lower subtree and in particular a2's ancestry of a1. QED.

The argument retains all gap subtrees, including S-bearing ones. It
does not use the generally false claim that any unaccessed hanging
subtree always retains its parent under arbitrary splays.

### Arbitrarily many occurrences of the same prepared pattern

Fix the same ordered anchors a1,...,an. Consider any finite collection
of distinct actual critical occurrences satisfying section 4, in one
paired-closed W or in actual repetitions of W. Their return segments
cannot overlap: the next occurrence would have to start at a genuine
KEEP an, while the interior of a previous segment contains only the
smaller genuine targets a_(n-1),...,a2 and identity repeats. An identity
repeat of an is not a qualifying start (its H=Q=0). Thus a second
qualifying critical occurrence cannot lie inside the first segment.

This proves disjointness of the NAMED repayment occurrences from the
original word. It does not assume independent solvency of return gaps,
disjoint cyclic complements, or a source-block partition. Preparation
histories and ancestry complements may overlap; they are not counted.

For s such actual occurrences, their C_e sets and F_e sets are disjoint,
and hence

    |union_e C_e|=(2m-1)s,
    |union_e F_e|=sum_e f_e<=(m-1)s.

The union of (7) is injective, extends the one fixed g1 and funds every
selected critical residual simultaneously. The proof holds for every
finite s and arbitrary permitted hanging populations. There is no
constant bound on the number of source occurrences in this theorem.

There is also a restricted prefix consequence of the actual assignment.
Start with no earlier selected unpaid claim, and pay a selected packet's
claims as their prescribed rho_e bits occur. All f_e claims finish by
its f_e-th return, before the next qualifying critical occurrence can
begin. Therefore the number of pending claims from THESE SELECTED
critical accesses at every executed prefix is at most m-1, independent
of the number of occurrences or repetitions. This follows from the
source-word separation just proved; it is not an assumed carry bound
and does not cover other residuals of W. It introduces no new ledger.

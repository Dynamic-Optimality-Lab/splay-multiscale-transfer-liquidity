# Fixed provisional ND assignments and the scalar problem

Status: source-independent matching lemma applied at the original RAW
same-period interface; separate review pending. No new Gate U permission.

Let g_P be ANY constructed raw physical partial injection on actual
demands D and actual units U of the ONE source period. If a complete
raw physical injection h:D->U exists, then one exists extending g_P.

Proof: start at each demand outside dom(g_P), follow its h edge and
then reverse g_P whenever that unit is provisionally owned. Unique
incoming edges prevent two starts meeting or an entered directed cycle;
the unpaid start itself has no incoming reverse-g_P predecessor. Total h
and finiteness give a distinct terminal outside im(g_P). Assign each
unpaid root its terminal and keep every g_P value. This is injective
on ALL demands. Both endpoints remain actual occurrences in the same
period. QED.

At the scalar inventory level this applies to original ND35 g+, the
lifetime variant or g_T: fixing one does not make complete RAW matching
existence harder. However normalization may LEAVE a proposed restricted
edge graph. Failure of fixed-g_P extension in that graph therefore says
nothing about all-source raw matching. If the graph itself is a licensed
eligibility restriction, that permission must be checked separately.

The CCR control illustrates this distinction exactly: its root-entry/own/
ND graph has a full22-demand matching. Its fixed-lifetime neutral
subgraph funds only7 of9 neutral claims, with claims(59,2),(68,2) having
empty remaining neighborhoods. Reassigning the provisional ND edges
inside the full graph removes that candidate restriction. No old h0,
fE, effective owner, actual source operation or historical successful
assignment was edited by this independent comparison calculation.

This lemma does NOT prove complete h exists. It also does NOT release
a genuinely frozen effective owner, an independently live reservation,
or an original temporal restriction. Objective II still requires the
unchanged licensed exchange and compatibility proof. Conditional on a
source-derived complete raw h, the accepted whole-block source selector
can include all physical units and normalize natural h0 with working2.
That budget comes from the constructed source injection; it must never
be assumed merely from a desired global cardinality inequality.

## 1. Exact existing normalization, with proof

Fix ONE original period and its finite demand and bit sets D,U. Let
p=g1 be the accepted raw partial injection. WHERE an independently
source-certified complete h:D->U exists, the following original rule
preserves p literally.

For each d0 in F=D minus domain(p), use h(d0). If that unit has p owner
d1, use h(d1); continue. Stop on the first unit outside image(p), and
assign that terminal unit to d0. Keep p(d) for every d in domain(p).

To prove termination, let T(d)=p^{-1}(h(d)) when defined. T is a partial
injective map, because both h and p are injective. No unpaid root has an
incoming T edge: it is outside domain(p). A finite walk from such a root
cannot enter a cycle, since entry would create two predecessors, or close
at its root, since that root has no predecessor. Total h prevents any
other stopping failure, so each walk ends at an actual free unit.

Walks from different unpaid roots cannot merge, by the same unique-
predecessor argument. Thus their free terminal units are distinct.
They are outside image(p), so p disjoint-union terminal assignments is
injective and total. Every h edge and p edge is on this SAME actual
period. The argument uses no adjacent symbolic copy, no causality
deadline, and no assumption that h(d0) itself is free.

This proves the implication EXISTING COMPLETE h -> extension of g1.
It does NOT prove h exists. The terminal pair need not be an edge in
the narrower source candidate graph even when every h edge is.
It grants no extra fE/Bank25/reservation permission.

## 3. A uniform quantitative constraint in the saved deeper family

This is a direct consequence of the EXISTING PC35 family and the fixed

For every m>=4 let n=2m+1, with ALL n keys genuine and marked. At the
specified natural boundary the saved family has

    B = right vine on 1,...,n,
    A = 1(empty,n(T,empty)),

where T is the descending left vine on 2,...,n-1. Its next operation is
KEEP n. No helper has been removed from this macro; it consists of that
one original access. Its source embedding, including the later DELETE
reset, is the one proved in PROJECTED_CLUSTER_ACCESS_THEOREM.md §4.

LEMMA. For THIS whole symbolic source family, at the distinguished macro

    B0=m-1, B1=1, B2=Z=0,
    |F at t|=m-1,
    |R1 intersect this macro's physical inventory|=1.

Consequently every completion preserving g1 requires at least m-2
distinct bits OUTSIDE this macro for its m-1 residual demands.

PROOF. A performs one zig, producing bits (t,1,0),(t,1,1). B has even
depth 2m and m RR doubles, with no terminal zig. The accepted literal
RR identity gives, for grandparent g'=n-2,n-4,...,1, new labels {g'}
and [g',n-1]. After A the proper A labels are [1,n-1] and [2,k],
2<=k<=n-1. Exactly one created B label is present in A: [1,n-1],
created at the final double. Every other double is neutral, and the
final double has one decrease. Hence the four displayed B counts.

Before the A zig Psi=n-2; immediately after it Psi=n-1. All internal
B rotations are neutral until the final drop to n-2. Thus the actual
cyclic level pairing for this last drop uses THAT A zig's positive
rotation bit (t,1,0), with no intervening same-level crossing. ND35
and g1 assign it to the final nonneutral demand (t,m). There are no
access-level release donors here. g1's image contains only positive

unit is occupied exactly as stated. Step 1 gives m-1 residuals. At most

## 4. Why the CD(6) suppliers do not finish the attempted induction

The reusable physical inductions are valid WHEN their source signs or
supplier conditions have been proved. In CD(6), the full original period
chooses one of cores6..11, and all owners/packets are included. The
arbitrary-A quotient lifts that six-key sign certificate; it does not
give signs for larger supports.

At arbitrary maximal support, the actual maximum-depth macro and its
actual q1 successor can have H=2,Q=r. Their net physical shortage is
r-4. This is not the number of current Step 1 residuals. The q1
successor is already paid by g1. The saved modified Gamma_r source
proves that unchanged packet-debt width must be at least r-4. The
old width3 fails for r>=8; the old physical incoming-carry implementation
exceeds Bank25 for r>=30. This rejects that extension, not scalar CD.

The prepared-family theorem DOES name distinct earlier ordinals, because
its actual preparation has H_p>=r-2,Q_p=1 and all other macros are
naturally funded. An arbitrary dirty source need not contain this reset
or this depth-drop bound. The creator theorem covers only the stated
H1/Q3 watched lifetimes and pays a creator group when its actual tail
dominates the group. The collateral theorem requires its independent
endpoint-change condition and disjoint source blocks. Step 2's CHAIN
condition proves none of these missing hypotheses on arbitrary sources.

Nor does source closure force a fresh deep recovery: the inherited
G<x<C<R R,G exact return restores every hanger. CCR's seven B-only
neutral lifetime cycles rule out an assumed birth for every component.
Natural interval trimming cannot bypass these cases: the saved ordinary
RL+zig versus trimmed RR identity disproves that source-reduction rule.

Hence the source-supply step of the proposed induction is not proved.
No new donor rule, width increase, reset assumption, or block partition
has been silently inserted to repair it.

## 5. Exact ownership closure still required of a partial supplier

For a SPECIFIED existing injective partial comparison s, the old
alternating rule sends demand d to s(d), then to its g1 owner if occupied.
It works from roots T only if s is defined on every reached demand E:

    T subset E subset domain(s),
    g1^{-1}(s(E) intersect image(g1)) subset E.

Then injectivity rules out a reached cycle, and finiteness gives distinct
free terminals. This is the existing normalization argument on its
actual domain. Source applicability, physical injectivity of s and that
domain closure must be established independently. We have not done so
on arbitrary overloaded chains. Merely asking for all these conditions
without a source supplier would rename the original funding problem.

In particular neither of the following is a proof:

* Counting the same root-entry event once for every nested label.
* Writing |R1|-|F|=2A-Q and assuming its sign.

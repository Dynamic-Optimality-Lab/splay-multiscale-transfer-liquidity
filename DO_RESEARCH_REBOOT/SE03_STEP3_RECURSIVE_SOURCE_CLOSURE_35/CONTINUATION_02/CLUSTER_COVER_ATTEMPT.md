# Attack on the remaining arbitrary-source supply, and the exact failed bridge

RC35 is genuinely source-uniform: k new endpoint clusters force k actual
rotations. Try using these births as compensation for all hard demands.
For one specified consecutive reset block whose k>=Q, RC35 proves local
solvency, and the accepted source-ordinal completion gives a full map.
AF35 is a nontrivial arbitrary-support instance of this rule.

The proposed universal extension would need:

    every hard source-simple component admits source-derived blocks whose
    missing-cluster births or other licensed spare ranges cover its
    residuals, without duplicated physical occurrences.

This is NOT implied by RC35. On a closed A interval the endpoint missing
cluster count is zero. On overlapping shorter intervals the SAME rotation
can be the first birth for several claimed targets. Furthermore an actual
rotation bit may already be in im(h0), or subject to an unretired Gamma
reservation. Neither counting it as a birth nor choosing an endpoint makes
it a comparison spare or an owner-free terminal. The original finite
exchange only handles these issues AFTER a compatible full comparison.

The exact small remaining bridge is a SOURCE-DERIVED disjoint charging or
block rule on the state-simple support>=7 leaf. A theorem merely asserting
that such blocks have nonnegative total weight would assume the needed
sign; it would not supply the source-derived rule. No proof of this bridge
has been found here. No exact complete-system countercertificate is known
either. FD35 shows what the latter would have to exclude.

## Alternatives actually examined

* Adjacent anchor supply succeeds in AF35; the inherited Sigma obstruction
  still refutes applying it to arbitrary genuine macros.
* Unchanged bounded debt/carry fails on inherited Gamma_r as r grows;
  static block emission avoids that metric only AFTER a block is licensed.
* Taking every physical bit as a free donor ignores a0/Gamma and is invalid.
* Taking the whole period as an automatically solvent block assumes CD(r).
  SC35 permits that block ONLY after independently proved smaller-cycle
  source comparisons; it does not license an unresolved simple leaf.
* Splitting an arbitrary simple leaf at a convenient access leaves nonclosed
  words outside the inherited CD<=6 theorem. No induction applies there.

These identify the mathematical obstruction. They are not a counterexample
to UCF35, a proof of impossibility of further research, or a CPU-cap stop.

# RC35 — endpoint clusters force real preparation cost

Status: mathematical derivation complete; audit scope in INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_STATUS.md.

## Box

Target: lower-bound the actual A StepEv count of ANY original access word
carrying an ordered BST T to T'. Conditions: same physical key set;
ordinary rotations only; no physical deletion. Inheritance: one StepEv is
one zig or one bottom-up double and has at most two rotations. Ambiguity:
this lower bound alone supplies no global funding or ownership permission.
Bridge: a specified local source reset with many new endpoint clusters
can certify its own macro's supply independently of a desired cost count.

For a tree T let K(T) be the set of key sets of all nonempty rooted
subtrees, including the whole tree. Each member is an ordered interval.
Let k=|K(T') minus K(T)|. Any such original word has

    number of rotations >= k,     H >= ceil(k/2).             (RC)

Proof. In a single rotation with old parent p and child v, every subtree
cluster except those at p and v is unchanged. The old cluster at p is
exactly the new cluster at v. Only the new cluster at p can be a new set.
Thus one rotation creates at most one previously absent target cluster.
Each of the k target clusters absent initially has a first creation along
the word; these first creations require k distinct rotations. A StepEv
has at most two rotations. Summing gives RC. Repeated destruction and
recreation cannot reduce this lower bound. QED.

This applies to arbitrary finite trees, anonymous ancestors, both
orientations and arbitrary access labels. Reflection maps all the clusters
bijectively and preserves the argument. It creates no physical credit by
itself: credits remain the actual events forced by RC.

## Anchored source instance, uniformly in r

Let r>=4, n=2r-1 and m=n-2. U is the odd right spine 1,3,...,m;
each odd z>=3 has the even leaf z-1 on its left. Let

    As=(n,(n-1,U,empty),empty),
    T =U with m.right=(n-1,empty,(n,empty,empty)).

K(As) contains the following r distinct clusters absent from K(T):

    [1,n-1], [1,m], [2,m], [4,m], ... , [m-1,m].

There are r-2 even-start intervals in the displayed tail. In T each odd
nonleaf's subtree reaches n, each even node below m is a singleton, and
the two high subtrees are [n-1,n] and {n}. Hence none of the displayed
intervals is present in T. Therefore ANY reset J:T->As has

    H(J)>=ceil(r/2),    2H(J)>=r>r-1.

For the immutable SI34 witness r=8 this gives eight required rotations
and at least four real A events. The next full KEEP n has r-1 B doubles
and zero A events at As, so its natural macro J;KEEP n is own-solvent.
No transformation word was numerically expanded to obtain this bound.

## Failed universal extrapolation

RC counts FIRST births of specified missing endpoint clusters. It does
not assign disjoint births to arbitrary overlapping recovery claims.
On an entire closed A period K(start)=K(end), so RC alone gives k=0.
Applying it to many overlapping intervals can count one rotation many
times. A source-derived disjoint charging rule is necessary before this
can fund arbitrary connected cuts. No such universal rule is asserted.

# 2. NEW METHODOLOGY — IAGS-R

## Inherited Alteration General Stage with Recursive Closure

Every mathematical problem is represented by a proof Box:

\[
\mathcal B=
(T,H,I,U,\Lambda,D).
\]

Where:

- T — exact theorem target.
- H — hypotheses and source domain.
- I — inherited established results.
- U — unresolved proof obligations.
- \(\Lambda\) — exact logical bridge to the parent.
- D — a justified measure or structural ordering showing that recursive refinement makes progress.

Each Box must also contain an explicit **dual falsification objective**.

For a candidate theorem T:

- Prove T.
- Or construct a valid counterexample to T.
- Or split the unresolved mathematical obligations into smaller Boxes whose established conclusions reconstruct T or its negation.

**A partial lemma does not close its parent Box.**

**A successful restricted source case does not close a universal Box.**

**A failed candidate proof does not prove the theorem false.**

The recursive process must preserve these distinctions.

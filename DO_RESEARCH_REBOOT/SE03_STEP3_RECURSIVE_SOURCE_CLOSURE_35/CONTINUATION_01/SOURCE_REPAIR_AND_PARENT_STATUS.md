# What the counterexample does and does not invalidate

The three selected consumers have immediate genuine predecessor KEEP1
macros. In this source each such predecessor has H=1,Q=1, while the
following selected macro has H=1,Q=3. Thus each actual disjoint pair is
source-solvent: Hpair2,Qpair4. The three pairs do not share operations.

An explicit independent comparison keeps natural own owners: predecessor
demand(h_i,1) uses(h_i,1,0); selected demands(v_i,1),(v_i,2) use their own
bits0,1; selected demand(v_i,3) uses(h_i,1,1), with h_i=v_i-1. All units
are actual original A-event bits. The extra units lie OUTSIDE N_m of
the selected consumers, exactly as the source pool calculation proves.

Entering each pair empty, admit its predecessor's two bits once, pay
its own demand and retain the other bit. Admit the selected event pair,
then pay its three demands and finish empty. Working peak is3<=Bank25.
This is a direct causal source-block construction and preserves natural
h0 on these blocks. It is not a claim that those bits are unowned under
an arbitrary supplied frozen a0, nor a universal bank/owner theorem.
The original full-comparison exchange remains conditional on completing
the rest of its actual source license and ownership interfaces.

Accordingly, the proposed RAW retirement-plus-own pool is too narrow.
The proof does not show that the frozen-extension architecture is false.
A replacement must permit independently source-licensed grouping or
exchange involving credits outside the retired-B-ancestor pools. A
blanket adjacent-predecessor theorem is NOT adopted: earlier source
counterexamples to that universal shortcut remain valid. The grouping
above is proved only at this explicit source pattern.

by the source cut. REAL35's specific matrix is now source-realized in

BIRTH35, KEEP35 and the original owner algebra are not invalidated.

can enlarge its counting neighborhoods. No unsupplied fixed fE/a0 is

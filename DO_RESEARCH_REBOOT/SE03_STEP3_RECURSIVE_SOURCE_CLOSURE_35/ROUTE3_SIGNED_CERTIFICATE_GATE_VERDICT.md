## 7. Exact missing representation/sign theorem

The precise reusable proof contract is the EXISTING CD5 pair of premises:

1. Every original source has one consistent represented CLOSED walk,
   retaining all source operations and exact shared boundary information,
   whose certified weight is dominated by the original 2A-Q_B (or by
   the SAME already justified remaining-credit-minus-deficit accounting).
2. A specified existing-domain or otherwise independently established
   signed function satisfies the required local inequality on EVERY edge
   of that complete source representation, including LOW/HIGH, zero/positive
   transfers, helpers, private operands, cyclic histories and any actual
   chart/annotation changes. Boundary potential values must close exactly.

This report supplies neither premise anew and introduces no signed function.
The five-key representation and Phi satisfy them only at their certified
domains. No inspected theorem establishes them for arbitrary r with the
Step 2 canonical provenance/g_1 annotations.

The smallest specific REPRESENTATION gap, if those annotations are to be
certificate coordinates, is an analogue of the old 11F boundary-consistency
and cost-simulation theorem: exact update/transport of the actual label-run,
event/owner information across one macro, equality of shared public states,
and closure at the original seam, without counting one unit twice. Step 2's
forward decoration is not yet that signed-state interface. A reverse
realization of every abstract walk is not required for a positive proof.

If instead the existing full actual A/B state is retained with original
source prices, its original source closure is already available, and the
first missing input is the all-support LOCAL signed-inequality theorem
itself. No existing table or admitted potential class supplies it on the
remaining residual-bearing cases. That theorem is not reduced to a smaller
finite obligation by merely invoking telescoping or recording nested tags.

These are separate obligations: a representation without proved signs
does not certify compensation; signs on an incomplete or boundary-inconsistent
representation do not prove the source theorem. Asking for an arbitrary
function whose closed-walk sums prove the whole target is not advertised
here as a new small lemma or evidence that the hard part is solved.

For a physical conclusion, any eventual source-signed budget still needs
the inherited static-block/carry and owner licenses at their actual scopes.
CD5's scalar base does not manufacture a stronger arbitrary-support
auxiliary allocator, and a raw-free bit is not automatically fE-free.

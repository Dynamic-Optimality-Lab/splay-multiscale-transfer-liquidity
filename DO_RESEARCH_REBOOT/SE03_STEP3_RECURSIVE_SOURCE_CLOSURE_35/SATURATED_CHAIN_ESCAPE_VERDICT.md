## 4. A uniform partial escape rule with a proved terminal

This is the strongest NEW compensation statement derived here. It uses
the existing lifetime components and EXISTING unused B2 credits. It
does not require a complete h, a free augmentation endpoint assumption,
or an all-support CD(6) extension.

### Definition on an arbitrary original W

Write P(c),E(c) for the first and second actual decrease slots of a B2
double c. Let

    b_c = g1(c) = lambda(P(c)),
    v_c = lambda(E(c)).

Call c eligible when its WHOLE original B access has B0=0. Every b_c
is a positive A rotation and hence a genuine birth in the existing
missing-label component graph. Let K_c be that birth-to-death component.
It is not a conjectured source object: it is the actual component of
the retained occurrence graph.

Define T_c to consist of residual demands d in F(W) for which at least
one of the two actual neutral ports belongs to K_c. A demand is included
once even if both ports lie in that global component.

The following greedy rule specifies a partial extension without assuming
a matching or sufficient capacity. Order eligible c by original absolute
B occurrence and double index. At each c, choose the first not-yet-chosen
member of T_c in original demand order, if one exists, and assign it v_c.
Do nothing at c if all its candidates have already been chosen or T_c
is empty. Let T*(W) be the demands actually selected by this rule.

### Theorem: component-to-B2-release escape

For EVERY original finite paired-closed W, at arbitrary genuine support
and arbitrary private A geometry, this rule constructs an injection

    e_B2 : T*(W) -> C_A(W) minus image(g1).

The union g1 disjoint-union e_B2 preserves every g1 assignment. It
uses each original unit at most once and no adjacent period copy.
Repeated or shared preparations and overlapping provenance chains do
not invalidate the conclusion. They can reduce how many roots are
selected; they cannot duplicate a terminal.

PROOF. For a B2 c, the first and second decrease slots are distinct.
The inherited ND35 level bijection assigns them distinct original A
positive bits b_c and v_c. Positive change is a pure missing-label
birth: a fine A step can remove at most one and add at most one label,
so total change +1 means one addition and no removal. Thus K_c is
well-defined. Original paired closure justifies the level pairing on
this one period, including a possible cyclic predecessor.

Step 1 keeps b_c as c's fixed nonneutral payment. It consumes release
slots of that access ONLY for its neutral demands, including the ZRC35
reservation when a neutral last double exists. If B0=0, there is no
such neutral demand or reservation. Its second B2 slot E(c) is therefore
never selected by Step 1. Since lambda is injective on ALL actual
decrease slots, v_c is outside the entire global image(g1), not merely
unused by c. This derives freedom of the endpoint from source types;
no unspecified free endpoint is an assumption.

Different eligible doubles have different E(c), hence different v_c.
The rule selects a demand at most once. Therefore the output pairs have
distinct domains and distinct values disjoint from image(g1). They are
all original physical units on W. No internal witness or preparation
bit is spent again: the lifetime path is only the source relationship
that selects a claim, and the ONLY new expenditure is v_c.

The proof uses no bound on component length, root-entry load, number
of preparations, or number of chosen claims. Shared descriptions keep
the same actual IDs. Both orientations obey the same literal splay and
SET-change identities; reflecting keys and child pointers preserves
the component/slot statements and physical ordinals. Actual private
ancestors and helper events stay in the original graph. QED.

## 7. Exact remaining obstruction and answer about closure

the legality of following original label runs around the seam. That
provides a real donor for an actual B2 release slot. It does NOT, by
the inherited arguments, guarantee enough such release slots or enough
distinct terminal routes for an arbitrary collection of residuals.

Whether the FULL original source dynamics and closure force enough
external credits for every overloaded collection remains OPEN. This
investigation gives no negative answer to that mathematical question.
It gives a positive answer for the new selected B2 class and for the
already prepared source family, and a precise counterexample to B2-only
universal coverage.

The uncovered compensation includes:

1. Several residual demands visiting one eligible A-born component:
   there is only one associated B2 release in the proved rule.
2. Residual ports in B-only cycles, with no component birth to relay.
3. Partial owner paths whose next demand lacks a source-certified

4. Several roots reaching one nonpositive or virtual unit; separate
   reachability does not prove simultaneous availability.
5. Any required independent owner/reservation or Bank25 license beyond
   the raw g1 interface.

The missing claim is not repaired by assuming endpoint expansion or
writing |R1|-|F|=2A-Q and asserting its sign. The new partial rule proves
availability of ITS specified release bit, not availability of an
unspecified number of future or external credits.

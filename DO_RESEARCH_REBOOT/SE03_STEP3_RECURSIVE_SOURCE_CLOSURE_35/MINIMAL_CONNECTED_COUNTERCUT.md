# MIN35 — exact countercut normalization, PROVED

Target: identify every possible minimal ROOT35 failure without asserting
its existence. Conditions: fixed source domain/definitions of ROOT35.
Inheritance: C34 localization, REM35 and DELETE-LOOP35. Dual: a genuine
negative source whose normalized negative remainder loses admissibility.
Measure: lexicographic (selected macro count, original period length),
over pairs of natural numbers, with connected-component splits also
strictly decreasing selected count.

Choose a negative pair (W,S) minimizing that pair. It is connected in the
RAW N-neighborhood overlap graph, has at least two macros, an interior
target and an own-excess macro. It has p_m(S)<Q_m for every m; otherwise
REM35 yields a strictly smaller negative coalition. It has no nonempty
DELETE-only exact A-return subword inside the legal period, since LOOP35
gives a shorter negative source. All these implications are proved source
or finite-set reductions; none assumes a source-global sign.

The birth and KEEP lemmas further restrict its actual operation/pivot
incidences. They do not bound its deficit: MODEL35 obeys their numerical
consequences yet is deficient as an abstract incidence system.

To finish the contradiction one must prove such a SOURCE-normalized cut
impossible, or source-realize one. The normalization has not supplied that
last implication. Taking an inclusion-minimal counterexample and simply
asserting it must reduce again is equivalent to assuming the desired law.

The ordinary C34 MOP counterpart includes owner stays in neighborhoods.
It is not substituted for this raw source normalization. Deleting owner-
occupied bits is never licensed by the raw LOOP35 proof.

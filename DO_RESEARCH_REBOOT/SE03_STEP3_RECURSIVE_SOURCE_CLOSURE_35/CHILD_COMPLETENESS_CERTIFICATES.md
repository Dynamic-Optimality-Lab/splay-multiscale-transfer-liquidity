# Exact reconstruction certificates

1. ATOM35 is exhaustive in r_t=0 or r_t>=1 and h_t in{0,1}. It gives the
   capacity identity and REM/CL without any sign premise.
2. PEEL35 plus REM reconstructs ROOT35 by induction on |S|. CORE35 gives
   a complete all-coalition certificate for a FIXED source if its full
   deletion order empties. Uniform source PEEL35 remains a child, not an
   established condition.
3. MIN35 plus COMP35 would reconstruct ROOT35: every failure has a
   lexicographically minimal normalized failure, which COMP35 must exclude.
   The normal form alone does not establish that exclusion.
4. Root coalitions split exhaustively by their number of interior macros:
   zero (EX-PR34), one (ONE35), at least two (MULTI35). Closing the latter
   two source laws would prove every Hall inequality, enabling ONE joint
   matching; independently fixing their allocations would not suffice.
5. SHALLOW35 exhausts its own depth<=2 hypotheses by depth1, RR and RL
   plus reflection. It excludes the consecutive constant-anchor monotone
   template, not the complement with gaps/turns/changed anchors/helpers.

All bridges are acyclic. No child uses CD or ROOT35 to prove its required
source sign. Unproved source conditions are explicitly left unresolved.
The abstract three-macro matrix is a countermodel only to weakened
numerical premises, not to any source-completeness statement.

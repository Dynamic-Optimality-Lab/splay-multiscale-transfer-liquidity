## 2. Every demand and credit, exactly once

Use the original Psi=|J_S(B) minus J_S(A)| at the original fixed carrier.
Write P,N,T_A for actual positive, negative and zero-Psi A rotations,
and V_A for the virtual second units of terminal A zigs. Then

    2A=P+N+T_A+V_A,
    P=N+B1+2B2+Z1,
    Q_B=B0+B1+B2.                                  (1)

Let L=B2+Z1. Define the ACTUAL Step 1 neutral-release usage

    m_t=min(B0_t,B2_t+Z1_t),     M=sum_t m_t.

These M neutral demands are already paid by existing release slots.
Their exact IDs and slot donors are frozen by Step 1. Consequently

    |domain(g1)|=B1+B2+M,
    |F|=B0-M.                                     (2)

The physical inventory has this disjoint partition, with lambda the
EXISTING cyclic level pairing:

| Physical unit category | Count | Step 1 status |
|---|---:|---|
| Actual negative A rotation's OWN bit, U_N | N | Unused by g1. |
| Actual zero A rotation bit, U_T | T_A | Unused by g1. |
| Virtual second A-zig unit, U_V | V_A | Unused by g1. |
| Positive bits lambda(A-negative slots), H_N | N | Released and unused by g1. |
| Positive bits lambda(UNSELECTED B2/zig releases), H_B | L-M | Unused by g1. |
| Positive bits lambda(B1/B2 payment slots) | B1+B2 | Fixed nonneutral payments of g1. |
| Positive bits lambda(SELECTED B releases) | M | Fixed neutral payments of g1. |

The first three rows are not positive-rotation bits. The last four
partition all positive bits by their DISTINCT actual decrease slots.
Injectivity of lambda makes the rows disjoint. A negative slot's own
bit and its released positive head are different physical units; they
are not an event counted twice. Each virtual unit is not a rotation.

Thus

    R1=U_N disjoint-union U_T disjoint-union U_V
       disjoint-union H_N disjoint-union H_B,
    |R1|=2N+T_A+V_A+B2+Z1-M.                       (3)

Subtract (2) from (3):

    |R1|-|F|=2N+T_A+V_A+B2+Z1-B0=2A-Q_B.          (4)

This proves the user's two formulations equivalent. It does NOT prove
their sign. In particular B2+Z1 is not all still unused after g1: M
of those release heads are already spent. They appear in the neutral
bound only because the SAME M paid demands cancel on the other side.
Counting all L as available to F would double-spend those selected bits.

If genuine support is empty, Q_B=F=0, so the target is immediate. The
nonempty-support carrier and port arguments are needed only otherwise.

## 3. A new compatibility corollary, and what it does not prove

intervals, witnesses or actual A StepEv IDs overlap. A raw extension

PROOF. Every e_B donor is lambda of an unused B2 second-decrease slot,
so belongs to the POSITIVE-bit pool H_B. Every e_G donor is either

## 4. What source geometry adds to the exact identities

Each residual double has two original neutral-rotation ports. Step 2
provides an injective map

    port p -> (actual A rotation beta(p), actual missing interval I_p).

At a root-changing promotion into S, its tag labels form a nested B
chain. There is no corresponding proof that they give different bits.
The chain length may grow with support. Its actual event still has
two units, some of which may already be positive g1 payments.

The zero-Psi lemma strengthens the lifetime description: all A positives
are pure missing-label births, all A negatives pure deaths, and A zeros
preserve the whole missing set. Therefore the occurrence components have

    P birth-to-death paths,
    N+B1+2B2+Z1=P deaths,
    and directed cycles with no A birth/death.

The only transfers of missing labels are B rotations of zero change.
A B0 double has two such transfers; a B1 double has one; B2 has none.
The terminal B zig always decreases Psi. Thus the exact transfer count is

    total transfers on the paths and cycles = 2B0+B1.       (6)

Equations (1) and (6) constrain the endpoints and lengths DIFFERENTLY.
Birth/death balance does not bound how many transfers a path or cycle
can contain. Zero A work and virtual units are not additional births
or transfers, although their physical credits remain in (3). Root movement
does force A entry into each live label before its next B removal, but
several labels or claims can name the same entry event.

This is where the attempted universal counting proof stops: TAG retains
the interval coordinate, while physical capacity counts ONLY the actual
bits. Dropping the interval coordinate and treating tags as distinct
units is unjustified. Attaching all missing compensation to one shared
preparation repeats the same physical IDs. Guarded bundles repair this
only when their source cadence proves distinct event IDs. Arbitrary
cycles and saturated entries do not have that proved property.

This is a statement about what the inherited implications establish,
not an assertion that the original dynamics fail the target inequality.

## 6. Attack on a hypothetical actual capacity failure

Suppose a valid original period W satisfies |F|>|R1|. Define

    delta(W)=|F|-|R1|=Q_B-2A
            =B0-(2N+T_A+V_A+B2+Z1)>=1.             (7)

This does not posit that such a source exists. The following are proved
necessary consequences if it did.

### 6.1 A genuine source-simple failing leaf exists

Use the EXISTING complete-state cycle reduction SC35. If two proper
genuine KEEP boundaries have the same FULL labelled A/B state, split
the actual closed walk there. Both child source histories are reachable
and paired-closed, retain all their actual operation costs, and have
fewer genuine macro edges. Scalar delta=Q-2A is additive, so at least
one child still has positive delta. Iterate to a source-simple leaf.

All original operations belong to one of the proof children; no source
operation is dropped from an accepted execution or certificate. The
child's reaching prefix reaches its actual full state and supplies no
credit to its period. No quotient, Psi value, chart, or root pair is
substituted for complete state equality.

Carrier, neutral classification and g1 may differ when a child's genuine
support changes. Parent F and R1 must NOT be transported as if those
classifications were unchanged. Only the original A/Q costs are additive
in this contradiction reduction. If all child inequalities were proved,
they would sum to the parent inequality and the parent's original g1
could then be extended. No historical parent map is altered here.

This reduction stops at a genuine source-simple leaf. Closing A/B over
the WHOLE word does not force a shorter repeated-state interval. Splitting
such a leaf into independently paid preparation blocks without a proved
source seam would assume precisely what the argument still needs.

### 6.2 It lies outside the completed support domains

A failing leaf cannot have <=6 genuine KEEP keys, by the preserved
CD(5)/CD(6) results. If it has seven, it cannot have full B core13,
by MAX7. Thus a possible failure has either

    r=7 with 7<=|S|<=12, or r>=8.

It also lies outside every complete prepared-family/source class to
which the existing source funding rules actually apply. Private A
population and source length are not bounded by these exclusions.

No theorem here shows that every source-simple leaf belongs to one of
the completed domains. That is an unproved source assertion, not a
consequence of the well-founded decomposition.

### 6.3 A bounded-cost critical macro is necessary, not sufficient

Let r be the failing leaf's genuine support. The inherited source core
bound |S|<=2r-1 gives Q_e<=r-1 on every genuine natural macro. Such a
macro starts synchronized at the preceding genuine root and ends at a
different genuine key, so it has H_e>=1, including ALL helper work.
Since sum_e(Q_e-2H_e)>0, some actual macro satisfies

    Q_e>=2H_e+1,
    1<=H_e<=floor((r-2)/2).                       (8)

This follows from actual original costs, not from an abstract edge sign.
It is not permission to erase the other macros: their source geometry,
credits and closure remain essential. It does not say that THIS macro's
Step 1 residual count equals Q_e-2H_e; g1 can pay it nonlocally.

At the first uncompleted seven-key support, the necessary critical
types refine as follows:

| Full B carrier size | Maximum Q_e | Possible Q_e>2H_e types |
|---|---:|---|
| 7 or8 | 3 | H1/Q3 |
| 9 or10 | 4 | H1/Q3, H1/Q4 |
| 11 or12 | 5 | H1/Q3, H1/Q4, H1/Q5, H2/Q5 |

This table is a necessary-condition derivation, not a feasibility test,
a new source representation, or a proof that any such failing leaf
exists. Higher supports have a support-growing cutoff. Neither a fixed
LOW threshold nor six-key carry is assumed universal.

### 6.4 Repetition amplifies, rather than repairs, an actual failure

If W were failing, its exact A/B closure permits W^k for any finite k.
All copies execute the same source operations on the same returning
trees, and each occurrence gets its own actual ID. Therefore

    delta(W^k)=k*delta(W).

An independently PROVED additive error bound independent of source
length on this fixed ambient system would rule that out. No such bound
is supplied by the existing range of Psi: its endpoints already cancel
on W, while cycle/path transfer counts remain uncontrolled. Positing
that additive bound would be another unproved premise, not progress.
This repetition argument is conditional reasoning, not a new source
query, a fabricated counterexample, or permission to borrow a copy's bit.

### 6.5 The contradiction does not follow

All consequences above can be stated without violating a source theorem.
They identify an unproved source-simple, genuinely underfunded interaction
class; they do not show that its full closed source dynamics are realizable
with negative delta or prove they are impossible. The proof cannot move
from a locally bad macro or an abstract deficit graph to an actual failing
closed history. Conversely it cannot declare the failing leaf impossible
merely because it has real root-entry witnesses.

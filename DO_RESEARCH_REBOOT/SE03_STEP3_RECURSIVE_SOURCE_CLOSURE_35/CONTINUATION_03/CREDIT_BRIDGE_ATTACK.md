# Attack on the remaining repayment bridge

## Exact new reduction, not a favorable global count

DL35 supplies a specified source comparison and the actual signed identity.
SE35 funds the entire seven-key/maximal-core complement of its four
exceptions. OR35 supplies a fixed-size active anonymous shadow for every
unbounded H1 run. All three statements are uniform over the stated private
populations and word lengths. They remove three old ambiguities, but do not
provide distinct donors for the exceptional releases.

In a closed core13 period the exact deficit of the new signed construction
is E-S as defined in SE35. Proving E<=S without a source rule and its
physical ownership proof would merely assume the missing credit sign.
q2/H1 exception E2b is especially instructive: its actual price2H-q is0,
while L2 drops by2. This is failure of the particular degree-layer debt
transfer, not evidence of a negative CD packet or an unpaid full demand.

## Attempts checked against the source

1. Raise the threshold from2 to3. This handles E2b, because both old
   degree3 ancestors become low-degree channels. But a leaf access q3/H1
   crosses THREE thresholds and has only TWO A bits. Reserving a third
   bit would create credit. The threshold swap is not a full repair.
2. Union two partial channel maps. Even when every demand occurs in one,
   their union can have an alternating path with demand vertices at both
   ends and no free unit. Individual coverage does not prove simultaneous
   funding. A source-forced endpoint theorem is still required.
3. Treat H1 macros as containing arbitrary unseen preparation. OR35 shows
   this is false: the only positive A access is the final key. Anonymous
   operands come from a fixed entry set. Actual H>=2 preparation remains
   at run boundaries and cannot be silently imported or spent twice.
4. Infer A=B merely because A and B process the same genuine word in an
   H1 run. Their entry trees may differ after the genuine source prefix.
   Identical deterministic operations on DIFFERENT initial states do not
   imply equal current states. No uniform synchronizing/repayment theorem
   was established; it is not used as a bridge.

There is a completed diagonal special case: if A=B at the entry of an H1
run, OR35 implies both execute exactly the same positive splay x in each
macro. They stay equal, and qF=floor(depthA/2)<=H=1, so own bits fund every
demand in that run. This does not handle the non-diagonal source-simple
entry states required by the root.

## Remaining concrete source theorem

For an arbitrary source-valid collection of H1 runs in a closed core13
word, finance their E2a/E2b/E3/E4 losses by a single source-derived joint
construction, preserving natural h0 and every actual owner/reservation.
OR35 permits a finite fixed-support ordered syntax independent of the
runs' lengths and hidden unary populations. It does not prove the sign on
that syntax or realize arbitrary negative abstract paths. Other core sizes
and supports remain required by UCF35 as well.

No complete original-source countercertificate to this joint construction
has been produced. No new finite passing source test is used as proof.
The current one-query remainder prevents an unrestricted fresh semantic
graph construction; this is a limit of that computational route, not a
claim that symbolic mathematics is impossible or the CPU allowance is gone.

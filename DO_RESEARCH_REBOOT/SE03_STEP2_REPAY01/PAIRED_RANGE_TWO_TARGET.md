This replaces the proposed width-three architecture as the next research
candidate for six keys. Neither candidate's debt function has been proved.
The old necessary range3 result concerns inequalities on EVERY SINGLE
natural full macro; it does not forbid width2 on the composed packets here.

## Exact original-source partition

For full core11, pair every q5 natural macro with its actual q1 successor,
as required by MAXIMUM_DEPTH_SUCCESSOR_REPAYMENT.md. These pairs are disjoint.
Keep every other natural macro as a singleton. For core10, keep all natural
macros as singletons. This partitions every original source occurrence
cyclically, including every DELETE, nongenuine KEEP and zero operation.
It deletes no source word segment. Every edge of this new mathematical
partition is an actual source segment with its original full endpoints.
The full-state cycle still closes at any partition boundary.

Let H be each segment's total actual A events and Q its total full B
doubles. A singleton has H>=1,Q<=4. A q5 pair has H>=2,Q=6.

Seek a state-defined D in[0,2] at these actual boundary states such that

    2min(H,4)-Q+D(after)-D(before)>=0.                    (1)

The complete NONAUTOMATIC source obligations are:

| Segment | H | Q | Required debt increase |
|---|---|---|---|
|singleton|1|1,2,3,4|D(after)-D(before)>=Q-2|
|singleton|2|3,4|D(after)-D(before)>=Q-4|
|q5 followed by q1|2|6|increase>=2|
|q5 followed by q1|3|6|increase>=0|

All remaining cases have clipped weight at least2 and are range-safe.
In particular singleton H3 is automatic, q0 singletons are automatic,
and paired H>=4 is automatic. The formerly new weight2 releases need
no additional source case when the range is2. Paired H3/Q6 ZERO edges
remain a genuine debt-preservation obligation and cannot be dismissed
merely because the un-clipped packet is independently CD-safe.

This is an exhaustive inequality partition proved by the bounds above;
it is not a finite test or a claim that a function satisfying (1) exists.
The source state still contains the full actual A and B trees. The new
partition is not the frozen nine-core graph and never enlarges or edits it.

IF (1) is proved, summation on the original fully closed period gives

demands may precede later A donors within a pair; the allocation is by

to Step1's actual period and apply the independent exchange lemma to keep
its common assignments fixed. These conclusions are conditional on (1).

## The width-two lower bound is actual, not just a scalar model

Modify the universal Gamma_r source word as follows. Immediately after
its critical Kn, perform K1 before the repair, and replace the old
D2 Dn K1 suffix by

    K1 Dn D2 Dn D1.

The two first KEEPs of this replacement are the critical Kn followed by
K1; their actual A costs are1,1 and full B doubles r-1,1. They form a
paired segment of full weight4-r. After K1, A has returned exactly to

The subsequent Dn D2 Dn recreate the first-deep-access full B state(6);
D1 then recreates the actual full B state after K1. All later original
KEEPs again execute identically in A and B and close at R_n.

Gamma_r, giving A=6r, QF=6r-6 and full slack6r+6. The auxiliary B word
is unchanged and has the same exact recurrence and signed marginal.
This is a reachable fully paired-closed arbitrary-fringe source schema.
Any bounded debt law on all these composed segments requires width>=r-4;
at r6 it requires width at least2. Width2 is therefore necessary for this
architecture, but its sufficiency is still unproved.

# A wider debt target and its exact remaining source obligations

This is an explicitly CONDITIONAL alternative to pointwise erasure
repayment. No debt function satisfying these obligations has been proved.
It does not complete Step2, CD(6), or support induction.

## Actual full macros and the candidate

Use the inherited natural full-source partition: a macro begins just
after a genuine full KEEP and ends at the next genuine full KEEP. It
contains all intervening DELETEs and nongenuine KEEPs. Let H be its
actual A StepEv sum, q its terminal full B doubles. H>=1, q<=5 for the
six-key cores10/11 under discussion. The root-change proof of H>=1 is
independent of the old symbolic h0 convention, which remains unchanged.

Seek a function D6(A,B,M) on the actual full boundary state, with
0<=D6<=3, defined independently of the desired period inequality. It
must satisfy

    2 min(H,4)-q + D6(after)-D6(before) >=0.          (1)

The range3 accommodates Gamma's compulsory increase3 at its critical
H1/q5 full macro. It is not proved sufficient for other sources.

For every H>=4, (1) is automatic from q<=5 and the range:8-q>=3.
Thus the complete remaining source obligation is (1) for H=1,2,3.
The following is an exact algebraic partition, not a finite source test.
Write delta=D6(after)-D6(before):

| H | q with a nonautomatic obligation | Required delta |
|---|---|---|
|1|0,1,2,3,4,5|delta>=q-2|
|2|2,3,4,5|delta>=q-4|
|3|4,5|delta>=q-6|

Other pairs have2H-q>=3 and are automatically range-safe. Core11 genuine
q>=1; core10 may have genuine depth1 and q0. No source is filtered using
this observation. Anonymous helper targets are arbitrary present keys.
An H3 macro can expose up to seven A search-path vertices, so its source
geometry cannot be identified with the completed M3 H<=2 LOW theorem.

Widening the old range from2 to3 creates new weight2 release obligations:
H1/q0, H2/q2 and H3/q4 may not drop debt3 to0. Treating every positive
edge as safe would omit these cases. Weight1 releases may drop at most1;
zero weights preserve debt; negative weights require the stated increase.

For arbitrary r, the analogous proposed parameters K=r-3,T=r-2 are the
smallest threshold for a HIGH proof using ONLY the range K and q<=r-1:
2T-(r-1)>=K. Neither this range nor its LOW preservation is established.
Source constraints could permit a different architecture; this is not a
necessity theorem for all possible proof methods.

## Conditional consequence and actual source units

IF D6 is constructed and (1) is proved for all source macros, summing on
a fully paired-closed period gives QF<=2 sum min(H,4)<=2A. This is a
valid conditional implication, not proof of the premise.

Its physical allocator is the already proved Step1 ownership induction
with threshold4 and debt width3: cut at an actual maximum-debt boundary;
retain Dmax-D6 working units; mint the two bits of each of the first
min(H,4) actual A events; pay the full terminal doubles FIFO; release
certified surplus and both bits of all later A events. The bank starts
and ends empty. Boundary bank<=3; within-macro queue<=3+8=11. All units
come from the one actual period. The source cost (1) proves payment
sufficiency and release nonnegativity by the same algebra, without
running an allocator on numerical traces.

## Preserve the completed auxiliary allocation by explicit exchange

Suppose such an independently proved full allocator fF exists. Keep
Step1's auxiliary allocator fE and its common-demand pairing fixed.
Let C be the common full demands, P the unmatched full demands, and
V=all actual A units minus fE(C)=U disjoint_union fE(N).

For p in P, start at u=fF(p). If u is in V, assign u to p. Otherwise
u=fE(c) for a unique common c; continue to fF(c). Repeat until reaching
V. This process must terminate. A repeated common vertex would, by
injectivity of fF and fE, force its predecessor to repeat too, eventually
identifying the initial unmatched p with a common vertex, impossible.
Equivalently, a finite common-only directed cycle has no outside incoming
edge because every unit and vertex has at most one predecessor.

Paths from two different p cannot meet: a first meeting and injectivity
would force their predecessors to coincide, recursively forcing the
initial unmatched demands to coincide. The terminal units are therefore
distinct, lie in V, and avoid every fixed common assignment. The procedure
is an explicit source-ID-preserving injection P->V, using no cardinality
assumption equivalent to CD. Every edge comes from an independently
certified physical assignment. It is a block/cyclic allocation, not a
new native matching-terminal eligibility claim.

The unresolved part is constructing D6 and proving its H1/H2/H3 source
rules, including all weight0,+1,+2 releases and arbitrary newly exposed
anonymous helpers. The exchange lemma cannot replace that source theorem.

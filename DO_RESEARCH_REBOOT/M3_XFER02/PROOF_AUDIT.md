## Public support separation, not a hidden allocation premise

At the small end, the two smallest marked labels a<b are present and
the b.right PORT is observed at depth1/2. Every other marked label is
greater than b. Proper-prefix connectivity and the original Score order
force each such key into that port, so it cannot be a present actual
operand. Only a,b are present marked Scores. The corresponding statement
at the large end uses the observed a.left port. This proof does not
determine private unmarked-Score or anonymous allocations in other blocks,
nor require the helper's support to be empty.

This property holds for EVERY fresh compatible source witness satisfying
the public guard. It is not inferred by transporting the previous edge's
private port bill. This is why the visible cut suffices despite independent
witnesses between edges. When a cut drops below the alpha shadow, that
argument is unavailable and the corresponding source is not claimed solved.

The root-kind coordinate and nonreal unused S/index fields stay free.
G fallbacks read the original slot0 label, a or b. They can duplicate
one member of the pair but cannot produce any of the other marked labels.
V uses a set and the kind0 filter, so multiplicity/unused-field artifacts
do not enlarge it. Real active operands follow the AER01 source path proof.
No original vertex is identified with a shadow or a canonical completion.

## Nonvacuity of the new anonymous/Score-bearing cases

The symbolic source b(p(A,a(B,C)),P), with p<a anonymous and the two
smallest marked keys a<b, is outside Q. Its H1 zigzag can expose arbitrary
new B/C roots. Ordered nine-core sources with b-a>=4 permit an actual
four-edge B path to a, giving a weight0 source outside E1 as well. No
new root evaluation or finite-population test is needed for this ordered
construction. The original source ports permit all other marks in P.

The Score-bearing Gamma1 branch is also nonvacuous, rather than an
empty formal case. Here is an analytic family using the already audited
universal RR identity. Let z be Score0, f the gap0 proxy, and a>z the
entry core. Source B has the prefix a(z(f(empty,empty),U),T), and core
b>a has an EVEN source depth d=2k in T below a. After splaying f,

    f(empty,z(empty,a(U,T))).

The first k terminal pairs are wholly in the old a subtree. Write their
arbitrary result as b(L,R). The remaining upper pair is

    f(empty,z(empty,b(L,R)))
       -> b(z(f(empty,empty),L),R).

Thus f is a childless final gap slot, not an invalid retained internal
gap. This is substitution into the universal paired-rotation identity
already audited in Transfer01/Transfer02, not a newly executed B case.
The terminal depth is2k+2, q=k+1, and w2=3-k.

Choose the A source a(f(A,z(B,C)),b(D,P)); f has Score z in its INITIAL

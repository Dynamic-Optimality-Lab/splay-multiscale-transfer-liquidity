## Exact child dynamics

old marked children plus the actual old marked ancestors. Every other
old marked ancestor loses its path child; all off-path marked-parent
edges are unchanged. Therefore, BETWEEN accesses to y, its child set
can only shrink. A child subtree still attached to y is unchanged until
an access into that subtree removes that child edge from y.

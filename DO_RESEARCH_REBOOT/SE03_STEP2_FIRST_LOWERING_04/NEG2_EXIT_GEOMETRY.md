Target: characterize all T11 exits without assuming the known examples
are exhaustive. Conditions: actual closed maximal six-key source;
unchanged cold NEG2 packet partition. Inheritance: all-SS source theorem,
fan-with-ports proof and exact marked-parent compression. Ambiguities:
the A inventory and legal order of off-path subtrees are not determined

and the actual off-path blocks, retaining all physical separators.

## Singleton H1/Q4

Its actual BF path has five marked and four unmarked vertices, in four
same-side marked-G/unmarked-P doubles. The output is the ordinary five-
mark fan F5, with each original off-path subtree in its exact source
port. Exactly one marked and one unmarked core vertex lie outside this
path. Their port has signed mass0, hence they form one actual two-node
block; no separate free capacity is invented.

The four old proper marked ancestors become direct marked children of
the accessed x. The remaining outside mark a retains its old nearest
marked parent, which is either x or one of those four ancestors. Thus
the exact MARKED-PARENT possibilities are:

| Type | Root children | Marked leaves | Off-path mark |
|---|---|---:|---|
| S5 | five marked leaves |5|child of x|
| S4 | four marked children; exactly one has one marked child |4|child of one old ancestor|

This is an exhaustive source-derived restriction, not a realization
claim for every ordering of the abstract marked tree. Actual BF ports,
separator placements, orientations and original gaps remain attached.

## Paired H2/Q6

The first q5 has an eleven-vertex Hamiltonian path and no outside marked
block. Its output is the exact six-mark fan F6: all five old proper
marked ancestors are direct marked children of its target x. Its ACTUAL
next genuine target y is one of the two extreme marks and has q1;
every intervening original operation belongs to that successor macro.
Since y is a marked leaf in F6, the q1 operation yields:

    Type P4: root y has ONE marked child x;
             x has FOUR marked leaf children.  L=4.

All q5/q1 NEG2 target marked-parent shapes have this form. The actual
unmarked fan arms are not collapsed in the source states or event counts.

## A and chart restrictions

For a singleton's H1 macro the unique positive A access targets x at
depth1/2 from synchronized old root g. It may be Kx or Dx followed by
root Kx. The resulting old g is at L,R,LL or RR according to the exact
zig/double formula; its parent can be an anonymous present key.

For a paired NEG2, EACH actual macro has H1. The first follows that
rule for x, the second for the forced extreme y from its actual midpoint.
No arbitrary A shape is inferred from the three marked-parent types.
Earlier charged preparations can rearrange A; D10 in the new witnesses
demonstrates why those arrangements matter.

The negative target is cold in its genuine old recent-five chart.
N/P1 targets later in a prefix use their actual charts, which need not
be the negative packet's old chart. Root repeats/zero DELETE identities
are retained with their true BE action. Off-core private populations
are unrestricted. S5/S4/P4 concern full M, not chart Score counts.

## Consequences for eligible next targets

In any ADMITTED all-SS BF splay, q equals the accessed key's marked-
parent depth. A marked-parent route alone need not be physically legal;
the conclusions below only restrict actual source operations.

- S5 has no admitted q2, hence no N. Any admitted P1 accesses a marked leaf.
- S4 has just one marked depth-two candidate: the outside mark a under
  its old path ancestor. If an actual N accesses it, its leaf change is0.
- P4's marked depth-two candidates are the four children of x. Any actual
  N to one of them is leaf-neutral.

Every such N from S4/P4 produces type D4:

    marked root z has two marked children:
    one leaf b and one vertex h with three marked leaf children.

All q2 candidates in D4 are leaves below h. Both proper marked ancestors
have off-path marks, so every ADMITTED H1/Q2 there is lowering. Thus D4
has NO N successor.

This is a universal arbitrary-length reduction: starting in T11, an N*
prefix has at most ONE packet. It follows by source correspondence and
induction, not bounded path search. It applies to the initial N* block
of E1 as well; the P1 intervention can create other forms afterward.

# Exact local entry conditions and the empty-prefix exclusion

Target: characterize L11 using actual source geometry. Conditions:
genuine core11 H1/Q2 singleton in an original closed history. Inheritance:
Campaign03 local classification and marked-parent source bridge.
Ambiguities: numerical leaf count alone is not sufficient for entry or
source realization. Bridge: combine exact A and BF paths with leaf status.

An actual packet is leaf-lowering iff its BF path is

    g,u,h,v,x,

with g,h,x marked, u,v unmarked, both physical pairs same-side, x a
marked leaf, and both g and h retaining off-path marked descendants.
Its actual four-edge path is LLLL, LLRR, RRLL or RRRR. BF depth5 is
excluded by closed maximal-source even-depth, not silently included.

Its synchronized A root is g. The unique positive A access to x has
depth1/2, one actual zig or one actual double. Anonymous parent operands
and arbitrary hanging populations are allowed. No positive request to
a different helper can fit H1. Zero requests, root repetitions and their
BE effects remain in the original macro. Existing source timestamps are
not erased by the endpoint description.

The three outside marked vertices divide into two nonempty off-path
groups of sizes1/2. After the access the new marked root x has exactly
two marked child branches of sizes2 and3; the loss is4->3 or3->2.

## No zero-length entry, universally

A lowering source has at least two and at most three marked children
at its root g: the path child h, plus at least one off-path marked child;
at most two off-path marks can belong to g since h also needs an off-
path mark. Every T11 exit, by the new exact exit theorem, has root
marked degree5,4 or1. Hence

    T11 intersect L11 is empty.

This excludes the empty E0 prefix for all A/BE/chart/inventory choices.
Combined with the N-prefix theorem, every actual E0 entry has EXACTLY
one N packet and the resulting D4 root form. Entry occurs precisely
when that actual one-event N leaves at least one of h's three leaf
targets on a legal same-side BF path and at A depth1/2. The new E0
family realizes those simultaneous conditions at key5.

## Two uniform source-depth lemmas

1. Initial target depth is at most2H in any macro with H A StepEvs
ending at that target. A StepEv performs at most two ordinary rotations;
each rotation lowers the depth of a fixed node by at most one. Since
the target ends at depth0, d_initial<=2H. This applies with arbitrary
helpers and arbitrary present-key populations.

2. A ONE-event splay to y which is not an ancestor of a different x
cannot reduce x's depth. From the exact source formulas:

| Event | Off-path subtree depth changes for x outside y's subtree |
|---|---|
|ZIG|other side gains1|
|LL/RR|the parent-side opposite hanger gains1; outer opposite hanger gains2|
|LR/RL|one opposite hanger stays fixed; the other gains1|

The old root and old parent also never decrease their depths. Every
possible non-descendant location is one of these operands/hangers;
internal hanger depths are unchanged. Thus all changes are nonnegative.

In the stored suffix terms every free gap Fi contains no distinguished
Score. A helper INSIDE such a gap cannot be an ancestor of a Score x.
Therefore, for an H2 macro Dy,Kx with y private and one event each,
x must ALREADY be at depth<=2. If x starts deeper, the private helper
cannot make its terminal access H1. If x starts shallow, a private
helper must itself occur in a gap meeting the first two A levels.
These are necessary conditions used for exclusion; they are not an
endpoint equivalence or deletion of a physical helper operation.

Let SC(alpha,r) be compatibility.py's explicit quantifier-free formula.
It reads only the existing seven kind/Score labels and synchronized
root, ignoring exactly the original unused fields. PORT/EMPTY stay
distinct. All original masks and sigma are free. Define

 Bad(s)=P(s) AND NOT SC(alpha(s),rootB(s));
 D_bad(s)=max(D_current(s),2*I[Bad(s)]).

SC is necessary for EVERY original fresh LOW source. It is not claimed
sufficient at a particular sigma. Here is a source-exact derivation.

At a child of root Score r, let[l,h] be its Score interval: [0,r-1]
on the left, [r+1,8] on the right. The five exhaustive cases are:

1. PORT: its two shadow descendants propagate PORT, with unused flags
   and indices still arbitrary and irrelevant.
2. EMPTY: no Score can be hidden, so l>h; both descendants propagate
   EMPTY. This is the original Score-cover implication, not rank strictness.
3. Observed Score r: uniqueness excludes a distinct present duplicate;
   the reference is G. Thus l>h by the completed protected-prefix
   grammar. Its descendants read literal owner0's current children:
   for L they are r/alphaR; for R they are alphaL/r.
4. An actual Score p in[l,h]: its observed children can be PORT,
   anonymous, an actual Score in the appropriate split interval, or
   EMPTY/G when that interval is Score-empty. G reads r, not p.
5. Anonymous: its private gap g satisfies l<=g<=h+1. For its left
   observed actual Score y!=r require l<=y<g; for its right Score
   z!=r require g<=z<=h. A left EMPTY/G forces g=l and a right
   EMPTY/G forces g=h+1. PORT/anonymous child imposes no further
   Score bound; equal-gap anonymous nodes may occur on BOTH sides.

Eliminate g by intersecting these integer lower/upper bounds. The
intersection is nonempty iff their maximum lower bound is at most
their minimum upper bound. compatibility.py encodes this without
discarding simultaneous bounds. No private gap value, selected tree,
inventory or persistent history becomes an observable.

every finite r in the inherited maximal-core domain, every source length,
repetition count and arbitrary source populations. R19's immediate old-root
restriction is likewise source-uniform on that domain. They follow from
actual original status/rotation rules, not finite sampling. Neither supplies
the missing usable A units.

ceil((r-1+floor((r+1)/2))/2); its origin hypotheses and audit qualification

blocks. It lifts uniformly to arbitrary populations in unaccessed ordered
off-path hanger cells, using the original operand-free substitution theorem.

ancestors on active paths, alter the B marked core, add KEEP keys, or cover
all seven-key histories. No finite list of graph states proves such an
extension. No arbitrary-r funding theorem is derived from F19.

for fixed r and bounded source H with arbitrary free terms. It is not
an all-r signed feasibility or collective supply theorem. The inherited
width>=r-4 obstruction and Bank25 are retained at their exact scope.

source language, marked/higher-H interactions and sufficient physical supply

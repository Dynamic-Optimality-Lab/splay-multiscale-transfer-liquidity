# A source-forced recovery barrier, and why it is not A-credit supply

Status: PROVED-DERIVATION / AUDIT-PENDING on the inherited maximal-core
domain, symbolic in r and all source populations/lengths.

THEOREM. A chronological consecutive run of H1/Q2,beta2,z1 natural zero
macros has length at most r-3. More generally a consecutive run of the
C16 natural-zero one-hole class H=c,Q=2c,u0,z1, ending with c_last,
has length at most r-1-2c_last.

PROOF. Each such macro changes the actual marked-leaf count by-1 through
the exact C15 identity Delta L=u-z. A marked tree with r>=2 has a marked
nonleaf root, so its initial leaf count is at most r-1. The last target
was a marked leaf. Its actual path-to-star splay gives it Q=2c_last
distinct old marked ancestor children. Every one has a marked leaf in
its disjoint subtree, so the final leaf count is at least2c_last. Therefore
k=L_before-L_after<=r-1-2c_last. At c_last=1, k<=r-3. QED.

Whole-period closure also forbids a nonempty period consisting only of
such zero macros. Positive leaf recovery must occur elsewhere. This is
a genuine source consequence, independent of ownership and finite tests.

It does NOT show that an arbitrary creator-ancestry/owner path is acyclic
or has bounded length. Such paths need not follow consecutive chronological
macros, and r is unbounded. It does NOT supply enough A units at the leaf
recovery. In the original A80/B82 witnesses the deep recovery macro has
H1,Q6 and leaf gain5, while its own unused comparison tail has only ONE
unit. Two transported LOW debts meet there. A proposed payment of one
new usable A bit per recovered B leaf is therefore source-refuted.

This sharpens the smallest missing bridge: chronological source recovery
exists, but a repayment theorem must relate its FULL surrounding actual
A preparation/recovery events to the collective debt inventory. Counting
the recovered B leaves, or proving that a recovery macro occurs, does not
establish that bridge. The nonlocal static block supply of F19 is one
verified restricted realization; arbitrary sources remain open.

# Collective source results and the unproved usable-supply implication

## Theorem T19 — distinct leaf-birth pressure for any zero-hole collection

Assume the inherited exact C15 maximal-core source rule. Let Z be ANY
deduplicated finite subset of H1/Q2,beta2,z1 zero macros. Each has one
unpaid branch demand d_e, marked target x_e and reserved first unit rho_e.
Pair its marked-leaf death to the NEXT leaf birth of the SAME x_e,
cyclically within the one actual closed period. Let b_e be that original
unary B-double demand.

Then e->b_e is injective, h_Lambda(b_e)=rho_e, and all b_e are unary
demands, disjoint from the branch holes d_e. This holds for arbitrary
finite r/source length/private populations, repeated targets and shared
creator descriptions. It does not need a fresh creator bit.

PROOF. Per key the Boolean marked-leaf status closes, so births and deaths
alternate cyclically. Every selected death has a unique next birth, and
two deaths of that key cannot share it. Different keys have different
unary ancestor-demand IDs, even in the same access. C15 chooses exactly
the immediately preceding same-key death unit for each such birth.
Distinct death macros have distinct actual reserved units. QED.

The leaf ledger also gives sum_e u_e=sum_e z_e over the closed period;
since every selected zero has u0,z1, its complement contains at least
|Z| actual unary births. The explicit pairing above is stronger than
merely this count. These are SOURCE B demands and already-used A units,
not new free A credits.

## Corollary — exact comparison transposition, no capacity gain

Replace the entries b_e->rho_e by d_e->rho_e simultaneously, keeping all
other comparison entries. The result is injective, has the SAME physical
image, covers those zero branch holes, and leaves the distinct b_e unpaid.
Any other old holes remain. Disjointness of the replaced demand sets and
unit sets proves the assertion directly. The construction is an
alternative proof comparison, not a mutation of any archived/frozen owner
or a licensed escape extending the old comparison during an ongoing run.

Therefore the eligible complement of the comparison image is unchanged.
Calling these reserved units new supply would be false. The theorem
collectively localizes pressure but does not prove U19's supply premise.

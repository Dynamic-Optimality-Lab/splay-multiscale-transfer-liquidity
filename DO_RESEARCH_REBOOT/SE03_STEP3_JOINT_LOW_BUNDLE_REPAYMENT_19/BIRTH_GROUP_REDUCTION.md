# Source-defined collective reduction at actual B leaf-birth macros

Status: PROVED-DERIVATION / AUDIT-PENDING. This is a new alternative
comparison construction, not mutation of frozen original owners.

Use T19 to transpose a deduplicated zero-hole collection Z to its distinct
unary leaf-birth demands. Group those arrivals by their ACTUAL original
natural macro e. Let k_e be the number of selected arrivals in that macro.
They are distinct actual unary demand IDs, so k_e<=u_e.

The transposed comparison has exactly the same physical image as h_Lambda.
In macro e that image is precisely its first min(2H_e,beta_e+z_e) units;
all its remaining T_e=2H_e-min(2H_e,beta_e+z_e) original units are unused
by the whole comparison. List the arrivals and those units in original
source order, and assign the first min(k_e,T_e) arrivals to the first
that many tail units. Distinct macro inventories are disjoint. Thus the
assignments are globally injective fresh-image extensions, uniformly for
arbitrary finite source/support/private populations. No Hall or positive
ancestor premise is used. Remaining arrival debt is exactly(k_e-T_e)_+.

In particular every arrival group with 2H_e>=Q_e+z_e is completely paid:
Q_e=u_e+beta_e implies T_e=2H_e-beta_e-z_e>=u_e>=k_e.
This is a sufficient source inequality, not an assertion all groups meet it.

For an H1 maximal-depth B macro, Q=r-1,u=r-1,beta0,z1. There is exactly
ONE unused own bit, the second bit of its unique A event. Any positive
number k of arrivals can use that one bit for one arrival; k-1 actual
unary demand debts remain. This is a simultaneous partial SOURCE payment,
not a claim that the additional k-1 nonlocal units exist.

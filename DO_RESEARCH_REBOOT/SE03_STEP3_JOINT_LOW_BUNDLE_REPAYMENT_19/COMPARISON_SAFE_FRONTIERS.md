# Marked-parent safety and a new immediate source restriction

The C18 watched rule is retained exactly: anonymous P->target; marked
zig root->target; marked-parent double OUTER root->P. A creator is a
physical event and bit, not a claim of free credit. Qualified RR7 and
marked spent-pair counterexamples remain controlling inherited evidence.

## Lemma R19 — one old-root birth and at most one other birth

In a maximal-core natural macro with H1, let G be the A/BF root at the
preceding genuine boundary, and v its final genuine target. Before the
unique positive A access its root is still G: every preceding zero-event
operation targets the current root. That positive access targets v;
subsequent zero-event operations cannot change the root. Thus G,v are
marked and G!=v. Apply the exact original single zig/double.

One qualified watched-edge birth has child G. Its old parent was absent
(G was root), so it is a genuine net new boundary edge. For SS it is P->G;
if P is marked its side is root-exposable (the new root v lies on the
opposite ordered side from G). If P is anonymous it qualifies directly.
For a mixed double or zig it is v->G, root-exposable. C18's JOINT two-birth
bound therefore leaves at most ONE other qualified birth in this macro.
No per-class two-bit budget is introduced.

By the C15 actual B path-to-star theorem, old marked root G is a child
of v in the marked-parent B tree after this genuine access. Therefore,
if the NEXT genuine target is G, its actual B-double count is1. It cannot
be an H1/Q2 zero frontier. Arbitrary zero-event/root-repeat gaps do not
change this conclusion. Both reflections and arbitrary source populations
are included, under the maximal-core source hypotheses.

Consequently an immediate anonymous-parent or marked-zig zero frontier
(whose watched child IS its target) cannot use the old-root birth. For
an SS anonymous producer G(P(v(A,B),C),D), the only possible such immediate
innovation is the marked root y of B. Its subsequent A access is the
opposite mixed frame at depth2. This proves the source geometry of the
actual immediate SS-to-mixed relay; it does not supply either debt's donor.

IMPORTANT exception: a marked-parent DOUBLE watches root->P, whose child
P is NOT the accessed target. It may destroy an old-root-child edge by
accessing a descendant instead. The q1 argument cannot exclude it. Delayed
frontiers likewise have intervening B transitions. No universal no-sharing
or creator-ancestry acyclicity follows from R19.

Producer profiles on A80/B82 check the statement against stored rows,
without new rotations. Their passing values are controls for the symbolic
proof, not a source-uniform finite enumeration. All new results await audit.

For the F19 family, comparison safety follows more strongly from the
complete global h_Lambda extension, including all marked-parent holes.
Its reserved-channel permutation changes no branch/frontier value or
physical image. Outside that family, C18 origin plus R19 remains a raw
source classification, and simultaneous usable nonlocal supply is OPEN.

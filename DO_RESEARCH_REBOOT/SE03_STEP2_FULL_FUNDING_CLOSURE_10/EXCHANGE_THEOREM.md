accepted full-exchange theorem is its special case domain(h)=D: no common
owner can be missing h, every unmatched path terminates at a free unit,
paths are disjoint and the final map covers ALL D while preserving every
fixed common assignment. Its validity is independent of support r.

global locked-owner conflicts without assigning duplicate bits.

Proof. Recent-window and complement bounds give
2r-6<=|C_U|<=2r-5 and2r-4<=|C_(U+v)|<=2r-3.
If v belonged to C_U, adjoining it could not enlarge this core, a
contradiction. If z belonged to C_(U+v), that core would equal the full

with at most4 nodes. If v is not above z it has no other mark descendant,

lie on their chain. A distance-three chain makes the first double's
unmarked grandparent or parent permanently mark-free. Every processed
subtree stays rigid in later outer frames; the lost old core label could
not return, contradicting full labeled-core constancy. Distance is1/2.

omitted oldest mark and lies outside the new recent r-1 core. It must be
a full-core leaf. Its processed subtree is already FINAL after the first
double; any proper Score descendant would either have another marked
descendant or be permanently lost. Both are forbidden. Thus v is already
a Score singleton. The first double has depth>=2 below the root when
Q>=2, so the global root stays g. No all-SS assertion was used: a marked-
parent mixed double is admitted when actual source geometry permits it.

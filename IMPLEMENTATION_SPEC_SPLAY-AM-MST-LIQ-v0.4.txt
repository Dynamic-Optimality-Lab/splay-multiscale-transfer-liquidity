# 1. Purpose

The v0.3 multiscale-transfer experiment found a finite survivor with:

```text
predicate = P_all
k = 6
C = 2
energy = #LATENT + #ACTIVE
```

and an activation operator that converted at most one LATENT credit into ACTIVE credit at each eligible transfer event.

Later universal-proof work discovered a legal repayment failure.

The canonical minimized witness is:

```text
n = 28

H =
DELETE 27
DELETE 28
KEEP   28
KEEP   27
```

At the final KEEP:

```text
a = 2
y = 15
need = 11
pre-access ACTIVE pool = 2
A activation opportunities = 1
B activation opportunities = 7
paid = 10
margin = paid - need = -1
```

while substantial LATENT reserve remains available.

Thus the failure is not adequately described as:

```text
insufficient credit creation
```

but instead as:

```text
sufficient stored reserve
+
insufficient conversion of reserve into spendable ACTIVE credit
before the repayment deadline
```

The large-size discovered family exhibits growing negative repayment margins, including the observed sequence:

```text
n=28   margin=-1
n=32   margin=-2
n=40   margin=-4
n=48   margin=-6
n=64   margin=-10
n=96   margin=-18
n=128  margin=-26
n=192  margin=-42
n=256  margin=-58
n=384  margin=-90
n=512  margin=-122
```

These observations are **development evidence**, not a universal theorem unless later proved symbolically.

The exact witness was independently replayed by a differently structured implementation, with agreement on the canonical \(n=28\) witness and larger representatives.

The new experiment therefore asks:

> Can the original multiscale-transfer architecture be repaired by explicitly parameterizing bounded local activation bandwidth, while leaving Splay, Pair Access, T7 injection, T6 discharge, credit support, energy, provenance, and the theorem architecture otherwise unchanged?

Except where this specification explicitly states otherwise, the following semantics are inherited byte-for-byte or definition-for-definition from the exact sealed parent and must be independently reverified before theorem-facing use.

## 3.1 Splay

Ordinary bottom-up Splay.

\[

\]

```text

ZIG-right
```

Tree semantics, pointer/functional semantics, rotation ordering, reference snapshots, key ordering, and cost convention are not changed.

## 3.2 Pair Access

KEEP:

\[
(A,B)\mapsto(S_xA,S_xB)
\]

with

\[
a=c(A,x),\qquad y=c(B,x).
\]

DELETE:

\[
(A,B)\mapsto(S_xA,B)
\]

with

\[
a=c(A,x),\qquad y=0.
\]

## 3.3 Regret

For candidate theorem constant \(C\),

\[
w_C=y-Ca.
\]

Repayment requirement:

\[
\operatorname{required}_C(y,a)
=
\max(y-Ca,0).
\]

## 3.4 Ledger energy

Credit types remain:

```text
LATENT
ACTIVE
SPENT
```

unless a separately preregistered inherited branch already contains additional signed types.

For unsigned Branch A:

\[
E(L)=\#LATENT(L)+\#ACTIVE(L).
\]

Activation does not create energy.

SPENT credits are excluded from available energy exactly as in the inherited calculus.

## 3.5 T7 injection

T7 is unchanged.

Each eligible A-side event injects at most \(k\) LATENT credits at the inherited cycling interior-boundary sites with inherited orientation/support/provenance semantics.

No B-side T7 injection is introduced.

## 3.6 T6 discharge

T6 is unchanged.

If the ACTIVE pool immediately before discharge is \(P\) and the required amount is \(d\), then:

\[
paid=\min(P,d).
\]

Discharge converts the selected ACTIVE credits to SPENT under the inherited deterministic ordering.

No LATENT credit may be spent directly.

This rule is central: the liquidity axis changes **when reserve becomes ACTIVE**, not what discharge means.

# 4. The only fundamental semantic change: T5 becomes bandwidth-aware

The inherited T5 converts at most one eligible LATENT credit to ACTIVE per invocation.

Define the old operation:

\[
T5_1(L,m)
\]

to be the exact inherited T5.

The new operator is deliberately defined by bounded iteration of the old one.

For event \(ev\), candidate activation profile \(\rho\), and mode \(m\):

\[
\boxed{
T5_\rho(L,m,ev)
=
T5_1^{\,\rho(ev)}(L,m)
}
\]

where exponentiation means functional iteration.

This is the primary surgical design choice.

It ensures that the new rule is not an unrelated ledger mutation.

It is literally repeated application of the already-defined activation primitive.

Consequently the following become immediate proof targets:

\[
E(T5_\rho(L,m,ev))=E(L),
\]

support is preserved,

provenance is preserved,

mass is preserved,

no SPENT credit is resurrected,

and only LATENT/ACTIVE status may change.

The deterministic activation selection order remains the inherited first-eligible/ledger-order rule.

If fewer than \(\rho(ev)\) LATENT credits are available, all available eligible LATENT credits are activated and no new credit is created.

# 5. Formal activation-bandwidth axis

The experiment does not treat \(\rho\) as an arbitrary state-dependent function.

That would create a dangerous overfitting channel.

Instead \(\rho\) belongs to a small preregistered structural family.

Define:

\[
\mu(ev)=
\begin{cases}
0,&ev=\mathrm{ROOT}\text{ or no rotation event},\\
1,&ev\text{ is a terminal ZIG},\\
2,&ev\in\{LL,RR,LR,RL\}.
\end{cases}
\]

\(\mu(ev)\) is the number of primitive rotations represented by the compressed Splay event.

Two activation families are permitted.

## 5.1 Flat activation

For integer \(r\ge1\):

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{\mathrm{flat},r}(ev)=r
}
\]

for every eligible non-ROOT StepEv.

Interpretation:

> Activate up to \(r\) credits per compressed StepEv.

The inherited calculus is exactly:

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{flat},1}.
\]

## 5.2 Primitive-rotation-weighted activation

For integer \(r\ge1\):

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{\mathrm{rot},r}(ev)=r\mu(ev)
}
\]

Thus for \(r=1\):

\[
\rho(ev)=
\begin{cases}
1,&ZIG,\\
2,&LL/RR/LR/RL.
\end{cases}
\]

Interpretation:

> Activate up to one credit per primitive rotation represented by the event.

The most structurally motivated new profile is therefore:

\[
\boxed{\rho_{\mathrm{rot},1}}.
\]

It is a candidate, not an assumption.

## 5.3 Frozen search range

Before target-guided synthesis the experiment must preregister:

```text
rho_family ∈ {
  FLAT,
  PRIMITIVE_ROTATION
}

rho_base ∈ {1,2,3,4,5,6}
```

Candidate profiles are:

\[
\rho_{\rm flat,r}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\rho_{\rm rot,r}.
\]

Larger activation families require a successor experiment.

No profile may depend on:

```text
n
tree identity
state ID
cycle ID
history index
future key
candidate residual
Bellman value
holdout membership
support ID
the amount currently required
whether the current KEEP would otherwise fail
```

Availability of LATENT credits may of course limit actual activation.

No proof may substitute:

\[

\]

for the required statement:

\[
\boxed{
ACTIVE_{\rm pre-discharge}\ge need.
}
\]

This distinction is the core reason v0.4 exists.

# 51. Final statement of intent

`SPLAY-AM-MST-LIQ-v0.4` is not an attempt to rescue a favored candidate by increasing a constant after seeing a failure.

The old candidate is preserved as false for synchronous repayment.

The new experiment responds to a qualitatively different obstruction:

\[
\boxed{
\text{reserve quantity and reserve liquidity are separate resources.}
}
\]

The original architecture parameterized the first through \(k\) but effectively fixed the second at one activation per compressed event.

The discovered legal counterexample demonstrates that substantial LATENT reserve may coexist with an underfunded repayment deadline.

Therefore activation bandwidth is promoted to an explicit, bounded, local, target-blind architectural axis:

\[
\boxed{
(P,k,C)
\longrightarrow
(P,k,C,\rho).
}
\]

The most structurally motivated new rule is not “activate two because the counterexample needs two,” but:

\[
\boxed{
\text{activation proportional to primitive rotations represented by the Splay event.}
}
\]

This rule is tested against flat bandwidth rather than assumed.

T7 remains the wealth-creation mechanism.

T5-rho becomes the liquidity mechanism.

T6 remains the spending mechanism.

The core question is now:

> **Can a local multiscale conservation law simultaneously create enough reserve, expose that reserve at sufficient bounded rate, and repay every synchronous KEEP in time?**

If yes, the experiment must continue through integrability, Pair Access, telescoping, and the exact Dynamic-Optimality bridge.

If no, the exact obstruction is preserved and used — only if structurally justified — to define the next architecture generation.

No failure is erased.

No finite survival is called a theorem.

No new axis is smuggled into a frozen experiment.

No candidate is repaired under the same identity.

The experiment either finds a liquidity-aware transfer architecture that survives proof, or it records precisely why this additional degree of freedom is still insufficient.

**End of normative implementation specification.**
